# Imóveis Portugal launches a three-language guide for people abroad preparing a purchase in Portugal

Lisbon, 1 December 2026. Imóveis Portugal opens six editions today, for Luxembourg, France, Switzerland, Brazil, the United States and Israel, in Portuguese, English and French. The site brings together the official rules for buying a home in Portugal, calculators for any budget from €100,000 to €10 million, and an interactive map of what each budget buys in each municipality, based on INE medians. A free newsletter arrives every Thursday.

"Buying from another country is entirely possible once you know the rules and the right numbers," say the editors. "Every figure on the site has its official source, and every step has a licensed professional who handles it."

Partners, always labelled as such, include the licensed estate agency Habinedita Gondomar (AMI 13250). Imóveis Portugal describes the rules and does not give legal, tax or financial advice.
