# Launch data story (prompt 18, D36): four budgets across Portugal

Replaces the single 500,000 € story (prompts 15 and 16) in the press kit, the launch newsletter and social. Every figure is in content/facts/facts.csv: INE municipality medians (`ine-m2-2026q2`, median value per m² of family dwellings sold in the 12 months to the 2nd quarter of 2026, released 23 October 2026), boundaries CAOP2025 (`caop-2025`), and, for 5 M€ and 10 M€, published listings checked on 25 October 2026 (`kf-*`), shown as examples, never as homes of our partner agency. Images: site/public/share/map-{300000,1000000,5000000,10000000}.png. Interactive version: the map page of each edition (`?budget=`).

Method note for journalists: the m² figures divide the budget by the INE median price per m² of each municipality. They are medians of actual sales, not specific homes. INE has no figure for 4 of the 308 municipalities. Municipality medians do not describe prime enclaves inside them (Quinta do Lago sits inside Loulé's median), which is why the two highest budgets show published listings instead.

## PT · O que 300 mil, 1 milhão, 5 milhões e 10 milhões de euros compram em Portugal

**300 000 €.** Ao preço mediano das casas vendidas, dá mais de 100 m² em 281 dos 304 concelhos com número oficial do INE, e mais de 200 m² em 189. No Porto, cerca de 83 m²; em Braga, 136 m²; em Viseu, 184 m².

**1 000 000 €.** Mais de 200 m² em todos os concelhos menos um. Em Lisboa, cerca de 193 m²; em Cascais, 205 m²; no Porto, 275 m².

**5 000 000 €.** As medianas deixam de descrever o mercado: em Cascais, a mediana daria cerca de 1 023 m². Os anúncios publicados mostram outra escala. Exemplo: uma moradia nova e contemporânea com quatro quartos e 381 m² em Cascais, anunciada por 5 250 000 €; ou uma quinta com olival e lagar, cerca de 50 hectares, em Mirandela, por 5 750 000 €.

**10 000 000 €.** Exemplos: uma moradia com seis quartos e 891,5 m² na Beloura (Sintra), anunciada por 8 500 000 €; casas novas com três a seis quartos na Quinta do Lago (Loulé), de 4 000 000 € a 9 250 000 €; e, acima deste orçamento, uma moradia contemporânea com sete quartos na Quinta do Lago por 15 750 000 €.

Fontes: INE, indicador 0012234 (2.º trimestre de 2026); DGT, CAOP2025; anúncios publicados pela Knight Frank, consultados a 25 de outubro de 2026 (preços pedidos, não preços de venda).

## EN · What €300,000, €1 million, €5 million and €10 million buy in Portugal

**€300,000.** At the median price of homes sold, it buys more than 100 m² in 281 of the 304 municipalities with an official INE figure, and more than 200 m² in 189. In Porto, about 83 m²; in Braga, 136 m²; in Viseu, 184 m².

**€1,000,000.** More than 200 m² in every municipality but one. In Lisbon, about 193 m²; in Cascais, 205 m²; in Porto, 275 m².

**€5,000,000.** Medians stop describing the market: in Cascais, the median would give about 1,023 m². Published listings show a different scale. For example, a newly built contemporary four-bedroom villa of 381 m² in Cascais, listed at €5,250,000; or a farm with olive groves and its own olive-oil production, about 50 hectares, in Mirandela, at €5,750,000.

**€10,000,000.** Examples: a six-bedroom villa of 891.5 m² in Beloura (Sintra), listed at €8,500,000; new homes with three to six bedrooms in Quinta do Lago (Loulé), from €4,000,000 to €9,250,000; and, above this budget, a contemporary seven-bedroom villa in Quinta do Lago at €15,750,000.

Sources: INE, indicator 0012234 (Q2 2026); DGT, CAOP2025; listings published by Knight Frank, checked on October 25, 2026 (asking prices, not sale prices).
