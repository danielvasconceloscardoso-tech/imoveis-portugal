## How we win attention

1. **Answer the question people are already asking.** Every topic starts from a real question a buyer types into Google or asks in a Facebook group. If nobody asks it, we don't write it.
2. **Numbers in the first line.** "250,000 € in Braga: what you pay on top" beats "Buying in Portugal: a guide".
3. **One pillar, thirty pieces.** Each week one deep article per edition. From it: the newsletter, 5 short videos, 3 carousels, 5 posts, 1 FAQ entry. Volume without extra research.
4. **Document, don't invent.** Real cases from the partner agency (anonymised, with the client's permission), real prices, real streets. A real 2-bedroom in Gondomar beats any stock photo.
5. **Every piece ends with a next step.** Download the guide, ask a question, or talk to a partner. Never a dead end.
6. **Be where they are.** Emigrants: Facebook groups and WhatsApp. Brazilians: Instagram and YouTube. Americans: Google, YouTube and Reddit. Israelis: Facebook, WhatsApp and LinkedIn. Same message, native format.
7. **Reply to everyone.** Every comment and message gets an answer within a day. Replies are content too: the best questions become next week's videos.
