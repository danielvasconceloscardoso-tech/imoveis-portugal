# Voice

How Imóveis Portugal sounds, in Portuguese, English and French (prompt 10). Applies to the site, the FAQ, the reports, the newsletters and social posts. Terms: content/GLOSSARY.md. Facts and sources: content/EDITORIAL_POLICY.md.

Target feeling: reliable, trustworthy, and "I want to belong to this".

## The five rules

1. **Belonging first.** We write to people who live abroad with Portugal at heart: "a sua casa em Portugal, mesmo a viver lá fora". We say "we" and "you" (PT: "o seu", "a sua", never "você"; FR: "vous"). Never "users", "clients" or "leads".
2. **Trust through proof.** Every figure has its source and the date we checked it. The partner agency is named with its licence: Habinedita Gondomar, AMI 13250. Sponsored content says so. We never call the platform independent or neutral.
3. **Short and specific.** Headlines under 8 words, with a number or a place where possible. One idea per paragraph. One action per screen.
4. **Describe, never advise.** We explain the rules; a professional advises on a person's case. No promises of returns. Banned: "paraíso", "oportunidade única", "investimento garantido", "paradis", "opportunité unique", "investissement garanti", "paradise", "once-in-a-lifetime", "guaranteed investment" or "guaranteed return".
5. **No invented proof.** No made-up numbers, testimonials, partner logos or "as seen in" (Código da Publicidade, DL 330/90). Audience figures only once they exist.

## FAQ answers

40–70 words. The answer in the first sentence ("Sim." / "Yes." / "Oui." or the number), then the detail, then the partner action (the button). Written so a 60-year-old emigrant and a 35-year-old American both get it on first read: no jargon without a gloss, no legal citations in the sentence unless they help (the source link sits under the answer).

## Words we like

PT: a sua casa, lá fora, de volta, com calma, contas feitas, a fonte, verificado.
EN: your home in Portugal, from abroad, step by step, the numbers, the source, checked.
FR : votre maison au Portugal, depuis l'étranger, pas à pas, les chiffres, la source, vérifié.
