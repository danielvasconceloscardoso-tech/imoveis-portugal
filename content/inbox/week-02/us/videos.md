## Video 1: 80 or 90

- **Hook:** "How much will a Portuguese bank lend you?"
- **Body:** "Up to 90% for your main home. Up to 80% for a vacation home."
- **Close:** "Source: Banco de Portugal."

## Video 2: the appraisal

- **Hook:** "Portuguese banks don't lend on the price."
- **Body:** "They use the lower of the price and their appraisal."
- **Close:** "The gap comes from your savings."

## Video 3: 45%

- **Hook:** "A new rule since August 2026."
- **Body:** "Your monthly loan payments should stay under 45% of net income. It used to be 50%."
- **Close:** "Source: Banco de Portugal."

## Video 4: 40 years

- **Hook:** "How long can a Portuguese mortgage last?"
- **Body:** "40 years if you're 35 or younger. 35 years after that."
- **Close:** "Source: Banco de Portugal."

## Video 5: proof of funds

- **Hook:** "Why does the bank ask where your money comes from?"
- **Body:** "Portuguese anti-money-laundering law requires banks, notaries and lawyers to check the origin of funds when the deal calls for it."
- **Close:** "Source: Law 83/2017."
