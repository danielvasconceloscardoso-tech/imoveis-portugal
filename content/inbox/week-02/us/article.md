# Can Americans get a mortgage in Portugal?

Each Portuguese bank decides whom it lends to and how it counts US income. What every bank in Portugal must follow are Banco de Portugal's limits. Here they are, so you know what to ask.

## How much: 90% or 80%

Banco de Portugal caps the loan as a share of the property's value (loan-to-value):

- **up to 90%** for an owner-occupied main home;
- **up to 80%** for any other purpose, such as a vacation home or a home you'll use before you move.

The value used is the lower of the price and the bank's appraisal. On a €400,000 vacation home, that means at least €80,000 of your own money.

## How much a month: 45% of income

Since August 1, 2026, the monthly payments on all of a borrower's loans should not exceed 45% of net income (it was 50%). Each bank may go above this for up to 10% of the lending it does each year.

## How long: 40 or 35 years

The maximum term is 40 years for borrowers aged up to 35, and 35 years for borrowers aged 35 or older.

## The taxes on a Portuguese mortgage

Stamp duty applies: 0.6% of the loan amount up front, and 4% of the interest over the life of the loan. On a €300,000 loan, that's €1,800 at signing.

## Where the money comes from

Banks, notaries and lawyers in Portugal must obtain information on the origin of funds when the transaction calls for it (Law 83/2017). Expect questions about the source of your down payment.

## Questions to ask a bank

- What share of the value would you lend in my case, and on what appraisal?
- How do you count US income, and in what currency?
- What is the maximum term for my age?
- What fees and taxes are due at signing?

A partner bank can answer these for your situation.
