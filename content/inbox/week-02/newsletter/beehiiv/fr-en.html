The full bill for a €250,000 home

"How much will I pay in total, not just the price?" It's the question we hear most. For a €250,000 home, if you aren't a Portuguese tax resident:

  • Price: €250,000
  • IMT (transfer tax): €18,750
  • Stamp duty, 0.8%: €2,000
  • Deed and registration at Casa Pronta: €375
  • Total: €271,125

The IMT line comes from the IMT code (article 17, no. 10, added by Decree-Law 97/2026): non-resident buyers of an urban property used exclusively for housing pay IMT at a flat rate of 7.5%, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies. One exception: you become a Portuguese tax resident within two years, and the difference is refunded on request. If you've been a Portuguese tax resident before, whether that counts under exception (a) is an open question for a lawyer or tax adviser. A resident buying the same home as their own permanent residence pays €259,417.

If you're a French tax resident and open a Portuguese bank account to pay for the home, that account is declared every year on form 3916, even if you don't use it.

With a mortgage, add 0.6% stamp duty on the loan, and Casa Pronta charges €700 because it registers the mortgage too. The lawyer's fee isn't in these numbers.
