## The full bill for a €500,000 home

"How much will I pay in total, not just the price?" It's the question we hear most. For a €500,000 home, if you aren't a Portuguese tax resident:

- Price: €500,000
- IMT (transfer tax): €37,500
- Stamp duty, 0.8%: €4,000
- Deed and registration at Casa Pronta: €375
- Total: €541,875, about CHF 500,000 at the ECB reference rate of October 23, 2026

The IMT line comes from the IMT code (article 17, no. 10, added by Decree-Law 97/2026): non-resident buyers of an urban property used exclusively for housing pay IMT at a flat rate of 7.5%, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies. One exception: you become a Portuguese tax resident within two years, and the difference is refunded on request. If you've been a Portuguese tax resident before, whether that counts under exception (a) is an open question for a lawyer or tax adviser. A resident buying the same home as their own permanent residence pays €530,612.

If the home in Portugal is to be your main residence, second-pillar money can be withdrawn early to buy it, with a minimum of CHF 20,000. For a second home, it can't.

With a mortgage, add 0.6% stamp duty on the loan, and Casa Pronta charges €700 because it registers the mortgage too. The lawyer's fee isn't in these numbers.
