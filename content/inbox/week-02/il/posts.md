## Post 1

Vacation home in Portugal with a mortgage? Portuguese banks lend up to 80% of the value. For a main home, 90%. Source: Banco de Portugal.

## Post 2

Since August 1, 2026, monthly payments on all your loans should stay under 45% of net income at Portuguese banks. Source: Banco de Portugal.

## Post 3

Longest mortgage term in Portugal: 40 years up to age 35, 35 years after that. Source: Banco de Portugal.

## Post 4

Borrowing 300,000 € in Portugal costs 1,800 € in stamp duty at signing (0.6%). Source: Portugal's stamp duty table.

## Post 5

Portuguese banks, notaries and lawyers must check where your money comes from when the deal calls for it (Law 83/2017). Question for the comments: would you borrow in Portugal or in Israel?
