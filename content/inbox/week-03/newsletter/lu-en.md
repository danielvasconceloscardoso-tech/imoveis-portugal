## You can sign without flying in

You don't need to fly to Portugal to sign. A power of attorney signed at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with the Hague Apostille, lets the person you choose, often a lawyer, sign the promissory contract and the deed in your name (source: Portugal's consular services, checked October 24, 2026). You can still come over for the visit, the keys or both.

On the day, a Casa Pronta office does the deed and the land registration in one sitting, for €375 (Justiça.gov.pt, checked October 25, 2026). Casa Pronta asks for the usage license, or its waiver, and the energy certificate. Taxes and the lawyer's fees are separate.

## What's different from Luxembourg

A power of attorney from a notary in Luxembourg carries the apostille; one signed at a Portuguese consulate doesn't need it. Because you live in the EU, a tax representative in Portugal is optional after the deed. You're paid in euros, so there's no exchange rate in the way. What helps is proof of where the money comes from: banks, notaries and lawyers must ask for it when the transaction calls for it.
