## You can sign without flying in

You don't need to fly to Portugal to sign. A power of attorney signed at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with the Hague Apostille, lets the person you choose, often a lawyer, sign the promissory contract and the deed in your name (source: Portugal's consular services, checked October 24, 2026). You can still come over for the visit, the keys or both.

On the day, a Casa Pronta office does the deed and the land registration in one sitting, for €375, about CHF 346 at the ECB reference rate of October 23 (Justiça.gov.pt, checked October 25, 2026). Casa Pronta asks for the usage license, or its waiver, and the energy certificate. Taxes and the lawyer's fees are separate.

## What's different from Switzerland

A power of attorney from a Swiss notary carries the apostille; one signed at a Portuguese consulate doesn't need it. Switzerland is outside the EU and the EEA, so after the deed you appoint a tax representative in Portugal or sign up for the tax office's electronic notifications. If the money comes from your 2nd pillar, the early withdrawal is allowed for your main residence, in Switzerland or abroad, and not for a vacation home.
