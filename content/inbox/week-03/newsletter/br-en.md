## You can sign without flying in

You don't need to fly to Portugal to sign. A power of attorney signed at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with the Hague Apostille, lets the person you choose, often a lawyer, sign the promissory contract and the deed in your name (source: Portugal's consular services, checked October 24, 2026). You can still come over for the visit, the keys or both.

On the day, a Casa Pronta office does the deed and the land registration in one sitting, for €375, about R$2,118 at the ECB reference rate of October 23 (Justiça.gov.pt, checked October 25, 2026). Casa Pronta asks for the usage license, or its waiver, and the energy certificate. Taxes and the lawyer's fees are separate.

## What's different from Brazil

A power of attorney from a Brazilian cartório needs the apostille; one signed at a Portuguese consulate doesn't. Brazil is outside the EU, so after the deed you appoint a tax representative in Portugal or sign up for the tax office's electronic notifications. The deed date matters at home too: the property goes in Bens e Direitos on your IRPF return, converted to dollars and then to reais at the Banco Central selling rate on the purchase date.
