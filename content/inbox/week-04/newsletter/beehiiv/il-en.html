This week's home

Our partner agency's first home goes here as soon as it sends one with permission to publish. Until then, this block runs the cost example: the bill for a €1,000,000 home if you aren't a Portuguese tax resident.

  • Price: €1,000,000
  • Transfer tax (IMT), 7.5% flat for non-residents: €75,000
  • Stamp duty, 0.8%: €8,000
  • Deed and registration at Casa Pronta: €375 (lawyer's fees are separate)
  • Total: €1,083,375, about ₪3.79 million at the ECB reference rate of October 23, 2026 (₪3.4939 to the euro)

The law lists exceptions to the 7.5%. In two of them the difference is refunded on request: you become a Portuguese tax resident within two years, or you let the home at a capped rent within six months, for at least 36 of the first 60 months. A third covers anyone who "has been considered" a Portuguese tax resident; how it reads for your own past is a question for a partner tax adviser: https://imoveisportugal.co.il/en/il/talk-to-a-partner/?cat=tax&utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=w04-launch-il#form

The story

Portugal house prices are easier to read as space. At the median price per m² of homes sold in the year to mid-2026 (INE), €1,000,000, about ₪3.49 million, buys:

  • 193 m² in Lisbon
  • 205 m² in Cascais
  • 242 m² in Loulé, in the Algarve
  • 275 m² in Porto
  • 283 m² in Funchal, Madeira
  • 393 m² in Grândola, Comporta's municipality

That last one is the municipality median: homes by the Comporta beaches sell for more. Across the country, the same budget buys more than 200 m² in 303 of the 304 municipalities with an INE figure.

Ten years ago, at Cascais's early-2016 median of €1,599 per m², the same money bought 625 m² there.

Medians aren't homes: they're the middle of everything sold in a year, old and new. They tell you where your money goes further, not what a given home costs.

The most-searched question

No reader questions yet, so we start with the search people in Israel run most: buy property in Portugal. Can you, from Israel?

Yes. You don't need to live in Portugal or be a resident; the transfer tax law even has its own rate for non-resident buyers. The first document is a Portuguese tax number (NIF), which you can request from abroad, online through a legal representative. Once you own the home, you have 15 days to name a tax representative or sign up for the tax office's electronic notifications; from outside the EU, one of the two is required. You don't have to fly in for the deed: a power of attorney signed before a notary with the Hague Apostille, or at a Portuguese consulate, does it.

The answer in full: https://imoveisportugal.co.il/en/il/faq/?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=w04-launch-il#en-buy-abroad

One number

4.8%. The gross yield in Funchal: the median rent of new leases, €14.03 per m² a month, times twelve, over the median sale price of €3,538 per m² (INE, mid-2026), before costs and tax. If you let the home, Portugal taxes the rent, at 25% for non-residents, and under the 2006 treaty Israel gives a credit for that Portuguese tax.
