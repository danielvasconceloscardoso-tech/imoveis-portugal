This week's home

Our partner agency's first home goes here as soon as it sends one with permission to publish. Until then, this block runs the cost example: the bill for a €500,000 home if you aren't a Portuguese tax resident.

  • Price: €500,000
  • Transfer tax (IMT), 7.5% flat for non-residents: €37,500
  • Stamp duty, 0.8%: €4,000
  • Deed and registration at Casa Pronta: €375 (lawyer's fees are separate)
  • Total: €541,875, about $606,500 at the ECB reference rate of October 23, 2026 ($1.1193 to the euro)

The law lists exceptions to the 7.5%. In two of them the difference is refunded on request: you become a Portuguese tax resident within two years, or you let the home at a capped rent within six months, for at least 36 of the first 60 months. A third covers anyone who "has been considered" a Portuguese tax resident. How that one reads if you lived in Portugal years ago is a question for a partner tax adviser: https://imoveisportugal.pt/en/us/talk-to-a-partner/?cat=tax&utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=w04-launch-us#form

The story

Portugal house prices are easier to read as space. At the median price per m² of homes sold in the year to mid-2026 (INE), €500,000, about $559,650, buys:

  • 96 m² in Lisbon
  • 102 m² in Cascais
  • 121 m² in Loulé, in the Algarve
  • 138 m² in Porto
  • 141 m² in Funchal, Madeira
  • 197 m² in Grândola, Comporta's municipality

That last one is the municipality median: homes by the Comporta beaches sell for more. Across the country, the same budget buys more than 100 m² in 303 of the 304 municipalities with an INE figure, and more than 200 m² in 263.

Ten years ago, at Lisbon's early-2016 median of €1,875 per m², the same money bought 267 m² in the capital.

Medians aren't homes: they're the middle of everything sold in a year, old and new. They tell you where your money goes further, not what a given apartment costs.

The most-searched question

No reader questions yet. Americans search "imt portugal" most, and the bill above answers it; next comes "nif portugal".

The NIF is your Portuguese tax number and the first document of any purchase. You can get it from abroad, online through a legal representative, and you don't need a tax representative to get it. Once you own the home, you have 15 days to name a tax representative or sign up for the tax office's electronic notifications; from outside the EU, one of the two is required.

On the US side, money waiting in a Portuguese bank account counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold. If you later live in Portugal, the Form 8938 thresholds for taxpayers abroad are $200,000 at year end or $300,000 at any time ($400,000 and $600,000 for joint filers).
