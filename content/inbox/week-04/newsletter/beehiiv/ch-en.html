  • Deed and registration at Casa Pronta: €375

There are exceptions to the 7.5%: if you become a Portuguese tax resident within two years, the difference is refunded on request. If you once lived in Portugal, the exception for anyone who "has been considered a tax resident" is a question for a partner lawyer.

On the Swiss side, 2nd-pillar money can be withdrawn early for your main residence, abroad too (minimum CHF 20,000), but not for a vacation home. It lowers your future pension and is paid back when you sell. In 2016, at €1,088/m², the same €300,000 bought 276 m² in Porto: prices there are up 234% in ten years.

A median isn't a home: it's the middle of everything sold in a year, old and new. It shows where your money buys more space, not what one apartment costs, nor the most sought-after spots, such as the Comporta coast.

The most-searched question

No reader questions yet, so here's the most-searched one in our FAQ: can I buy a home in Portugal if I live abroad? Yes, and the IMT code even has its own rate for non-residents. You'll need a NIF, the Portuguese tax number, which you can get from Switzerland through a representative. Outside the EU and the EEA, as in Switzerland, you have 15 days after the purchase to name a tax representative or sign up for the tax office's electronic notifications. The deed can be signed by power of attorney, at a Portuguese consulate or before a Swiss notary with the Hague Apostille.

A partner lawyer can handle all of this: https://imoveisportugal.ch/en/ch/talk-to-a-partner/?cat=lawyer&utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=w04-launch-ch#form
