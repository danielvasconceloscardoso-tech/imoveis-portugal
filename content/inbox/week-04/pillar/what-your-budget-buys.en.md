Portugal house prices have a median of €2,264 per square meter, according to INE, Portugal's national statistics office.[[ine-m2-2026q2]] With €300,000, that's about 58 m² in Lisbon, 83 m² in Porto and 118 m² in Grândola, the municipality Comporta belongs to.

The figure covers the 12 months to the second quarter of 2026. A year earlier it was €1,923. Imóveis Portugal last checked it on October 25, 2026.

You don't need to visit Portugal to know what your budget buys there. The official figures show it, municipality by municipality.

## What does a median price tell you?

For each municipality, INE publishes the median value per square meter of family homes sold in the last 12 months. It covers new and existing homes.

The median is the middle value. Half the sales were above it and half below. These are real sales, not asking prices in listings.

A median isn't a home. It shows where your money buys more space, not what one apartment costs. It also leaves out the most wanted spots inside a municipality, such as the Comporta coast within Grândola.

INE has a figure for 304 of the country's 308 municipalities.[[caop-2025]]

## What does €300,000 or €1 million buy in Portugal?

The math is the budget divided by the municipality's median.

| Place | Median per m² | With €300,000 | With €1,000,000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lisbon | €5,192 | 58 m² | 193 m² |
| Cascais | €4,889 | 61 m² | 205 m² |
| The Algarve (Loulé) | €4,137 | 73 m² | 242 m² |
| Porto | €3,631 | 83 m² | 275 m² |
| Madeira (Funchal) | €3,538 | 85 m² | 283 m² |
| Grândola, Comporta's municipality (municipality median) | €2,543 | 118 m² | 393 m² |

With €300,000, you get more than 100 m² in 281 of the 304 municipalities with an official figure. You get more than 200 m² in 189 of them. With €1 million, you get more than 200 m² in every municipality but one.[[ine-m2-2026q2]]

## How have Portugal house prices changed in ten years?

INE's series starts in the first quarter of 2016. The method changed in the fourth quarter of 2019. The charts on the site mark that break.[[ine-m2-history]]

| Place | Q1 2016 | Q2 2026 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lisbon | €1,875 | €5,192 | +177% |
| Cascais | €1,599 | €4,889 | +206% |
| The Algarve (Loulé) | €1,564 | €4,137 | +165% |
| Porto | €1,088 | €3,631 | +234% |
| Madeira (Funchal) | €1,227 | €3,538 | +188% |
| Grândola (Comporta) | €770 | €2,543 | +230% |
| Portugal | €830 | €2,264 | +173% |

In 2016, €300,000 bought 160 m² in Lisbon. Past prices say nothing about the next ten years.

## How much rent does a home earn?

INE also publishes the median rent per square meter of new leases.[[ine-rent-2026q2]] Gross yield is one year's rent divided by the price, before costs and tax.

In Lisbon, the median rent is €17.43 per m² a month, a gross yield of 4.0%. In Porto it's €14.48, or 4.8%. Cascais gives 4.1%, Funchal 4.8%, Loulé 3.6% and Grândola 5.3%.

If you're a non-resident, rent is taxed in Portugal at 25%, with reductions for long leases.[[pt-rent-tax-nonresident]]

## What does buying cost besides the price?

If you aren't a Portuguese tax resident, the transfer tax (IMT) is 7.5% of the price, unless an exception applies.[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]] Add stamp duty of 0.8% and the deed and registration at Casa Pronta, €375 without a mortgage.[[pt-is-purchase]][[pt-casa-pronta-fees]] On a €300,000 home, the total cost with taxes, deed and registration is €325,275; on a €1 million home, €1,083,375.

Casa Pronta is the public counter where the deed is signed and the home is registered. You can run your own price in the [total-cost calculator]({{sims}}total-cost/?price=300000&resident=0). Every line of the bill is in our [article on the total cost of buying]({{post:total-cost-of-buying}}).

The law has three exceptions to the flat rate.[[pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions]] One covers anyone who "has been considered a tax resident" in Portugal. If you once lived in Portugal, whether that applies to you is a question for a partner lawyer or tax adviser before you sign the promissory contract.

## What changes in the country where you live?

**Luxembourg.** One square meter of an existing apartment in Luxembourg City costs €10,269 on average: about 2 m² in Lisbon or 4 m² in Grândola.[[lu-m2-2026q2]] That's a mean, not a median, for July 2025 to June 2026. If you rent the home out, the tax treaty lets Portugal tax the rent. Luxembourg exempts it, but may count it when it sets your tax rate.[[lu-pt-treaty-property]]

**France.** The median square meter of an existing apartment in Paris is €9,692, about 1.9 m² in Lisbon.[[fr-m2-2026q3]] A bank account opened in Portugal is declared in France every year, on form 3916.[[fr-3916]]

**Switzerland.** The median square meter of a condominium apartment in the canton of Geneva was CHF 10,853 in 2024, about €11,762 at the ECB rate of October 23, 2026, or 2.3 m² in Lisbon.[[ch-ge-m2-2024]][[ecb-eur-chf]] Second-pillar money can be withdrawn early for your main residence, abroad too, but not for a vacation home.[[ch-2nd-pillar-abroad]] Outside the EU, once you own the home, you name a tax representative or sign up for electronic notifications.[[pt-tax-rep]]

**Brazil.** €300,000 is about R$1.69 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-brl]] With €1 million, assets abroad pass $1 million, the threshold for the annual declaration to Brazil's central bank (CBE).[[br-cbe]]

**United States.** €300,000 is about $335,800, and €1 million about $1.12 million.[[ecb-eur-usd]] The FBAR is the yearly US report of foreign bank accounts. A Portuguese bank account counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold.[[us-fbar]]

**Israel.** €300,000 is about ₪1.05 million.[[ecb-eur-ils]] If you rent the home out, Portugal may tax the rent. Israel gives a credit for the Portuguese tax.[[il-pt-treaty-property]]
