Buying property in Madeira works as it does anywhere in Portugal, and you can buy as a non-resident.[[pt-nonresidents-buy]] In the year to mid-2026, the median home sold for €3,538 per square meter in Funchal, the capital. Across the region, the median was €2,690.[[ine-m2-2026q2]]

These figures come from INE, Portugal's national statistics office. Imóveis Portugal last checked them on October 25, 2026.

You don't need to move to Madeira to buy there. Here's how the prices, the steps and the costs work.

## How much does a home in Madeira cost?

The Autonomous Region of Madeira has 11 municipalities. Ten are on Madeira itself, and Porto Santo is on the neighboring island.[[caop-2025]]

For each one, INE publishes the median price per square meter of family homes sold in the last 12 months, new and existing. A median is the middle sale. It isn't a listing price, but it shows where your budget buys more space.

| Municipality | Q1 2016 | Q2 2026 | Change | Median rent per m² a month | Gross yield |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Funchal | €1,227 | €3,538 | +188% | €14.03 | 4.8% |
| Santa Cruz | €888 | €2,853 | +221% | €11.46 | 4.8% |
| Machico | €760 | €2,070 | +172% | €7.08 | 4.1% |
| Calheta | €791 | €1,865 | +136% | €6.08 | 3.9% |
| Ponta do Sol | €715 | €1,767 | +147% | no current figure | no current figure |

INE's price series starts in early 2016, and its method changed at the end of 2019.[[ine-m2-history]] Rents are INE's median for new leases in the year to mid-2026.[[ine-rent-2026q2]] For Ponta do Sol, INE has a rent figure only for 2021.[[ine-rent-history]]

Gross yield is one year of median rent divided by the median price, before costs and tax. Past prices say nothing about the next ten years.

At the median, €400,000 buys about 113 m² in Funchal and 226 m² in Ponta do Sol.[[ine-m2-2026q2]] Type your budget into [the map]({{map}}?budget=400000) to see every municipality on the island.

## How do I fly to Madeira?

Madeira airport handled 5,390,975 passengers in 2025.[[eurostat-lpma-2025]] Lisbon and Porto are the busiest routes.

In 2024, the latest year Eurostat publishes by route, 1,393,914 passengers flew between Madeira and Lisbon. Another 606,548 flew between Madeira and Porto.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] The country notes below give the routes from Luxembourg, France and Switzerland.

## How do I buy a home in Madeira while living abroad?

1. **Get a NIF, your Portuguese tax number.** A legal representative can request it online. You don't need a tax representative to get it.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]
2. **Choose the municipality.** The map narrows the search, and the first visits can be on video.
3. **Sign a power of attorney.** You sign it at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with a Hague Apostille. Someone can then sign in your name.[[pt-poa-apostille]]
4. **Arrange the loan.** Banco de Portugal caps new mortgages at 90% of the value for your permanent home. For other homes, such as a vacation home, the cap is 80%.[[pt-ltv-limits]]
5. **Sign the CPCV, the promissory contract.** It usually comes with a deposit. If you back out, the seller keeps it. If the seller backs out, you can claim double.[[pt-sinal-442]]
6. **Pay the taxes and sign the escritura, the deed.** The next section shows the numbers.
7. **Set up your tax contact.** If you live outside the EU and the EEA, you have 15 days after the deed to act. You can name a tax representative or join the tax authority's electronic notices.[[pt-tax-rep]]

Each step has its own page in [Step by step]({{journey}}).

## What does it cost to buy a €400,000 home in Madeira?

If you aren't a Portuguese tax resident, the transfer tax (IMT) on a home is a flat 7.5%, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies.[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]] Stamp duty is 0.8%.[[pt-is-purchase]] On €400,000, that's €30,000 of IMT and €3,200 of stamp duty: €433,200 before the deed and registration.

The deed cost on the island isn't on this page yet. We haven't confirmed that the national Casa Pronta prices apply there, so a partner lawyer gives you that figure. The [calculators]({{sims}}) run the sum with your own price.

The law has three exceptions to the flat rate.[[pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions]] One covers anyone who "has been considered a tax resident" in Portugal; another, anyone who becomes one within two years of the purchase. If you once lived in Portugal, how the first exception applies to you is a question for a partner lawyer or tax adviser, before you sign the promissory contract.

Each year after that, IMI, the municipal property tax, is 0.3% to 0.45% of the home's tax value. The municipality sets the rate.[[pt-imi-rates]]

## Can I rent out a home in Madeira?

If you're not a Portuguese tax resident, rent from a home in Portugal is taxed in Portugal at 25%. Leases of five years or more get reductions.[[pt-rent-tax-nonresident]]

Short-term rental has its own registration rules. We haven't checked how they apply in the autonomous region, so a partner lawyer confirms them for your address.

## What changes with the country I live in?

**Luxembourg.** The Madeira–Luxembourg route carried 27,909 passengers in 2024.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] Under the tax treaty, Portugal may tax the rent. Luxembourg exempts it but may count it to set your rate.[[lu-pt-treaty-property]]

**France.** In 2024, 79,943 passengers flew between Madeira and Paris-Orly, and 37,043 to Lyon. Beauvais had 35,812 and Charles de Gaulle 28,811.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] A Portuguese bank account goes on form 3916 every year.[[fr-3916]]

**Switzerland.** Zurich carried 54,800 passengers to and from Madeira in 2024, Basel-Mulhouse 31,967 and Geneva 26,853.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] €400,000 is about CHF 369,080 at the ECB reference rate of October 23, 2026.[[ecb-eur-chf]] Second-pillar pension money can go to your main home abroad, not to a vacation home.[[ch-2nd-pillar-abroad]] Switzerland exempts rent and gains taxed in Portugal but may use them to set your rate.[[ch-pt-treaty-property]]

**Brazil.** €400,000 is about R$2.26 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-brl]] The home goes into the IRPF, the Brazilian income tax return, under Bens e Direitos. The price is converted to dollars, then to reais, at the Banco Central selling rate on the purchase date.[[br-bens-exterior]]

**United States.** €400,000 is about $447,720 at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-usd]] A Portuguese bank account counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold.[[us-fbar]] Eurostat's 2024 table has no Madeira–US route. Routes change by season, and the airlines publish their own schedules.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]]

**Israel.** €400,000 is about ₪1.40 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-ils]] Portugal may tax the rent, and Israel gives a credit for the Portuguese tax.[[il-pt-treaty-property]]
