The New Year's Eve fireworks in Madeira, often searched for as the Funchal fireworks, are a show the regional government buys every year through a public tender. One procedure covers two years: the 2025/2026 show, with a base price of €1,155,000, and the 2026/2027 show, with a base price of €1,212,750.[[madeira-nye-fireworks-2026]] Imóveis Portugal read the notices in the EU's Official Journal on October 27, 2026, and checked the flights, the island's hotel capacity and its home prices the same week.

## The regional government buys the New Year's show through a public tender

The buyer is the Secretaria Regional do Turismo e Cultura, the Madeira government's department for tourism and culture, based in Funchal. Its 2025 notice, "Espetáculo de Fogo-de-Artifício para as Festas de Passagem do ano 2025/2026 e 2026/2027", split the work into two lots, one per New Year, and both were awarded to the same pyrotechnics company.[[madeira-nye-fireworks-2026]] The notice lists the place of performance as the whole region. A base price is the ceiling the tender sets, not necessarily the amount paid.

What the notice doesn't give: the number of firing points, the length of the show or its size. This page doesn't quote them, and it doesn't cite a world record either, because we couldn't open the official sources for those figures when we checked. For the night's program, times and viewing areas, the regional government's and Visit Madeira's own announcements are the reference.

## December is a busy month at Madeira airport

"How do people actually get there for New Year's?" By air, and Lisbon and Porto are the two busiest routes. Madeira airport handled 374,369 passengers in December 2025, and 5,390,975 over the whole year.[[eurostat-lpma-2025]] In 2024, the latest year Eurostat publishes by route, 1,393,914 passengers flew between Madeira and Lisbon, and 606,548 between Madeira and Porto.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] Routes from Luxembourg, France and Switzerland are in the country sections below. Routes change by season, and the airlines publish their own schedules.

## Six in ten of Madeira's tourist beds are in Funchal

"Where do people stay?" INE's count of tourist accommodation shows where the beds are. In 2025, establishments in the Madeira region could host 42,789 people at once; 25,667 of those places were in Funchal, 22,685 of them in hotels and similar establishments.[[ine-tourism-capacity-2025]] Next came Santa Cruz with 4,693, Machico with 2,307, Calheta with 2,087 and Ponta do Sol with 456. INE's count covers the establishments in its survey, so it's a floor, not every bed on the island.

## A December trip can move a purchase forward

"If I'm on the island anyway, can I start buying?" Yes, and the paperwork can be ready before you land.

1. **A tax number first.** A legal representative can request your NIF online, without a tax representative.[[pt-nif-nonresident]] With it, you can make an offer while you're there.
2. **Visits on the island.** First viewings can happen on video before the trip, so the days on the island go to the homes on your list.
3. **No second flight for the deed.** A power of attorney signed at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with the Hague Apostille, lets someone sign the deed for you.[[pt-poa-apostille]]
4. **The tax on the purchase.** If you aren't a Portuguese tax resident, IMT on a home is a flat 7.5%, plus stamp duty of 0.8%, unless an exception applies.[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]][[pt-is-purchase]] One exception covers anyone who "has been considered a tax resident" in Portugal; if you once lived here, how it applies to you is a question for a partner lawyer or tax adviser.[[pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions]]

The [total-cost calculator](/en/us/calculators/total-cost/?price=400000&resident=0) runs your own price, and [your path](/en/us/your-path/?door=buy&step=1) lists every station.

**France.** In 2024, 79,943 passengers flew between Madeira and Paris-Orly, 37,043 to Lyon and 28,811 to Charles de Gaulle.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] A bank account opened in Portugal for a purchase goes on form 3916 every year.[[fr-3916]]

**Switzerland.** Zurich carried 54,800 passengers to and from Madeira in 2024, Basel-Mulhouse 31,967 and Geneva 26,853.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] €400,000 is about CHF 369,080 at the ECB reference rate of October 23, 2026.[[ecb-eur-chf]] 2nd-pillar money can go to your main residence abroad, not to a vacation home.[[ch-2nd-pillar-abroad]]

**Brazil.** €400,000 is about R$2.26 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-brl]] Eurostat's 2024 route table has no Madeira–Brazil route; Lisbon and Porto are the largest connections in it.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]]

**United States.** €400,000 is about $447,720 at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-usd]] A Portuguese bank account counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold.[[us-fbar]] Eurostat's 2024 table has no Madeira–US route; check the airlines' current schedules.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]]

## Questions

**Who pays for the Madeira New Year's Eve fireworks?**

The Madeira regional government. Its department for tourism and culture buys the show through a public tender published in the EU's Official Journal. The 2025 procedure covers two New Years: the 2025/2026 show, with a base price of €1,155,000, and the 2026/2027 show, with a base price of €1,212,750. Both lots went to the same pyrotechnics company.

**How do I get to Madeira for New Year's Eve?**

By plane, to Madeira airport, which handled 374,369 passengers in December 2025. Lisbon and Porto are the largest routes: in 2024, 1.39 million passengers flew Madeira–Lisbon and 607,000 Madeira–Porto. There were also routes to Paris, Lyon, Zurich, Geneva, Basel-Mulhouse and Luxembourg. Routes change by season, so the airlines' own schedules are the reference.

**Where do people stay in Madeira over New Year's?**

INE's count shows where the beds are. In 2025, tourist establishments in the region could host 42,789 people at once, and 25,667 of those places were in Funchal, most of them in hotels. Santa Cruz had 4,693, Machico 2,307 and Calheta 2,087. The count covers the establishments in INE's survey, so it doesn't include every bed on the island.

**Can I start buying a home while I'm in Madeira for New Year's?**

Yes. With a NIF, which a legal representative can request online before you travel, you can make an offer while you're there. A power of attorney lets someone sign the deed later, so you don't need a second trip. If you aren't a Portuguese tax resident, IMT on a home is a flat 7.5%, unless an exception applies.
