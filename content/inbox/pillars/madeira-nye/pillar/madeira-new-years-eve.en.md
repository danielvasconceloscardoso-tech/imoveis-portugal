Madeira New Year's Eve is famous for the Funchal fireworks, a show the regional government buys every year through a public tender. The 2026/2027 show has a base price of €1,212,750, and the 2025/2026 show had one of €1,155,000.[[madeira-nye-fireworks-2026]]

Imóveis Portugal read the notices in the EU's Official Journal on October 27, 2026. The same week, it checked the flights, the island's hotel capacity and its home prices.

## Who pays for the Madeira New Year's Eve fireworks?

The buyer is the Secretaria Regional do Turismo e Cultura, the Madeira government's department for tourism and culture.

Its 2025 tender covers two New Year's Eves, one lot for each. Both lots went to the same pyrotechnics company.[[madeira-nye-fireworks-2026]] The notice lists the whole region as the place of the show.

A base price is the ceiling the tender sets. The amount paid can be lower.

The notice doesn't give the number of firing points or the length of the show. This page doesn't quote them, or any world record. The regional government and Visit Madeira, the island's tourism board, announce the program, the times and the viewing areas.

## How do I fly to Madeira for New Year's Eve?

Madeira airport handled 374,369 passengers in December 2025, and 5,390,975 over the whole year.[[eurostat-lpma-2025]]

Lisbon and Porto are the two busiest routes. In 2024, the latest year Eurostat publishes by route, 1,393,914 passengers flew between Madeira and Lisbon. Another 606,548 flew between Madeira and Porto.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]]

The country notes below give the routes from Luxembourg, France and Switzerland. Routes change by season, and the airlines publish their own schedules.

## Where can I stay in Madeira over New Year's?

INE, Portugal's national statistics office, counts the beds in tourist accommodation. In 2025, establishments in the Madeira region could host 42,789 people at once.[[ine-tourism-capacity-2025]]

Funchal had 25,667 of those places, 22,685 of them in hotels and similar establishments. Santa Cruz followed with 4,693, then Machico with 2,307, Calheta with 2,087 and Ponta do Sol with 456.

INE's count covers the establishments in its survey. The island has more beds than that.

## Can I start buying a home during a December trip?

You can start buying while you're on the island for New Year's. The paperwork can be ready before you land.

1. **Get a NIF first.** The NIF is your Portuguese tax number. A legal representative can request it online, without a tax representative.[[pt-nif-nonresident]] With it, you can make an offer while you're there.
2. **See the homes on video first.** First viewings can happen before the trip. Your days on the island then go to the homes on your list.
3. **Sign a power of attorney.** You sign it at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with a Hague Apostille. Someone can then sign the deed for you, with no second flight.[[pt-poa-apostille]]
4. **The tax on the purchase.** If you aren't a Portuguese tax resident, IMT on a home is a flat 7.5%, plus stamp duty of 0.8%, unless an exception applies.[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]][[pt-is-purchase]] One exception covers anyone who "has been considered a tax resident" in Portugal; if you once lived here, how it applies to you is a question for a partner lawyer or tax adviser.[[pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions]]

The [calculators]({{sims}}) run your own price. [Step by step]({{journey}}) has a page for each station.

## What changes with the country I live in?

**Luxembourg.** The Madeira–Luxembourg route carried 27,909 passengers in 2024.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]]

**France.** In 2024, 79,943 passengers flew between Madeira and Paris-Orly, 37,043 to Lyon and 28,811 to Charles de Gaulle.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] A Portuguese bank account opened for a purchase goes on form 3916 every year.[[fr-3916]]

**Switzerland.** Zurich carried 54,800 passengers to and from Madeira in 2024, Basel-Mulhouse 31,967 and Geneva 26,853.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]] €400,000 is about CHF 369,080 at the ECB reference rate of October 23, 2026.[[ecb-eur-chf]] Second-pillar pension money can go to your main home abroad, not to a vacation home.[[ch-2nd-pillar-abroad]]

**Brazil.** €400,000 is about R$2.26 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-brl]] Eurostat's 2024 route table has no Madeira–Brazil route. Lisbon and Porto are the largest connections in it.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]]

**United States.** €400,000 is about $447,720 at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-usd]] A Portuguese bank account counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold.[[us-fbar]] Eurostat's 2024 table has no Madeira–US route, and the airlines publish their current schedules.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]]

**Israel.** €400,000 is about ₪1.40 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-ils]] Eurostat's 2024 table has no Madeira–Israel route.[[eurostat-lpma-routes-2024]]

## Who can start the paperwork before my flight?

A partner lawyer can request your NIF online and prepare the power of attorney. They can also check the documents of the homes you plan to see. All of this can start a few weeks before the trip.
