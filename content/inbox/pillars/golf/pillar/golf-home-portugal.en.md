Golf property in Portugal sells at the prices of the municipality around the course. In the year to mid-2026, the median home sold for €4,137 per square meter in Loulé. In Grândola, the municipality of Comporta, it was €2,543.[[ine-m2-2026q2]] These figures come from INE, Portugal's national statistics office. Imóveis Portugal last checked them on October 25, 2026.

You don't need to live in Portugal to buy a home on a golf resort. Here's how the prices, the steps and the costs work.

## How much does golf property cost in the Algarve and Comporta?

INE doesn't publish a price for homes on golf courses. It publishes one for each municipality.

That figure is the median value per square meter of family homes sold in the last 12 months, new and existing. Half the sales were above it, half below.

So a villa by a fairway and a flat in town count in the same number. The resort and the house set the final price.

Here are five municipalities with golf resorts: four on the Algarve, and Grândola on the Alentejo coast.

| Municipality | Q1 2016 | Q2 2026 | Change | Median rent per m² a month | Gross yield |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Loulé | €1,564 | €4,137 | +165% | €12.33 | 3.6% |
| Lagos | €1,518 | €3,949 | +160% | €12.77 | 3.9% |
| Albufeira | €1,333 | €3,490 | +162% | €12.22 | 4.2% |
| Lagoa | €1,353 | €3,371 | +149% | €9.93 | 3.5% |
| Grândola (Comporta) | €770 | €2,543 | +230% | €11.13 | 5.3% |

INE's price series starts in the first quarter of 2016. Its method changed at the end of 2019.[[ine-m2-history]] Rents are INE's median for new leases in the 12 months to mid-2026.[[ine-rent-2026q2]]

Gross yield is one year of median rent divided by the median price, before costs and tax. Past prices say nothing about the next ten years.

At the median, €500,000 buys about 121 m² in Loulé and 197 m² in Grândola.[[ine-m2-2026q2]] [The map]({{map}}?budget=500000) has a golf layer. It shows each course, its municipality and what your budget buys there.

## How do I buy a golf home while living abroad?

The order is the same as for any home in Portugal. One thing is added: you read the resort's own documents.

1. **Get a NIF, your Portuguese tax number.** A legal representative can request it online. You don't need a tax representative to get it.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]
2. **Choose the municipality.** The map narrows the search, and the first visits can be on video.
3. **Sign a power of attorney.** You sign it at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with a Hague Apostille. Someone can then sign in your name.[[pt-poa-apostille]]
4. **Arrange the loan.** For a vacation home, Banco de Portugal caps the loan at 80% of the value. For your permanent home, the cap is 90%.[[pt-ltv-limits]]
5. **Sign the CPCV, the promissory contract.** Before you sign, a partner lawyer reads the resort's rules and yearly charges.
6. **Sign the escritura, the deed.** Casa Pronta, the state's one-stop deed service, lists the papers. You need the IDs and tax numbers of both sides and the energy certificate. Add the technical file if one exists, and the usage licence or its waiver.[[pt-casa-pronta-docs-all]]

Each step has its own page in [Step by step]({{journey}}).

## What does it cost to buy a €500,000 golf home?

If you aren't a Portuguese tax resident, the transfer tax (IMT) on a home is a flat 7.5%, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies.[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]] Stamp duty adds 0.8% of the price. At Casa Pronta, the deed and registration cost €375 without a mortgage.[[pt-is-purchase]][[pt-casa-pronta-fees]]

On €500,000, that's €37,500 of IMT, €4,000 of stamp duty and €375 for the deed: a total cost with taxes, deed and registration of €541,875. The [calculators]({{sims}}) run the same sum with your own price.

The law has three exceptions to the flat rate.[[pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions]] One covers anyone who "has been considered a tax resident" in Portugal; another, anyone who becomes one within two years of the purchase. If you once lived in Portugal, how the first exception applies to you is a question for a partner lawyer or tax adviser, before you sign the promissory contract.

The [article on IMT for non-residents]({{post:imt-nonresidents}}) explains the rate in more detail.

## What do owners pay each year?

IMI, the yearly municipal property tax, is 0.3% to 0.45% of the home's tax value. Each municipality sets its own rate.[[pt-imi-rates]]

AIMI, an additional property tax, applies only to the part of a person's urban property tax values above €600,000.[[pt-aimi-deduction]]

## Can I rent out a golf home?

Many owners play in spring and autumn and rent the home out in between. Short-term rental, called alojamento local, needs an online registration first. The municipality can object within 60 days, or 90 days in the containment areas it sets.[[pt-al-registration]]

If you're not a Portuguese tax resident, rent from a home in Portugal is taxed in Portugal at 25%. Leases of five years or more get reductions.[[pt-rent-tax-nonresident]]

The yields in the table come before the resort's charges, IMI and that tax.

## What changes with the country I live in?

**Luxembourg.** Under the tax treaty, Portugal may tax the rent. Luxembourg exempts it but may count it to set the rate on your other income.[[lu-pt-treaty-property]]

**France.** Rent and a gain on selling the home may be taxed in Portugal under the treaty.[[fr-pt-treaty-source]] A Portuguese bank account goes on form 3916 every year, even if you rarely use it.[[fr-3916]]

**Switzerland.** €500,000 is about CHF 461,350 at the ECB reference rate of October 23, 2026.[[ecb-eur-chf]] Second-pillar pension money can be withdrawn early for your main home, in Switzerland or abroad. A second home doesn't qualify.[[ch-2nd-pillar-abroad]] Switzerland exempts rent and gains taxed in Portugal but may use them to set your rate.[[ch-pt-treaty-property]]

**Brazil.** €500,000 is about R$2.82 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-brl]] The home goes into the IRPF, the Brazilian income tax return, under Bens e Direitos. The price is converted to dollars, then to reais, at the Banco Central selling rate on the purchase date.[[br-bens-exterior]]

**United States.** €500,000 is about $559,650 at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-usd]] A gain on selling the home may be taxed in Portugal. The US taxes its citizens too and gives a foreign tax credit, so you don't pay twice.[[us-pt-treaty-gains]] A Portuguese bank account counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold.[[us-fbar]]

**Israel.** €500,000 is about ₪1.75 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-ils]] Portugal may tax the rent, and Israel gives a credit for the Portuguese tax.[[il-pt-treaty-property]]

## Who can add up the numbers for my case?

A partner tax adviser puts IMT, IMI, the rent tax and your own country's tax into one yearly figure. Your residence, your income and the resort's charges all go into it.

A partner lawyer handles the documents, the promissory contract and the deed.
