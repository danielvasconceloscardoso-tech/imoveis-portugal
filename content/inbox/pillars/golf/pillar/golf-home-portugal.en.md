INE, Portugal's national statistics office, doesn't publish a price for homes on golf courses. It publishes one for the municipalities where the courses are. Over the 12 months to the second quarter of 2026, the median home sold for €4,137 per square meter in Loulé, €3,949 in Lagos, €3,490 in Albufeira, €3,371 in Lagoa and €2,543 in Grândola, Comporta's municipality (municipality median).[[ine-m2-2026q2]] Imóveis Portugal last checked these figures on October 25, 2026, and keeps each municipality's ten-year line on the [house prices](/en/us/house-prices/) page.

## A golf home sits inside a municipality median

"I want a house by a fairway. What does that cost?" The honest answer starts one level up. For each municipality, INE publishes the median value per square meter of family homes actually sold in the last 12 months, new and existing. Half the sales were above that figure, half below. It's a price for a whole municipality, from town apartments to villas, not a price for a home on a course.

That matters on the Algarve and around Comporta, where a resort with a course is one corner of a larger market. Where INE also publishes parish figures, Imóveis Portugal compares the parish with its municipality: see the golf premium data. That comparison describes a gap between two medians. It doesn't say the course caused it.

The golf layer on [the map](/en/us/map/) shows where the courses are, so you can see which municipality, and which median, a resort belongs to.

## Five golf municipalities over ten years

This page follows five municipalities with golf resorts: Loulé, Lagoa, Lagos and Albufeira on the Algarve, and Grândola on the Alentejo coast. At the median, €500,000 is about 121 m² in Loulé, 127 m² in Lagos, 143 m² in Albufeira, 148 m² in Lagoa and 197 m² in Grândola.[[ine-m2-2026q2]] Type your own budget into [the map](/en/us/map/?budget=500000) to see the square meters in every municipality.

## How a purchase on a golf resort works, step by step

"The resort is in Portugal and I'm not. Where do I even start?" The order is the same as for any home, with one extra read of the resort's documents.

1. **A tax number.** A legal representative can request your Portuguese NIF online; you don't need a tax representative to get it.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]
2. **Where to look.** The map and the house prices page narrow the municipality; the first visits can be on video.
3. **Someone to sign for you.** A power of attorney signed at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with the Hague Apostille, lets someone sign in your name.[[pt-poa-apostille]]
4. **Finance.** For a home that isn't your own permanent residence, such as a vacation home, Banco de Portugal caps the loan at 80% of the value; for a permanent home, 90%.[[pt-ltv-limits]]
5. **The promissory contract (CPCV).** Before you sign it, a partner lawyer reads the resort's own rules and charges, and checks the documents the deed will need.
6. **The deed.** Casa Pronta lists what the deed needs: the identification and tax numbers of both sides, the technical file if one exists, the usage licence or its waiver, and the energy certificate.[[pt-casa-pronta-docs-all]]
7. **After the purchase.** IMI, the yearly municipal tax, is 0.3% to 0.45% of the home's tax value, at the rate the municipality sets.[[pt-imi-rates]] The additional IMI (AIMI) only applies to the part of an individual's urban property tax values above €600,000.[[pt-aimi-deduction]]

Each station has its page on [Step by step](/en/us/step-by-step/?door=buy&step=1).

## The cost of buying a €500,000 golf home

If you aren't a Portuguese tax resident, the transfer tax (IMT) on a home is a flat 7.5%, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies.[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]] Add stamp duty of 0.8% and the deed and registration at Casa Pronta, €375 without a mortgage.[[pt-is-purchase]][[pt-casa-pronta-fees]]

On €500,000, that's €37,500 of IMT, €4,000 of stamp duty and €375 for the deed: a total cost with taxes, deed and registration of €541,875. Run your own price in the [total-cost calculator](/en/us/calculators/total-cost/?price=500000&resident=0).

The law has three exceptions to the flat rate.[[pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions]] One covers anyone who "has been considered a tax resident" in Portugal; another, anyone who becomes one within two years of the purchase. If you once lived in Portugal, how the first exception applies to you is a question for a partner lawyer or tax adviser, before you sign the promissory contract.

## Renting the home out while you're away

"I'll play in spring and autumn. Can the house earn something the rest of the year?" Short-term rental (alojamento local) needs prior registration through the government's online desk, and the municipality can object within 60 days, or 90 days in the containment areas it sets.[[pt-al-registration]] If you're not a Portuguese tax resident, rent from a home in Portugal is taxed in Portugal at 25%, with reductions for leases of five years or more.[[pt-rent-tax-nonresident]] The gross yields in the table above are a starting point, before the resort's charges, IMI and that tax.

## Where you live changes the paperwork

### Luxembourg

If you rent the home out, the Portugal–Luxembourg treaty lets Portugal tax the rent; Luxembourg exempts it but may take it into account to set the rate on the rest of your income.[[lu-pt-treaty-property]]

### France

Rent from the home and a gain on selling it may be taxed in Portugal under the Portugal–France treaty.[[fr-pt-treaty-source]] A Portuguese bank account opened to pay the resort's charges goes on form 3916 every year, even if you rarely use it.[[fr-3916]]

### Switzerland

€500,000 is about CHF 461,350 at the ECB reference rate of October 23, 2026.[[ecb-eur-chf]] 2nd-pillar money can be withdrawn early for your main residence, in Switzerland or abroad, but not for a second home.[[ch-2nd-pillar-abroad]] Rent and gains may be taxed in Portugal; Switzerland exempts them but may apply the rate it would have used.[[ch-pt-treaty-property]]

### Brazil

€500,000 is about R$2.82 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-brl]] The home goes into the IRPF under Bens e Direitos, at the purchase price converted to dollars and then to reais at the Banco Central selling rate on the purchase date.[[br-bens-exterior]]

### United States

€500,000 is about $559,650 at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-usd]] A gain on selling the home may be taxed in Portugal, and the US taxes its citizens too, with a foreign tax credit to avoid paying twice.[[us-pt-treaty-gains]] A Portuguese bank account counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold.[[us-fbar]]

### Israel

€500,000 is about ₪1.75 million at the ECB rate.[[ecb-eur-ils]] If you rent the home out, Portugal may tax the rent and Israel gives a credit for the Portuguese tax.[[il-pt-treaty-property]]

## Questions

**How much does a home on a golf course cost in Portugal?**

INE doesn't publish a golf-only price. It publishes the median per square meter of homes sold in each municipality: €4,137 in Loulé, €3,949 in Lagos, €3,490 in Albufeira, €3,371 in Lagoa and €2,543 in Grândola, in the 12 months to mid-2026. A home on a course is one part of that market, and its price depends on the resort and the house.

**Can I rent out my golf home when I'm not using it?**

Yes, as short-term rental, once it's registered through the government's online desk. The municipality has 60 days to object, or 90 in containment areas. If you're not a Portuguese tax resident, the rent is taxed in Portugal at 25%, with reductions for long leases. Your own country may count it too. A partner tax adviser can work out the net figure.

**Do I pay the 7.5% IMT on a golf home?**

If you aren't a Portuguese tax resident, yes, unless an exception applies: IMT on a home is a flat 7.5%, plus 0.8% stamp duty. On €500,000, the total cost with taxes, deed and registration is €541,875. If you become a tax resident within two years, or once were one, a partner lawyer can tell you whether an exception covers you.

**Can I use my Swiss pension to buy a golf home in Portugal?**

Not from the 2nd pillar, if the home is a second home. Swiss rules allow an early withdrawal only for your main residence, in Switzerland or abroad, with a minimum of CHF 20,000. If you plan to live in the home, the rules are different. A partner tax adviser can look at your pension fund's conditions with you.
