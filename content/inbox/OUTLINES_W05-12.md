## Week 5 · Mon 7 Dec · A mortgage as a non-resident

- **Target:** PT "crédito habitação não residentes" · EN "portugal mortgage non resident" · FR "crédit immobilier portugal".
- **Page:** `mortgage-non-resident`; the monthly-payment and what-can-I-afford calculators; path, buy · 04 finance. Partner: bank.
- **Answer first:** Portuguese banks lend to non-residents within the Banco de Portugal limits: up to 90% of the value for a permanent home and 80% for other homes, with all loan repayments at most 45% of net income since 1 August 2026. Average new-mortgage rate and terms from the Banco de Portugal.

## Week 6 · Mon 14 Dec · Moving or retiring to Portugal: what changes

- **Target:** PT "morar em portugal" · EN "moving to portugal from usa" · FR "retraite au portugal".
- **Page:** `moving-to-portugal`; path, buy · 01 where. Partners: lawyer, tax adviser.
- **Answer first:** Buying a home does not give residence by itself; residence comes from a visa or, for EU citizens, registration. What changes for tax is the year you become resident (to add: the CIRS art. 16 residence test), with the IFICI regime for some professions.
- **Facts:** pt-visa-own-income, pt-cplp-residence, pt-ifici-20pct, pt-irs-68-2026, pt-ari-realestate-ended, pt-hpp-tax-domicile, pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions (becoming resident within two years).
- **Newsletter angle:** CH and LU, coming back after a career abroad; US and IL, the D7 visa route (to add: AIMA requirements, checked); BR, CPLP residence.
- **Posts:** myth-vs-law (golden visa through property ended), one-number (two years for the IMT refund), postcard (Porto, Lisbon), reader-question. Christmas peak: viewings while people are home (PLAN_CONTENT note).

## Week 7 · Mon 21 Dec · The yearly costs of owning: IMI and AIMI

- **Target:** PT "imi casa portugal" · EN "property tax portugal" · FR "impôt maison portugal".
- **Page:** `yearly-costs-imi`; the IMI calculator; path, buy · 09 after. Partner: tax adviser.
- **Answer first:** IMI is a yearly tax of 0.3% to 0.45% of the tax value of an urban home, set by each municipality; AIMI applies only above 600 000 € of tax value per person.
- **Facts:** pt-imi-rates, pt-aimi-rates, pt-aimi-deduction, pt-imi-offshore-75 (one line, for companies in listed regimes), pt-tax-rep.
- **Newsletter angle:** the same in every edition, with the yearly amount in the reader's currency; FR adds fr-3916 for the Portuguese bank account.
- **Posts:** one-number (0.3%), cost-sheet (a year of owning, illustrative), myth-vs-law, reader-question. Light week (Christmas).

## Week 8 · Mon 28 Dec · Renting out from abroad: rent, yield and tax

- **Target:** PT "arrendar casa em portugal" · EN "renting out property in portugal" · FR "louer son bien au portugal".
- **Page:** `renting-out-from-abroad`; gross yield on the map; the rental-yield calculator; path, buy · 09 after. Partner: tax adviser.
- **Answer first:** Rental income from a home in Portugal is taxed in Portugal at an autonomous 25% rate, reduced for contracts of five years or more, with the home country's treaty deciding what happens there. Gross yields by municipality from INE rents and prices.
- **Facts:** pt-rent-tax-nonresident, pt-al-registration, ine-rent-2026q2, ine-rent-history, ine-m2-2026q2; lu-pt-treaty-property, ch-pt-treaty-property, br-pt-treaty-property, il-pt-treaty-property, fr-pt-treaty-source.
- **Posts:** ten-years (rents), budget-buys with yield, myth-vs-law (local accommodation licence), reader-question.

## Week 9 · Mon 4 Jan · The inherited house: partilhas

- **Target:** PT "partilhas herança" · EN "inheriting property in portugal" · FR "héritage maison portugal".
- **Page:** `inherited-house`; path, inherit · 01. Partner: lawyer.
- **Answer first:** Children, spouses and parents who inherit are exempt from stamp duty on inheritance; the estate must be declared to the tax office, and the partilha divides it, by agreement at a notary or in court.
- **Facts:** pt-is-inheritance-exempt, pt-is-inheritance-filing, pt-poa-apostille (signing the partilha from abroad), pt-nif-nonresident (every heir needs a NIF).
- **Newsletter angle:** LU, FR, CH: the parents' house in Portugal; BR, US, IL: heirs with a Portuguese parent.
- **Posts:** carousel (the steps after a death, in order), myth-vs-law, one-number, reader-question.

## Week 10 · Mon 11 Jan · Selling from abroad: capital gains

- **Target:** PT "mais valias venda casa" · EN "capital gains tax portugal property" · FR "plus-value vente portugal".
- **Page:** `selling-from-abroad`; the capital-gains calculator; path, sell · 01. Partners: tax adviser, agency (pending agreement).

## Week 11 · Mon 18 Jan · Moving the money: transfers and currency

- **Target:** PT "transferir dinheiro para portugal" · EN "send money to portugal" · FR "virement vers le portugal".
- **Page:** `moving-the-money`; path, buy · 04 finance. Partner: money transfer (fx).
- **Answer first:** Banks, notaries and lawyers must ask where the money comes from when the operation calls for it (anti-money-laundering law), so the money for a purchase has to be traceable. The euro is the reference; CHF, USD, BRL and ILS at the ECB rate of the day.
- **Facts:** pt-aml-funds, pt-aml-realestate, ecb-eur-chf, ecb-eur-usd, ecb-eur-brl, ecb-eur-ils, br-cbe, us-fbar, us-8938-abroad, fr-3916.
- **Posts:** one-number (an exchange-rate move on 300 000 €, illustrative), myth-vs-law (to add: the rule on cash payments at the deed), reader-question, edition-specific cards (CBE, FBAR, 3916).

## Week 12 · Mon 25 Jan · Residence after the golden visa

- **Target:** PT "autorização de residência portugal" · EN "portugal golden visa requirements" · FR "visa portugal".
- **Page:** `residence-portugal`; path, buy · 01 where. Partner: lawyer.
- **Answer first:** Buying property no longer qualifies for the residence permit for investment: real estate was removed in 2023. The routes now are the visas for people with their own income, work or study, and CPLP residence for Brazilians.
- **Facts:** pt-ari-realestate-ended, pt-visa-own-income, pt-cplp-residence, pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions; to add: AIMA's current requirements and fees, checked on the AIMA site.
- **Posts:** myth-vs-law (golden visa), one-number, reader-question, LinkedIn data post for advisers.
