## The story

Gondomar sits across the Douro, right next to Porto. In one year, the median price per square metre sold went from €1,902 to €2,348, up 23%. In Porto it rose 19%, to €3,631. The gap is still wide: the same money buys about 55% more home in Gondomar than in Porto. These are INE medians, not specific homes.

## Here vs there
