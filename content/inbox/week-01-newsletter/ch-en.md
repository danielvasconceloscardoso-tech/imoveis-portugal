## This week's home

For the first issue we run the numbers on a €245,000 home bought by someone who is not a Portuguese tax resident. Transfer tax (IMT): €18,375 (flat 7.5%). Stamp duty: €1,960. Deed and registration at Casa Pronta: €375. Total: €265,710, or about CHF 245,171 at the ECB reference rate of 23 October. From next week, this space shows a real home from our partner agency, with its permission, and the same breakdown.

## The story

With €500,000, at the median price of homes sold, you get more than 100 m² in 303 of the 304 municipalities with an official INE figure, and more than 300 m² in 209. The one exception is Lisbon, at about 96 m². Nationally, the median square metre sold costs €2,264, about 18% more than a year ago. These are medians, not specific homes.
