## Carousel 1: "400,000 €: purchase taxes"

1. A 400,000 € home in Portugal. Purchase taxes?
2. Transfer tax (IMT), non-resident: 30,000 €
3. Stamp duty: 3,200 €
4. Total: 33,200 €
5. Not included: deed, registration, legal fees, exchange

## Carousel 2: "The exceptions to 7.5%"

1. Non-resident? IMT is a flat 7.5%. Unless…
2. … you become a Portuguese tax resident within 2 years
3. … you rent it out as a home at a capped rent, 36 months in 5 years
4. Then you claim back the difference within 6 months
5. Source: Decree-Law 97/2026

## Carousel 3: "After you buy"

1. You bought. What now?
2. Within 15 days: tax representative or electronic notifications
3. Every year: IMI, 0.3% to 0.45% of tax value
4. In the US: FBAR above $10,000 in foreign accounts
5. A partner tax adviser can help. Link in bio.
