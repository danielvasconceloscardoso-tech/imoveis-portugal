welcome to Imóveis Portugal. We write about buying a home in Portugal from Israel, step by step, with the source under every number. The first issue arrives on Thursday, December 3, and a person reads every reply.

Until then, three short emails. This one starts where every purchase starts.

If a trip to Lisbon just to open a file isn't in your plans, it doesn't need to be. Before any offer or deed, you need a NIF, the Portuguese tax number: the deed can't be signed without it. A legal representative, such as a lawyer, can request it online for you, and you don't need a tax representative to get it.

The tax representative comes after the purchase. Once you own the home, you have 15 days to appoint one or join the tax authority's electronic notices, and because Israel is outside the EU and the EEA, one of the two is mandatory.

Source: Portugal's Tax and Customs Authority (its NIF leaflet for non-resident foreigners) and the General Tax Law, article 19. Last checked October 24, 2026.

If you rent the home out later, the Portugal–Israel treaty lets Portugal tax that income, and Israel gives a credit for the Portuguese tax.
