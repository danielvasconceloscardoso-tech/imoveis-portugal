welcome to Imóveis Portugal. We write about buying a home in Portugal from France, step by step, with the source under every number. The first issue arrives on Thursday, December 3, and a person reads every reply.

Until then, three short emails. This one starts where every purchase starts.

If you can't take a week off in France just to stand in line at a tax office in Portugal, you don't have to. Before any offer or deed, you need a NIF, the Portuguese tax number: the deed can't be signed without it. A legal representative, such as a lawyer, can request it online for you, and you don't need a tax representative to get it.

The tax representative only comes up after the purchase. France is in the EU, so appointing one is optional for you. Owners who live outside the EU have 15 days after the purchase to appoint one or join the tax authority's electronic notices.

Source: Portugal's Tax and Customs Authority (its NIF leaflet for non-resident foreigners) and the General Tax Law, article 19. Last checked October 24, 2026.

If you open a bank account in Portugal for the purchase, as a French tax resident you declare it every year on form 3916, even if it sits unused.
