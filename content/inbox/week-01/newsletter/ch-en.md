Subject: The NIF from Switzerland, requested online

{{first name | Hi}},

welcome to Imóveis Portugal. We write about buying a home in Portugal from Switzerland, step by step, with the source under every number. The first issue arrives on Thursday, December 3, and a person reads every reply.

Until then, three short emails. This one starts where every purchase starts.

If you'd rather not spend a week of vacation at a tax office in Portugal, you don't have to. Before any offer or deed, you need a NIF, the Portuguese tax number: the deed can't be signed without it. A legal representative, such as a lawyer, can request it online for you, and you don't need a tax representative to get it.

The tax representative comes after the purchase. Once you own the home, you have 15 days to appoint one or join the tax authority's electronic notices. Switzerland is in neither the EU nor the EEA, so for you one of the two is mandatory.

Source: Portugal's Tax and Customs Authority (its NIF leaflet for non-resident foreigners) and the General Tax Law, article 19. Last checked October 24, 2026.

Later on, if you rent the home out or sell it, the Portugal–Switzerland treaty lets Portugal tax the rent and the gain; Switzerland exempts them but may apply the rate it would have used.
