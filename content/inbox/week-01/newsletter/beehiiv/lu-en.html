If you can't take days off in Luxembourg just to stand in line at a tax office in Portugal, you don't have to. Before any offer or deed, you need a NIF, the Portuguese tax number: the deed can't be signed without it. A legal representative, such as a lawyer, can request it online for you, and you don't need a tax representative to get it.

The tax representative only comes up after the purchase. Luxembourg is in the EU, so appointing one is optional for you. Owners who live outside the EU have 15 days after the purchase to appoint one or join the tax authority's electronic notices.

Source: Portugal's Tax and Customs Authority (its NIF leaflet for non-resident foreigners) and the General Tax Law, article 19. Last checked October 24, 2026.

If you rent the home out one day, the Portugal–Luxembourg treaty lets Portugal tax the rent; Luxembourg exempts it but may count it when it sets the rate on the rest of your income.
