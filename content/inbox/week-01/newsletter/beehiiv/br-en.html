If a flight from Brazil just to start the paperwork isn't in your plans, you don't need one. Before any offer or deed, you need a NIF, the Portuguese tax number: the deed can't be signed without it. A legal representative, such as a lawyer, can request it online for you, and you don't need a tax representative to get it.

The tax representative comes after the purchase. Once you own the home, you have 15 days to appoint one or join the tax authority's electronic notices, and because Brazil is outside the EU and the EEA, one of the two is mandatory.

Source: Portugal's Tax and Customs Authority (its NIF leaflet for non-resident foreigners) and the General Tax Law, article 19. Last checked October 24, 2026.

Back in Brazil, the home goes into your IRPF under Bens e Direitos, at the purchase price converted to dollars and then to reais at the Banco Central selling rate on the purchase date.
