## Video 1: 26,250 €

- **Hook:** "A 350,000 € apartment in Portugal. How much transfer tax if you live in Israel?"
- **Body:** "Non-residents pay a flat 7.5% since May 2026. That's 26,250 €."
- **Close:** "Source: Portugal's IMT code, article 17."

## Video 2: the exceptions

- **Hook:** "Two ways to get part of that tax back."
- **Body:** "Become a Portuguese tax resident within two years, or rent it out as a home at a capped rent for 36 months in five years."
- **Close:** "Claim within six months. Source: Decree-Law 97/2026."

## Video 3: stamp duty

- **Hook:** "The other tax every buyer pays."
- **Body:** "Stamp duty: 0.8% of the price. 2,800 € on 350,000 €."
- **Close:** "Source: Portugal's stamp duty table."

## Video 4: 15 days

- **Hook:** "Bought in Portugal from Israel? The clock starts."
- **Body:** "You have 15 days to appoint a tax representative or sign up for electronic notifications."
- **Close:** "Source: Portuguese Tax Authority."

## Video 5: renting it out

- **Hook:** "Who taxes your Portuguese rent: Portugal or Israel?"
- **Body:** "Portugal taxes non-residents' rent at 25%. Under the treaty, Israel gives a credit for the Portuguese tax."
- **Close:** "Source: Portugal–Israel tax treaty."
