## Post 1

Buying in Portugal from Israel? Since May 2026, non-residents pay a flat 7.5% property transfer tax. On 350,000 €, that's 26,250 €. Source: Portugal's IMT code, article 17.

## Post 2

Planning to relocate? Become a Portuguese tax resident within two years of buying and you can claim back the difference on the transfer tax. Source: Decree-Law 97/2026.

## Post 3

Stamp duty on a home in Portugal: 0.8% of the price. Source: Portugal's stamp duty table, item 1.1.

## Post 4

Annual property tax in Portugal (IMI): 0.3% to 0.45% of the tax value, set by each municipality. Question for the comments: Porto or Lisbon?

## Post 5

The Portugal–Israel tax treaty: rental income from a Portuguese home may be taxed in Portugal, and Israel allows a credit for that tax. Source: treaty, articles 6 and 23.
