# Buying a home in Portugal from Israel: the real total cost

A 350,000 € apartment in Porto or Lisbon costs a buyer living in Israel 29,050 € in purchase taxes. Since May 2026, Portugal charges buyers who are not Portuguese tax residents a flat property transfer tax, and it is the largest item on the bill.

## Property transfer tax (IMT): 26,250 €

IMT is the tax paid on purchase. Since May 2026, non-residents buying a home pay a flat 7.5%, with no exemption or reduction. On 350,000 €, that's 26,250 €. A Portuguese tax resident buying the same apartment as a main home would pay about 14,237 € under the 2026 brackets.

There are exceptions. The flat rate doesn't apply if you become a Portuguese tax resident within two years of the purchase, or if you rent the apartment out as a home, at a capped rent, for at least 36 months in the first five years. You pay 7.5% first and claim back the difference within six months.

## Stamp duty: 2,800 €

Every buyer pays 0.8% stamp duty on the price: 2,800 € on 350,000 €. A Portuguese mortgage adds 0.6% of the loan amount and 4% of the interest.

## Tax number and representative

You need a Portuguese tax number (NIF) before signing. Once you own property in Portugal, as a resident outside the EU, you have 15 days to appoint a tax representative in Portugal or sign up for the tax authority's electronic notifications.

## If you rent it out

Rent from a home in Portugal earned by a non-resident is taxed in Portugal at 25%, with reductions for long leases. Under the Portugal–Israel tax treaty, Portugal may tax that income and Israel, as your country of residence, allows a credit for the tax paid in Portugal.

## Every year: IMI

The municipality charges IMI every year, between 0.3% and 0.45% of the property's tax value.

| --- | --- | --- |
| IMT | 26,250 € | 14,237 € |
