You can get a Portuguese tax number (NIF) without leaving home: a legal representative requests it online for you, and you don't need a tax representative to get it.[[pt-nif-nonresident]] The deadline comes later. Once you own a home in Portugal, you have 15 days to appoint a tax representative or join the tax authority's electronic notices, and if you live outside the EU and the EEA, one of the two is mandatory.[[pt-tax-rep]] Imóveis Portugal last checked both rules on October 24, 2026, against the Tax and Customs Authority's leaflet for non-resident foreigners and article 19 of the General Tax Law.

## The NIF is the first document of any purchase

"I haven't even picked a town yet. Why do I need a tax number?" Because the purchase can't be signed without one. The NIF (número de identificação fiscal, often called número de contribuinte) is the number the Portuguese tax authority gives every taxpayer, resident or not.

Portugal doesn't stop foreigners from buying. The transfer tax code even sets its own rate for people who aren't Portuguese tax residents, which only makes sense because they buy.[[pt-nonresidents-buy]] What every deed does need is identification. At Casa Pronta, the government desk that handles the deed and the registration in one place, the list of documents starts with the civil and tax identification of the seller and of you.[[pt-casa-pronta-docs-all]] Your passport covers the first. The NIF covers the second.

That's why it sits at step 2 of [Step by step](/en/us/step-by-step/?door=buy&step=2), right after choosing where to look.

## You can request it online, through a legal representative

"I can't take a week off to stand in line at a tax office in Lisbon." You don't have to. The Tax and Customs Authority lists two routes for a foreign non-resident.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]

1. **Online, through a legal representative.** Someone you authorize, such as a lawyer, files the request on the e-balcão, the online service desk of the Portal das Finanças. You send your documents from home; the representative tells you which ones your case needs.
2. **In person**, at a tax office (Serviço de Finanças), if you're already in Portugal.

Getting the number doesn't require a tax representative.[[pt-nif-nonresident]] These are the routes the tax authority describes for foreign non-residents. If you lived in Portugal before, or hold Portuguese nationality, you may already have a NIF; a partner lawyer can check your details with the tax office before the purchase.

The two roles are easy to mix up: the legal representative only files the NIF request, while the tax representative is a separate duty that starts once you own property.

From there, the purchase follows its usual order:

1. With the NIF in hand, you can make offers, sign the promissory contract and plan the deed.
2. If you can't fly in to sign, a power of attorney does it. It's signed at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with the Hague Apostille.[[pt-poa-apostille]]
3. At the deed, the NIF goes on the documents.[[pt-casa-pronta-docs-all]]
4. Within 15 days of owning the home, you appoint a tax representative or join electronic notices.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]

## The tax representative comes after the purchase

"Do I need someone in Portugal to deal with the tax office for me?" It depends on where you live. The rule is in article 19 of the General Tax Law.[[pt-tax-rep]]

- **Outside the EU and the EEA**, appointing a tax representative in Portugal is mandatory.
- **Inside the EU or the EEA**, it's optional.
- **Electronic notices waive it.** If you join the tax authority's electronic notification service on the Portal das Finanças, you don't need a representative.

The clock starts when you come to own property in Portugal: 15 days.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]

## Where you live changes one rule

The NIF is requested the same way from every country. What changes is the tax representative rule, and the paperwork back home.

### Luxembourg

Luxembourg is in the EU, so the tax representative is optional.[[pt-tax-rep]] If you later rent the home out, the treaty between Portugal and Luxembourg lets Portugal tax that income; Luxembourg exempts it but may take it into account to set the rate on the rest of your income.[[lu-pt-treaty-property]]

### France

France is in the EU, so the representative is optional here too.[[pt-tax-rep]] If you open a bank account in Portugal for the purchase, French tax residents declare every account held abroad each year on form 3916, even an unused one.[[fr-3916]]

### Switzerland

Switzerland is in neither the EU nor the EEA. Within 15 days of owning the home, you appoint a tax representative or join electronic notices.[[pt-tax-rep]] Under the Portugal–Switzerland treaty, rent from the home and gains on selling it may be taxed in Portugal; Switzerland exempts them but may apply the rate it would have used.[[ch-pt-treaty-property]]

### Brazil

Brazil is outside the EU, so the same 15-day choice applies: a representative or electronic notices.[[pt-tax-rep]] In Brazil, the home goes into the IRPF under Bens e Direitos, with the purchase price in euros converted to dollars and then to reais at the Banco Central selling rate on the purchase date.[[br-bens-exterior]]

### United States

The US is outside the EU: a tax representative or electronic notices, within 15 days.[[pt-tax-rep]] A Portuguese bank account also counts on the US side: US persons file an FBAR when their foreign accounts together exceed $10,000 at any time during the year.[[us-fbar]]

### Israel

Israel is outside the EU, so the same 15-day choice applies.[[pt-tax-rep]] If you rent the home out, the Portugal–Israel treaty lets Portugal tax that income, and Israel gives a credit for the Portuguese tax.[[il-pt-treaty-property]]

## The NIF comes before the tax bill

Once the NIF is sorted, the next number is the transfer tax (IMT). If you're not a Portuguese tax resident, IMT on a home is a flat 7.5%, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies.[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]] The first exception covers anyone who "tenha sido considerado residente para efeitos fiscais", has been considered a Portuguese tax resident; the second, anyone who becomes one within two years of the purchase.[[pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions]] If you lived in Portugal before, how that first exception applies to you is a question for a partner lawyer or tax adviser. ## Questions

**Can I get a NIF without going to Portugal?**

Yes. A legal representative, such as a lawyer, can request it online for you through the Portal das Finanças e-balcão. You send your documents from home and never stand in line at a tax office. The in-person route at a Serviço de Finanças exists too, if you happen to be in Portugal. A partner lawyer can file the request for you.

**Do I need a tax representative to get my NIF?**

No. The tax authority's leaflet for non-resident foreigners says a tax representative isn't needed to get the number. The representative becomes a question only after the purchase: once you own property in Portugal, you have 15 days to appoint one or join the electronic notification service. Inside the EU or the EEA, the representative itself is optional.

**I live in the US. Do I need a tax representative after I buy?**

Yes, or the alternative. The US is outside the EU and the EEA, so within 15 days of owning the home you either appoint a tax representative in Portugal or join the tax authority's electronic notices. The same applies from Brazil, Switzerland and Israel. A partner lawyer can tell you which of the two fits your plans.

**I live in France. Is a tax representative mandatory?**

No. France is in the EU, so appointing a tax representative in Portugal is optional, and the same goes for Luxembourg. The NIF is still the first step of the purchase. On the French side, a bank account you open in Portugal goes on form 3916 every year, even if you don't use it. A partner lawyer can request the NIF for you.

**Can the lawyer who gets my NIF also sign the deed for me?**

Yes, with a power of attorney. You sign it at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live, and in that case it needs the Hague Apostille to be valid in Portugal. That way the purchase moves ahead even if you only fly in to see the home. A partner lawyer can handle the NIF and the deed together.
