You can get your NIF Portugal tax number from the country where you live, online through a legal representative. You don't need a tax representative to get it.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]

The NIF is your Portuguese tax number. It's the first document of any home purchase. Imóveis Portugal checked these rules on October 24, 2026, against the official sources cited.

## What the NIF is for when you buy a home

NIF stands for número de identificação fiscal, the tax identification number. The Portuguese tax office gives one to every taxpayer. People also call it the número de contribuinte.

Residents and non-residents both get one. IMT is the transfer tax you pay when you buy a home. The transfer tax code even sets its own rate for people who aren't Portuguese tax residents, which only makes sense because they buy.[[pt-nonresidents-buy]]

The escritura is the deed, the final purchase contract. You can sign it at Casa Pronta, a government desk that does the deed and the registration in one place. There, the list of documents starts with the civil and tax identification of the seller and of you.[[pt-casa-pronta-docs-all]]

Your passport covers the civil part. The NIF covers the tax part. That's why the NIF is [station 2 of Step by step]({{journey}}?door=buy&step=2), right after choosing the area.

## How to get a NIF from abroad

The Autoridade Tributária is the Portuguese Tax and Customs Authority. It lists two ways to get one when you live abroad.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]

1. **Online, through a legal representative.** This is a person who acts for you, such as a lawyer. They file the request on the e-balcão, the online request service of the Portal das Finanças.
2. **In person**, at a tax office (Serviço de Finanças), if you're in Portugal.

On the online route, you send your documents from home. The representative tells you which ones you need.

If you lived in Portugal before, or hold Portuguese nationality, you may already have a NIF. In that case, the step is to check that your details with the tax office are up to date.

## Legal representative vs tax representative

These are two roles, and the names are easy to mix up. The legal representative only files the NIF request. Their job ends when the number arrives.

The tax representative is someone else. It's a person in Portugal who deals with the tax office for you. You don't need one to get the NIF.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]

The question only comes up once you own a home in Portugal. From that day, you have 15 days to choose one of two options:

- appoint a tax representative;
- join electronic notices, the tax office's messages that reach you on the Portal das Finanças.

The rule is in article 19 of the General Tax Law.[[pt-tax-rep]] Outside the European Union (EU) and the European Economic Area (EEA), one of the two is mandatory. Inside the EU or the EEA, the tax representative is optional.

## What changes with the country you live in

You request the NIF the same way from every country. What changes is the tax representative rule, and the paperwork back home.[[pt-tax-rep]]

| Where you live | After the purchase | Back home |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Luxembourg | Tax representative optional | If you rent the home out, Portugal taxes the rent. Luxembourg exempts it but may count it for your rate.[[lu-pt-treaty-property]] |
| France | Tax representative optional | A Portuguese bank account goes on form 3916 every year.[[fr-3916]] |
| Switzerland | Tax representative or electronic notices, within 15 days | Rent and gains on a sale may be taxed in Portugal. Switzerland exempts them but may count them for your rate.[[ch-pt-treaty-property]] |
| Brazil | Tax representative or electronic notices, within 15 days | The home goes in the IRPF under Bens e Direitos, converted to dollars, then to reais.[[br-bens-exterior]] |
| United States | Tax representative or electronic notices, within 15 days | A Portuguese account counts toward the FBAR, due when foreign accounts top $10,000 in total.[[us-fbar]] |
| Israel | Tax representative or electronic notices, within 15 days | If you rent the home out, Portugal taxes the rent and Israel gives a credit for that tax.[[il-pt-treaty-property]] |

## How long it takes and what it costs

The law sets one deadline, and it comes after the purchase: 15 days for the tax representative or electronic notices.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]

For the NIF request, the official sources we checked give no time frame. They give no price either. Each legal representative sets their own fee, so we don't publish amounts.

A partner lawyer can tell you both for your case, before you decide.

## What comes after the NIF

With the NIF, you can make an offer and sign the CPCV, the promissory contract. It sets the price, the date and the deposit before the deed.

You can sign everything from abroad, with a power of attorney. It's the document that lets another person sign for you. It's signed at a Portuguese consulate, or before a notary where you live with the Hague Apostille.[[pt-poa-apostille]] The apostille is a certificate that makes the notary's signature valid in Portugal.

Next comes the transfer tax. If you're not a Portuguese tax resident, IMT on a home is a flat 7.5%, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies.[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]] The first exception covers anyone who "tenha sido considerado residente para efeitos fiscais", has been considered a Portuguese tax resident; the second, anyone who becomes one within two years of the purchase.[[pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions]] If you lived in Portugal before, how that first exception applies to you is a question for a partner lawyer or tax adviser.
