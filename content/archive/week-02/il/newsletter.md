thinking of a Portuguese mortgage? Three numbers apply at every bank in Portugal:

**80% or 90%.** A loan can reach 80% of the value for a vacation home and 90% for your main home.

**45%.** Since August 1, 2026, monthly payments on all your loans should stay under 45% of net income.

**40 or 35 years.** The longest term, depending on whether you're 35 or younger.
