## Post 1

Buying a home in Portugal while living in the US? Since May 2026, non-residents pay a flat 7.5% property transfer tax. On €400,000, that's €30,000. Source: Portugal's IMT code, article 17.

## Post 2

Planning to move to Portugal? If you become a Portuguese tax resident within two years of buying, you can claim back the difference on the transfer tax. Source: Decree-Law 97/2026.

## Post 3

Stamp duty on a home purchase in Portugal: 0.8% of the price, for every buyer. Source: Portugal's stamp duty table, item 1.1.

## Post 4

Property tax in Portugal (IMI): 0.3% to 0.45% of the tax value each year, set by the municipality. Question for the comments: which region are you looking at?

## Post 5

A Portuguese bank account counts toward your FBAR. Foreign accounts over $10,000 combined at any time in the year: file with FinCEN. Source: IRS.
