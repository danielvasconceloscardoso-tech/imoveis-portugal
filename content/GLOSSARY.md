# Glossary

One term per concept, used the same way on the site, in the FAQ, in the reports and in the newsletters (prompt 09). PT is European Portuguese, EN is American English, FR is written for a reader in Luxembourg or Paris. On first use in EN and FR, keep the Portuguese acronym and explain it, e.g. "transfer tax (IMT)", "compromis (CPCV)".
