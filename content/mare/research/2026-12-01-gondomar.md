## 2. The threads, verified

### The gold (the van Zeller thread: bench, hallmark, shop window)
- **The bench.** Workshops are concentrated in São Cosme, then Valbom, Jovim, Fânzeres, São Pedro da Cova, Rio Tinto and Foz do Sousa (`mare-gdm-filigree-inventory-2023`). Eleven steps, by hand; the wire is pulled through ever narrower holes "to the thickness of a hair", two wires are twisted into one, the skeleton is filled wire by wire (`mare-gdm-filigree-steps`). Five workshops receive visitors on the Rota da Filigrana, which turned ten on 17 Oct 2026 (`mare-gdm-rota-estacao-2026`). CINDOR, in Gondomar, is the country's only training centre for the trade (`mare-gdm-cindor`). Topázio (1874) works silver by hand in its Gondomar factory: about 30,000 pieces a year (`mare-gdm-topazio`).
- **The hallmark.** Every piece goes to the state assay office before sale. The Contrastaria (INCM) has its Gondomar delegation in the Goldpark building; INCM calls it its site with the most capacity and is automating its marking there (`mare-gdm-contrastaria-gondomar`). In 2023 the Contrastaria hallmarked **1,004,838 gold pieces** nationwide (`mare-gdm-contrastaria-2023`). Since March 2024 handmade filigree has its own Contrastaria certification mark (`mare-gdm-filigree-contrastaria-brand`).
- **The shop window.** The Câmara lists the exports: Spain, Hong Kong, Germany, Italy, the USA, France, Switzerland (`mare-gdm-gold-cluster-2023`). A filigree piece is in a window in Times Square (`mare-gdm-times-square`). **No Hong Kong window was found**: Hong Kong is only an export market.
- **The cluster (Câmara, via Jornal de Negócios, 18 Apr 2023):** 42% of national output, about 445 firms (70% sole traders), about €50M turnover, about 800 jobs. The "€60M of exports in 2022" sentence may refer to the national sector, not to Gondomar alone: write "o setor" or ask the Câmara. Tourism material says 60%: not used.
- **Status:** national intangible heritage since 7 Sep 2023; UNESCO candidacy formalised 17 Jun 2025 and, in Nov 2025, still awaiting the Comissão Nacional da UNESCO (`mare-gdm-filigree-unesco`). Say "candidata", never "património da humanidade". Gondomar became Portugal's second "Cidade Europeia Autêntica" (EUIPO) on 22 Jun 2026 (`mare-gdm-cidade-autentica`). Topázio: 1874 holds; "third-largest exporter" is Forbes 2018 citing INE: date it or drop it.

### The river

- Estação Náutica: certified by Fórum Oceano on 26 Feb 2026, **inaugurated on 17 Oct 2026** at the Casa Branca de Gramido (`mare-gdm-estacao-nautica-cert`, `mare-gdm-rota-estacao-2026`). Marinas: Angra do Douro (Lixa) and Melres. Beaches: Lomba and Melres classified in 2022; Zebreiros sands named in 2026; the 2026 bathing list was not checked (`mare-gdm-river-beaches`).

- Futsal: Ricardinho, born in Valbom, six times the world's best player (Futsal Awards 2010, 2014 to 2018); world champion 2021, European champion 2018; Valbom's pavilion carries his name. The Câmara's 2022 text says seven times; the seventh (2021) award was not found: write "seis vezes" (`mare-gdm-ricardinho`).
- Football: André Silva, the striker, was born in Baguim do Monte, back at FC Porto in 2026 (`mare-gdm-andre-silva`). Not the same person as the André Silva born in 2000.
- Volleyball, futsal, handball: 2021/22 national titles and promotions (Ala de Nun'Álvares volleyball back in the 1st division, EDC Gondomar women's futsal, Gondomar Cultural handball); European City of Sport 2017 (`mare-gdm-gondomar-desporto`). More recent seasons not checked.

One factual line, set apart, no photo, nothing near the game, the sponsor or prices.

### Art and people

### "And I don't know what…": the gems
1. **The Arrábida's rehearsal is in Gondomar.** Edgar Cardoso's bridge at Foz do Sousa (1948) was the trial run for his Ponte da Arrábida (Câmara; DN) (`mare-gdm-foz-do-sousa-bridge`). Recommended: one line, with a photo.

## 6. The game: five questions, four options each
