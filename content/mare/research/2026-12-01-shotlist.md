Operator, 4 Nov 2026: "give me a shot list per block". One photo per block, in the order of the story plan (`2026-12-01-gondomar.md`, section 4). **Size rule (rule 17 acceptance list):** every image at least twice its display width and under 200 KB once cut. The email column is 600 px wide, so a full-width image needs **at least 1200 px wide**; the opening image, which also runs full-bleed on the Maré web page, needs **at least 2000 px on the long edge**. Commons files below are listed at their original size; cut them from the original, not from a thumbnail.

**Licence rule:** CC BY-SA needs the author's name and the licence next to the photo (or in the photo credits line at the foot of the issue) and a link to the licence. Photos of people need their consent; no photo of Diogo Jota.
