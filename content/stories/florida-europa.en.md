United's UA281 leaves New York (Newark) at 9:30 p.m. and lands in [Faro](place:faro) at 9:50 the next morning, 7 hours and 20 minutes later. It flies four times a week in summer; in 2026 the season opened on May 15.[[flight-ewr-fao-ua281-times]][[flight-ewr-fao-united]] You sleep over the Atlantic and have the whole day ahead of you.

In Vilamoura, in the municipality of [Loulé](place:loule), the course at The Els Club is Ernie Els's design. From July 31 to August 2, 2026, it hosted the first Portugal Invitational of the PGA Tour Champions: 78 players, a $3 million purse, and the first PGA Tour-sanctioned individual stroke-play event held in Portugal. The agreement behind it runs for five years.[[pga-champions-portugal-invitational-2026]]

Vilamoura's marina is the largest in Portugal, with 825 berths for boats up to 60 meters. In 2024, its 50th year, it launched a new marina with 68 berths for vessels of 20 to 50 meters.[[vilamoura-marina-berths]]

Farther west, in [Lagos](place:lagos), Palmares has 27 holes in three nines, named Alvor, Lagos and Praia, designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr. The course sits above the Alvor estuary and Lagos Bay.[[palmares-golf-27-holes]]

In [Portimão](place:portimao), Formula 1 returns to the Algarve International Circuit for the Portuguese Grand Prix in 2027 and 2028, under a two-year agreement. The 2027 race weekend is June 18 to 20.[[f1-portimao-2027-2028]][[f1-portugal-2027-dates]]

Praia da Falésia, in [Albufeira](place:albufeira), runs east toward Vilamoura below its cliffs. Tripadvisor named it the best beach in the world for 2024, in its Travelers' Choice Best of the Best Beaches, from a year of travelers' reviews.[[falesia-tripadvisor-2024]]

Up the coast, beyond the Algarve, Comporta is a parish of [Alcácer do Sal](place:alcacer-do-sal). Its 1,094 residents live between the ocean and the Sado estuary, from fishing, rice, wine, hotels and restaurants. Carrasqueira, with its stilt pier, is one of its villages.[[comporta-alcacer-do-sal]][[comporta-freguesia-cm]] Farther south, in [Sines](place:sines), Microsoft is investing more than $10 billion in AI infrastructure from early 2026, with Nscale, NVIDIA and Start Campus. It is the company's largest investment in Portugal.[[sines-microsoft-ai-hub]]

Arrow Global, the investor behind Vilamoura's marina and Palmares, calls the Algarve “the Florida of Southern Europe,” for the ocean views and the temperate climate.[[arrow-florida-southern-europe]]
