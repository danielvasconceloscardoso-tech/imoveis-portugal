At Antiqvvm, the meal begins at the counter. The first snacks are made there, in full view, for anyone who wants to watch. Then you move to the dining room, in a building next to the Romantic Museum at Rua de Entre Quintas, 220, with a garden facing the Douro where you can also eat. There are two [tasting menus](fact:michelin-antiqvvm): Orgânico, which is vegetarian, and Ensaios Sensoriais.

Vítor Matos's cooking is built on the produce and does not hide it: carabinero prawn, red mullet, caviar, wagyu, black truffle, in dishes that look simple. One of them is called Gamba violeta & Moqueca.[[michelin-antiqvvm]]

The restaurant holds two Michelin stars, and the [Porto region](fact:michelin-2026-porto-2stars) has two more like it, both just outside the city line: The Yeatman Gastronomic Restaurant, in [Vila Nova de Gaia](place:vila-nova-de-gaia), and the Casa de Chá da Boa Nova, in Leça da Palmeira, [Matosinhos](place:matosinhos). At Boa Nova, sommelier Carlos Monteiro received this year's Michelin Sommelier Award. Inside [Porto](place:porto) itself, Antiqvvm stands alone on that rung.

The 2026 guide was presented on March 10, in Funchal, and gave three stars to no Portuguese restaurant. Nine hold two, and the only newcomer at that level was Fifty Seconds, in [Lisbon](place:lisboa). Forty-four hold one star, ten of them new.[[michelin-2026-portugal]]

Four of those new stars landed in Porto, all in the same year, and each place serves in its own way.[[michelin-2026-porto-new]] In the center, dop belongs to Rui Paula, the same chef as Boa Nova, with resident chef Sandro Teixeira and a menu called Não há futuro sem memória (no future without memory), in 6, 10 or 14 courses. Éon occupies the Palacete Severo, a boutique hotel full of art, where Tiago Bonito serves the Lés a Lés menu in 9 or 14 dishes. Gastro by Elemento, near the Estádio do Dragão, is Ricardo Dias Ferreira's and cooks over fire and in a wood oven, on a 15-course menu. In Diferente belongs to Brazilian chef Angélica Salvador, who works with fish from the auctions of Matosinhos and Aveiro.

In the dining room at Vila Foz, also in Porto, service is run by Adácio Ribeiro, winner of the 2026 Michelin Service Award.[[michelin-2026-portugal]]
