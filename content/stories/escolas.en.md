At Rua da Cerca, 338, in the Foz district of [Porto](place:porto), the [Oporto British School](fact:school-obs) is 300 meters from the beach. The school's website opens with five numbers side by side: 1894, the year it was founded; 577 students; 30 nationalities; an average of 16 per class; and the 300 meters to the sand. It takes children from the age of 3 and keeps them until they are 18, and it describes itself as a British international school.

At Rua Guerra Junqueiro, 162, the [Deutsche Schule Porto](fact:school-dsporto) counts 125 years. It is bilingual from kindergarten to the international Abitur, which is taken in German, and children do not need German to start kindergarten. Pupils work in four languages, German, Portuguese, English and French, and Portuguese universities accept the Abitur for admission.

[CLIP, Oporto International School](fact:school-clip), runs from preschool to Form 12, with 1,226 students from 56 nationalities. It presents itself as a Cambridge School, accredited by the Council of International Schools.

Lisbon's American school started in an apartment. In 1956, Anthony McKenna opened a school with nine pupils in Pedrouços, [Lisbon](place:lisboa), to bring American education to Portugal. Today it is the [Carlucci American International School of Lisbon](fact:school-caisl), at Rua António dos Reis, 95, on the Linhó campus in [Sintra](place:sintra), with the IB among the accreditations it lists.

[St. Julian's School](fact:school-stjulians) is older. It has been at the Quinta Nova in Carcavelos since 1932, between Carcavelos beach and Lisbon, in the municipality of [Cascais](place:cascais). It offers the International Baccalaureate Diploma and the IB Career-related Programme, and runs a bilingual curriculum for ages 6 to 14.

At Avenida Duarte Pacheco, 32, in Lisbon, the [Lycée français Charles Lepierre](fact:school-lfcl) belongs to the AEFE network, the agency for French schools abroad: 600 schools in 138 countries, all teaching the French national curriculum, the same one students would follow at a school in France.

On the map, the Porto addresses all sit on the western side of the city, near the sea. The Lisbon ones split between Carcavelos, Linhó and the city center. Visits are booked directly with each school, through the contacts it publishes on its own website.
