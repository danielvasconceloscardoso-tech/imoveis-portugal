On the old customs quay of [Vila do Conde](place:vila-do-conde) sits the replica of a 16th-century nau, open as a [branch of the town museum](fact:vdc-museums) since 2007. In the building next to it, the old Royal Customs House, the shipbuilding museum has been open since 2001. In the 16th century, this is where ships were built for the Age of Discovery, with the port and the customs house at the center of the town's life.[[vdc-history]]

The town is far older than the ship. It first appears in writing in 953, as Villa de Comite, in a deed for the sale of property. In 1502, King Manuel passed through on his way to Santiago de Compostela, and the visit gave the push for the Mother Church, the Praça Nova and the town hall.[[vdc-history]]

Above the Ave River stands the Monastery of Santa Clara, founded in 1318 by Afonso Sanches and Teresa Martins on the site of the hill fort of São João Baptista, and restored and enlarged in the 18th century.[[vdc-history]] To bring water to its fountain, an aqueduct was built between 1705 and 1714 from a spring at Terroso, in [Póvoa de Varzim](place:povoa-de-varzim): 999 arches over about 5 kilometers, the second-longest in the country. The aqueduct and the church of Santa Clara, where the Founders' Chapel holds Manueline tombs, have been [national monuments](fact:vdc-aqueduct) since 1910.

Bobbin lace has had its own museum since 1991, and the house of the poet José Régio has been a museum since 1975.[[vdc-museums]] In the center, the Borges & Irmão bank, built between 1982 and 1986, is one of the works cited in Álvaro Siza's Pritzker.[[pritzker-siza-1992]] The municipality has 18 kilometers of beaches and a protected area, the Vila do Conde Coast and Mindelo Bird Reserve.[[vdc-history]][[vdc-museums]]

From Porto, you get there by metro. The Bx express leaves Trindade and reaches Vila do Conde in 41 minutes on weekdays; the regular B, stopping everywhere, takes 48. Those times come from the [September 2026 timetable](fact:metro-b-vila-do-conde), which allows them two minutes either way. The line runs north to Póvoa de Varzim and, heading south, passes through Trindade on its way to the Estádio do Dragão.
