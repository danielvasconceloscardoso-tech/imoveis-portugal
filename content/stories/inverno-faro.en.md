On the night of February 5, 2012, the thermometer at IPMA station 554 in [Faro](place:faro), five meters above the sea, dropped to minus 0.3 °C (31.5 °F). It was the coldest night, and the lowest reading, in thirty years of [climate normals](fact:ipma-faro-normals), from 1991 to 2020. On the line for frost days, meaning the days when the low falls below freezing, Faro's yearly average is 0.0.

At Luxembourg's Findel airport, the same line adds up to [64.4 days a year](fact:meteolux-findel-normals), 16.9 of them in January alone. For anyone flying out of Luxembourg that month, the difference sits in a single row of the table: the average January high is 3.8 °C (39 °F) at Findel and 16.3 °C (61 °F) in Faro, where average lows stay at 8.3 °C.[[ipma-faro-normals]][[meteolux-findel-normals]]

Faro's winter lasts three months. December has an average high of 17.1 °C, January 16.3 and February 17.0, and by March the average is already 18.9.[[ipma-faro-normals]] Across the year, Faro's average high is 22.3 °C. The warmest January of the three decades had a day of 21.9 °C, on January 28, 2003.[[ipma-faro-normals]]

Rain fits in a few short rows of the same table too. Faro gets 455 millimeters a year, a little over half of Findel's 831, and January averages six days with more than a millimeter.[[ipma-faro-normals]][[meteolux-findel-normals]] In summer the math runs the other way. July has an average high of 29.4 °C in Faro, August 29.2, and the station logs 115 days a year with highs of 25 °C or more. Findel logs 38.[[ipma-faro-normals]][[meteolux-findel-normals]]

[Luxair flight LG785](fact:flight-lux-fao) takes off from Luxembourg at 4:00 p.m. and lands in Faro at 5:55 p.m., local time, after 2 h 55 in the air; with the time difference, you gain an hour on arrival. To Porto, the same airline makes the trip in [2 h 30](fact:flight-lux-opo).

The municipality of Faro holds both the city and its airport. To the west lies [Loulé](place:loule); to the east, [Olhão](place:olhao) and [Tavira](place:tavira). Both stations' data sheets, with their coordinates and the month-by-month values, are linked in the sources, for anyone who wants to set their own city's figures beside them.
