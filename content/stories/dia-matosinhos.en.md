Over the Cidade Salvador roundabout, by the beach in [Matosinhos](place:matosinhos), Janet Echelman's net throws shadows on the ground that shift with the wind. It has hung there since 2005, it is called She Changes, and it weighs one ton. It hangs from a 20-ton steel ring that tilts toward the ocean, 13.5 meters off the ground at its lowest point and 27 at its highest, held up by three masts between 25 and 50 meters tall, white with red stripes, painted like the smokestacks and lighthouses nearby. The net joins 36 mesh sections of different densities, spliced by hand. The architect of the ensemble was Eduardo Souto de Moura.[[echelman-she-changes]]

People who live in Matosinhos see different things in it: fishing nets, masts, the old factory smokestacks, Portuguese lace. The town has made it its [graphic symbol](fact:echelman-she-changes).

The smokestacks belonged to canneries. The port of Leixões, built in 1884, changed the local economy, and by the early 20th century Matosinhos had 59 canneries, known for the quality of their fish and the design of their tins. The industry still employs hundreds of people, and [two thirds of them are women](fact:matosinhos-gastronomy-history). The fish auction keeps the town supplied with fish and shellfish, and the municipal markets of Matosinhos and Angeiras hold more than 80 stalls and 13 restaurants.[[matosinhos-gastronomy-history]]

Those are the people who signed the UNESCO bid: more than 400 restaurants, fishermen, fishwives, cannery workers, chefs with and without Michelin stars, scientists, farmers and designers. On October 31, 2025, World Cities Day, UNESCO's Director-General, Audrey Azoulay, announced the result. Matosinhos joined the [Creative Cities Network](fact:matosinhos-unesco-gastronomy), which counts 408 cities in more than a hundred countries. Only 68 are there for gastronomy.

Across the harbor, in [Leça da Palmeira](place:matosinhos), the Casa de Chá da Boa Nova, which Álvaro Siza designed in 1963, is now chef Rui Paula's restaurant.[[pritzker-siza-1992]] It holds two Michelin stars, one of three restaurants in the [Porto](place:porto) region to do so.[[michelin-2026-porto-2stars]] Its sommelier, Carlos Monteiro, received the 2026 Michelin Sommelier Award.[[michelin-2026-portugal]]

In February, on Thursdays, [Degustar Matosinhos](fact:matosinhos-degustar-2026) puts a shellfish platter for two at a fixed price on the tables of the participating restaurants.
