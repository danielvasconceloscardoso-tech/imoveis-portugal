Down in Ribeira, by the river, on the night of June 23, 2026, a concert with the Luís I bridge as its backdrop ended at 11:59 p.m. A minute later, the sky between that bridge and the Arrábida bridge filled with fireworks for 12 minutes. The show cost [215,000 euros](fact:porto-sao-joao-2026), split evenly between the city halls of [Porto](place:porto) and [Vila Nova de Gaia](place:vila-nova-de-gaia), and Porto's mayor, Pedro Duarte, called it the most beautiful festival in the world.

The night starts long before the fireworks. At 8 p.m., the music kicks off on the big stages in the center: Avenida dos Aliados, Largo do Amor de Perdição in Cordoaria, and the Casa da Música, where in 2026 the Youth Stage marked Porto's year as National Youth Capital. At 10 p.m., the neighborhood stages light up, from Fontainhas to Foz. In 2026, Nunca Mates o Mandarim played the Casa da Música on the stroke of midnight, D.A.M.A took the Cordoaria stage at 12:20 a.m., and Tony Carreira performed at Aliados after midnight. At Aliados and Cordoaria, DJs kept the music going until 4 a.m.[[porto-sao-joao-2026]]

The paper sky lanterns, a fixture of the night, run on a schedule of their own. In 2025, they could only go up between 9:45 p.m. on the 23rd and 1:01 a.m. on the 24th. The Aliados and São Bento metro stations closed at 10 p.m., and the metro and the STCP buses ran [extra service](fact:porto-sao-joao-2025) all night.

São João actually starts several weeks earlier. At the Bolhão Market, the Cascata Comunitária gathers the symbols of Porto in ceramics from June 8 to 28, and in Fontainhas the carousels and fartura stands take over the square from May 29 to June 29.[[porto-sao-joao-2026]]

On the 24th at 6 p.m., the Banda Sinfónica Portuguesa plays the São João Concert at the Palácio de Cristal, with free admission. On the 27th, the Rusgas bring the neighborhood groups to Aliados, and the festival ends where the big night began.[[porto-sao-joao-2026]]

For visitors from abroad, by plane or by train, the calendar does the math on its own: arrive on the 23rd, sleep on the 24th.
