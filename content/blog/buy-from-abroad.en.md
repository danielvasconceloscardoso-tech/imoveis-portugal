You don't need to live in Portugal, or be here on the day of the deed, to buy a home. You do need to follow an order, and almost every step has an official rule behind it. This article goes through them, from the first document to the keys.

## 1. The NIF comes first

The NIF is the Portuguese tax number, and there is no purchase without it. If you live abroad you request it online, through a legal representative, or in person. You don't need a tax representative to get the NIF.[[pt-nif-nonresident]]

The tax representative duty comes after the purchase. If you live outside the EU and the EEA, you must appoint a tax representative in Portugal. For residents of the EU or EEA it's optional, and signing up for the tax authority's electronic notices waives it. The deadline is 15 days after you come to own property in Portugal.[[pt-tax-rep]]

## 2. The budget has more lines than the price

Besides the price, there's the transfer tax (IMT), stamp duty and the deed. Stamp duty on the purchase is 0.8% of the price.[[pt-is-purchase]] With a loan of five years or more, add 0.6% on the amount borrowed,[[pt-is-loan-5y]] and the bank also charges 4% stamp duty on the interest, over the life of the loan.[[pt-is-interest]]

IMT depends on the price, on how the home is used and on whether the buyer is a tax resident. For a non-resident it's 7.5% since May 2026, with exceptions. It's explained in the [article on IMT for non-residents]({{post:imt-nonresidents}}).

## 3. The mortgage has limits and terms

A Portuguese bank can lend to someone who lives abroad, and each bank decides case by case. Banco de Portugal's rules apply to everyone. A mortgage can go up to 90% of the value for a main home and up to 80% for other homes, and the lower of the price and the appraisal counts.[[pt-ltv-limits]] The payments on all your loans together shouldn't exceed 45% of net income,[[pt-dsti-45]] and the maximum term is 40 years up to age 35, and 35 years from that age.[[pt-maturity-limits]]

When the bank approves, it hands over the European Standardised Information Sheet (FINE) with the terms and the draft contract. The offer binds the bank for at least 30 days, and you can only accept it after the first seven, the reflection period.[[pt-mortgage-fine-30-7]] The bank may require insurance linked to the loan, but must accept another provider's policy if the cover is equivalent.[[pt-mortgage-insurance]]

On a 300,000 € purchase by a non-resident with an 80% loan, the own funds needed come to 86,340 €: the down payment, the 7.5% IMT[[pt-imt-nonresident-flat]] and the stamp duty on the purchase and on the loan. The full calculation is in the [free guide]({{guide}}).

## 4. The promissory contract sets price, date and deposit

The promissory purchase contract (CPCV) is signed before the deed and sets the price, the date and the deposit. If the buyer backs out through their own fault, they lose the deposit. If the seller does, the buyer can claim double what they paid (Civil Code, article 442).[[pt-sinal-442]] It pays to have a lawyer read the contract before you sign.

## 5. A power of attorney lets you sign without being here

If you can't come to Portugal, you can give a power of attorney, for example to a lawyer, who signs for you. It can be done at a Portuguese consulate or before a notary where you live. In the second case it needs a Hague Apostille to be valid in Portugal.[[pt-poa-apostille]]

## 6. The bank and the notary ask where the money comes from

Banks, notaries and lawyers must ask for information on the origin of funds when the risk profile or the transaction calls for it (Law 83/2017).[[pt-aml-funds]] Having proof at hand avoids delays.

## 7. Taxes are paid before the deed

IMT is due on the day it's assessed or within the following 30 days.[[pt-imt-payment]] Your lawyer, or the office where the deed is done, confirms the deadline for each case.

## 8. The deed and the registration happen in one place

At a Casa Pronta office, the deed and the registration are done in the same place. It costs 375 € with one registration act and 700 € with a mortgage, plus 50 € per extra property. Lawyer fees are separate.[[pt-casa-pronta-fees]]

Bring the civil and tax identification of sellers and buyers, the building's technical file if one exists, the deed's reference to the usage licence (or its waiver, depending on the construction date) and the energy certificate.[[pt-casa-pronta-docs-all]]

## After the purchase

The owner pays IMI every year, 0.3% to 0.45% of the tax value, at the rate the municipality sets.[[pt-imi-rates]] The questions that come up most at this stage are in the [FAQ]({{faq}}), and for case-by-case help there's the [talk to a partner]({{talk}}) page.
