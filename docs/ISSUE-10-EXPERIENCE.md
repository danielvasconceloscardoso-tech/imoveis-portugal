# Regressar — programme-led experience brief

## The five questions

1. **Feeling:** a return that felt abstract becomes a place the visitor can picture, with its numbers and everyday needs considered.
2. **Hero:** first the declared take-home salary; then one Portuguese result; finally one named concelho with a real image. Each moment has one main action.
3. **Arrival:** Imóveis Portugal introduces the Programa Regressar as the subject of an independent calculator. Photography carries the atmosphere; a short sentence carries the invitation.
4. **Next question:** “Can I use the relief?”, “What changes in my net?”, “What does that allow here?”, “Which schools and hospitals are nearby?” Each answer appears where the question arises; technical detail stays behind a tap.
5. **One more thing:** the place is not a random prize. It fits every selected supported preference and the stated housing scenario, and leads directly to the real map.

## Implementation prompt

Build an original, programme-led return-to-Portugal journey. Begin with country and declared net salary; never ask for foreign gross. Make the annual Portuguese gross/net/IRS/social-security comparison transparent. Put a source-linked, tri-state eligibility check before enabling the Article 12-A scenario: unanswered conditions cannot grant relief. Keep the standard scenario available. Use imagery and short questions to select evidenced tastes, then reveal only a municipality satisfying all chosen constraints and the housing budget. Explain indicative rent/buy floor area with explicit assumptions and required deposit; never imply that income means mortgage approval. Show source-backed schools and actual hospitals, preserving distance units, dates, incomplete coverage and availability limitations. Prefer warm photography, serif names, spare controls, calm movement and excellent reduced-motion/keyboard behavior. Do not build a tax-form dashboard or a lottery. Keep salary in browser memory, and keep the existing main map and Golf modules untouched.

## Programme identity boundary

The provided composition is explicitly headed “Proposta de colaboração”. It is a collaboration proposal, not evidence that an agreement exists. A public `Imóveis Portugal × Programa Regressar` signature can read as co-branding or endorsement; it must not be presented as an established partnership without confirmation.

Until an official asset and its intended use are available, a typographic reference names the programme accurately and links to its official site. It is not a recreated official logo. The public note is explicit: “Simulador independente sobre o Programa Regressar. Sem parceria ou validação governamental.” A proposal mockup, if shown, retains its proposal status and is not the calculator’s public partner badge.

## Editorial and data rules

Functional language stays short; tax uncertainty and access/availability limitations are the small-print exceptions that prevent mistakes. A named school is not evidence of a place available, an international curriculum or a confirmed commute unless that specific fact exists in the source. A health centre is not relabelled as a hospital. “No verified data” remains an honest empty state.

The three-language copy module supplies programme context, eligibility state, housing purchasing power, nearby services, and six image-question prompts. Photographs must be existing licensed assets chosen for what they actually depict. No generated institutional marks, invented partnerships, copied visual identity or new image production is needed.

Source verification remains a separate gate: current primary legislation/programme pages must be verified before this indicative model is promoted as a validated fiscal service. Editorial approval does not establish fiscal accuracy or programme eligibility.
