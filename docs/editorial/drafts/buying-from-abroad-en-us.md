## How to buy a home in Portugal without getting on a plane

*The country can be 3,000 miles away. The paperwork doesn't have to be.*

A home in Portugal might begin with a photograph: a terrace above the Atlantic, a quiet street in Porto, a house close enough to the beach to make an early swim feel ordinary. The purchase, however, begins somewhere considerably less romantic: with a Portuguese tax number.

That's not a reason to abandon the dream. It's a reason to know the sequence.

**The short answer:** Yes, you can buy from abroad. You'll usually need a Portuguese tax identification number (NIF), a realistic budget, financing if required, a carefully reviewed promissory contract, tax payments and a final deed. A properly prepared power of attorney can allow a representative to sign in Portugal when you can't be there yourself.

### First, the number

A **NIF** is the tax identification number used throughout a Portuguese property purchase. You can obtain one while living overseas, although the appropriate application process and representation requirements depend on your circumstances.

If you live outside the EU or EEA, tax-representation obligations can arise once you hold property in Portugal. The official tax authority guidance sets out when representation or electronic notifications are required. It's worth resolving that detail before you exchange documents—not discovering it when a deadline arrives.

[Portuguese Tax Authority: NIF guidance](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/apoio_contribuinte/Folhetos_informativos/Documents/Atribuicao_de_NIF_a_cidadaos_estrangeiros_nao_residentes.pdf) · [Tax representation rules, LGT Article 19](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/lgt/Pages/lgt19.aspx)

### Budget for the purchase, not just the house

A listing price is only the beginning. Plan for transfer tax (IMT), purchase stamp duty, registration and deed expenses, legal fees and, when financing, loan-related costs.

A house at **€300,000** and an 80% mortgage do not mean you only need €60,000 in cash. The source article's worked 2026 example estimates **€86,340** in own funds once specified taxes are included. That figure depends on the assumptions and rules used; it is not a universal quotation.

For the precise breakdown, use our [purchase calculators](https://www.imoveisportugal.pt/en/us/calculators/) and check the current [IMT rules](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cimt/Pages/cimt17.aspx) and [stamp duty schedule](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/selo/Pages/ccod-selo-tabgiselo.aspx).

### A mortgage can be arranged from abroad

Portuguese lenders consider applications from non-residents. Whether a bank will lend, and on what terms, depends on the borrower, the purpose of the property and the bank's assessment.

Banco de Portugal's lending framework includes loan-to-value and debt-service limits; these should not be mistaken for a bank's promise of approval. Ask for the **FINE** (European Standardised Information Sheet), compare actual offers and examine the total cost of credit—not only the headline rate.

[Bank of Portugal borrowing framework](https://www.esrb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/other/Esrb.notification260702_Othermacroprudentialmeasures_PT~155a9efa59.en.pdf) · [Mortgage credit decree-law](https://www.bportugal.pt/sites/default/files/anexos/legislacoes/267709260_1.doc.pdf)

### The contract before the keys

The **CPCV**, or promissory purchase agreement, normally sets the agreed price, deposit and completion date. It is a consequential document: Portuguese Civil Code Article 442 governs important deposit consequences if a party defaults.

This is the stage for a lawyer to ask the questions that an attractive photo cannot answer: title, licences, charges, deadlines, financing conditions and whether the contract actually protects you.

[Civil Code, Article 442](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/1966-34509075-59065106)

### You don't always need to fly over to sign

A **procuração**—a power of attorney—can authorize a trusted person, often your lawyer, to represent you at the deed. Portuguese consulates can help with the appropriate formalities, while documents executed abroad may need authentication or an apostille depending on where and how they are issued.

[Portuguese Consulate in Newark: powers of attorney](https://newark.consuladoportugal.mne.gov.pt/pt/assuntos-consulares/servicos-consulares/procuracao)

### Then comes the deed

Before completion, have the source of funds documentation ready, settle the relevant taxes and confirm the documents required for the property. In a **Casa Pronta** office, deed and registration services can be combined. The source article records standard charges of €375 for a single registration act or €700 in a mortgage case, excluding separate professional fees; verify applicable prices for the particular transaction.

[Casa Pronta official service](https://justica.gov.pt/Servicos/Balcao-Casa-Pronta) · [Money-laundering prevention law](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/Lei_83_2017.pdf)

### The ownership question comes next

The sale closes. Your obligations don't. Annual municipal property tax (**IMI**), insurance, maintenance and—where relevant—management costs are part of owning a home. The municipal IMI rate and taxable value matter more than a rough national average.

[IMI Code, Article 112](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cimi/Pages/cimi112.aspx)

Buying from afar shouldn't mean buying blindly.
