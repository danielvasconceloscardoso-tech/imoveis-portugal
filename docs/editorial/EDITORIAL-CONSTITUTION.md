# Imóveis Portugal — Editorial constitution (draft, 5 November 2026)

## Reader
Financially comfortable, curious families with purchasing power but without the insider connections to discover exceptional Portugal. Premium taste, never snobbery. Both diaspora and international readers. Real estate is one outcome of sustained trust; not every story should sell homes.

## Voice
**A well-connected friend with excellent taste and impeccable facts.** Sharp and direct like a useful startup explanation, intimate and assured like a great concierge, curious and observant like a great magazine. Original writing, never stylistic impersonation.

### Positive rules
- Open with a **specific surprise, scene, person, contradiction or question**, not "Portugal has long been known for…"
- Answer practical questions in the first screen, then tell the compelling story.
- Show the concrete detail that proves expertise: an artist's medium, a restaurant's dish, a course's architect, a wave's conditions, a verifiable government source, a journey time from a named airport.
- Taste is demonstrated through selection and reporting, not the adjectives "luxurious", "premium", "exclusive" or "iconic".
- Make every paragraph earn its place. One action at a time.
- Three native editions with shared reporting: **en-US**, **pt-PT**, **fr**. French is not optional.
- Credit photographers, verify rights, date time-sensitive claims, disclose sponsored material.

### Prohibited patterns
"Nestled in", "hidden gem" without reporting, "vibrant tapestry", "something for everyone", "where tradition meets modernity", faux-first-person reporting ("we visited" unless real), invented quotations, generic Michelin/star claims, made-up flight schedules, unsupported sports/F1 calendar promises, manufactured luxury exclusivity.

## Editorial departments
1. **The Portugal Edit** — art, painting, architecture, craftsmanship, compelling businesses and founders.
2. **Places With a Pulse** — restaurants, coast, surf, golf, beaches, extraordinary people and regional character.
3. **The Good Life** — Vilamoura, Quinta do Lago, Lagos, Comporta, Porto, Lisbon and overlooked Portugal through family and lifestyle.
4. **The Property Brief** — grounded, sourced buying, ownership, costs, tax, policies, market research.
5. **Maré** — free, launching **Dec 1, 2026**, Tuesday `The Brief` (analysis/ideas) and Friday `The Escape` (one exceptional place/experience), same editorial core in three languages. Currently zero subscribers.

## Story hypotheses requiring reporting, not ready-made facts
- **"Portugal is closer than you think"**: compare explicit routes and airport pairs. For example Portugal's Algarve relative to NYC versus Los Angeles; verify geodesic distances, actual schedules and time zones. Distinguish NYC->Algarve from NYC->LA (different comparison). Numbers need citations and a date.
- **"Comporta is not the Hamptons"**: interrogate the analogy: dune ecology, local labour, rice fields, infrastructure, seasonality and culture; don't flatten a place to a Manhattan billionaire's vacation shorthand.
- **"The Algarve beyond the brochure"**: how golf, architecture, the sea and the food connect in Vilamoura/Quinta do Lago/Lagos. Use Details/Arrow verified management facts where relevant, disclose sponsorship.
- **"Portugal's culture is not décor"**: painters, patrons, artists, galleries, historic trades and modern makers; sourced, specific.
- **"The restaurant worth crossing town for"**: reportage with first-hand visit only if commissioned, otherwise attributed secondary reporting.

## Editing template
For each original content file: canonical path/slug; current copy; new opening/headline/dek; the reported observation; exact official/primary sources, photography licence; accuracy changes; geographic/audience localization; action; frontmatter/SEO; test results. No silent fact substitution.

## Release policy
Draft on editorial branch, build/test source links and localization, independent fact review for legal/financial/immigration matters, PR, release with verified deployment. Don't overwrite dates or fabricate "last checked". Correct mistakes visibly.
