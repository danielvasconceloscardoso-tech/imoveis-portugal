# Maré · Volume I — Editorial Production Bible (draft)
_Editorial issue start: 1 December 2026; year-one collectible target: December 2027_

## The vision
**Every Tuesday and Friday is a miniature collectible story, delivered as a beautiful email.** One publication serves: Portugal lovers, the diaspora and international families interested in places/property. Make every issue worth rereading. The annual object is a newly edited, art-directed book, not a stack of printed emails. Two strands: *The Brief* (people, history, business, art, culture, discoveries) and *The Escape* (places, exceptional kitchens, experiences and lives behind them).

## Each issue stores, not just renders, its materials
1. Title, deck, subject options, preheader, date, language, author and fact-checker; PT-PT, EN-US, FR versions written natively.
2. Source ledger: claim/URL/checked date/rights to quote/archival status and correction history. When a particular fact isn't proven, remove or label it before send.
3. Photography: specific subject, photographer, caption, alt text, original resolution, owner, license scope, attribution obligations, **email + web + print rights explicitly checked**. Do not confuse images from the site with automatically printable rights.
4. If an interview is used: audio/transcript permission, on/off-record terms, quote signoff as appropriate, releases. Do not invent intimate descriptions of homes, private addresses, quotes or endorsements.
5. Illustration and maps: editable source files, placement, provenance of data, font license for print and web.
6. Interactive: short question, choices, correct answer, instant explanation and source; accessible HTML link fallback and print puzzle counterpart; no visitor email or salary in URL.
7. Website canonical, source date, actual updated date, internal map link, tracking campaign, unsubscribe and GDPR handling.

## Editorial pass criteria
- *A dinner-party fact*: one striking and verified detail that someone could retell without exaggerating.
- Specific scene and place: no generic generic "hidden gems", no invented first-person travel experiences.
- A memorable, human rhythm: no box grids or relentless headings; the right facts, not a pile of facts.
- One illustration or documentary photograph central to the story; source credits; alt text. In email a tasteful mobile fallback when images disabled.
- No fake social proof, comments, poll totals, "best in Portugal" without an objective verified source, or implied sponsored/official endorsement.
- Sources preferably original archives, reputable reporting and direct custodians, with last checked date.
- No forced property CTA. Links exist because they help the reader.

## Email & site mechanics
- Email: 600px max width, embedded as table-based HTML with inline styles, readable without CSS and disabled images; proper unsubscribe inserted by Beehiiv, real buttons link to hosted interactive pages; no scripts in email.
- Site: accessible quiz / puzzle route with direct answer and source, preferring buttons with direct immediate state change; reduced motion; no cookie-required interaction. Log only non-identifying counts where consent/basis permits.
- Social: micro-story image + fact + source link, no click-bait withholding the answer.
- Newsletter is sent only by an explicit operator approval after test to real inboxes and QA.

## Distinctive print series: not a giant 100-email reprint
Working title: **MARÉ · O Ano em Portugal / A Year Worth Keeping / Une année portugaise**.
- Edit around 4 thematic sections: *People*, *Places*, *The Things We Made*, *History Still Happening*.
- Consider 20–35 best longform pieces expanded with new reporting from 100+ issues; shorter sidebars, print puzzles, maps and an updated chronology. Number and selection determined by real quality.
- Choose signature design traits now: dark forest-green cloth or textured paper cover, a small warm copper detail, warm ivory uncoated pages, serious original typography; deliberate white space, occasional centerfold map and full-bleed photography. Test print samples for accessibility, color fidelity and ink usage; don't copy another publisher's protected layout.
- **Standard**: beautiful bound book. **Collector's edition**: limited numbered copies with authorized signed bookplates or actual signed original inserts, genuine contributor features and provenance, not artificial scarcity.
- Do not set edition size, price or endorsements until permission/financial viability verified. Printing requires artwork rights beyond website/email license.

## Interview and signing outreach (permission-first)
- Build warm relationships with people genuinely featured: chef, artist, architect, entrepreneur, athlete or respected local expert. First ask for a conversation about their work; only once positive, discuss a signed issue/bookplate. Avoid suggesting that covering them secures an endorsement.
- Use their published professional representative, restaurant, publicist or gallery. Never counterfeit/scan signatures.
- Document grant of quote/publication rights, autograph format, edition size and any agreed fee, travel/security and timing before selling.
- Invite working craftspeople and local voices, not only celebrities: fishermen at Carrasqueira could be far more interesting than another famous holiday-home story.
