# Issue 10 — Regressar experience

The visitor should feel that a possible life in Portugal has become concrete without being asked to complete a tax return.

## Five decisions

1. **Feeling:** a personal possibility, grounded in figures that can be checked.
2. **Hero:** one annual Portuguese gross figure, followed by one place. No competing dashboard tiles.
3. **Arrival:** a quiet photographic stage and the question “E se fosse em Portugal?”. Country and known take-home pay are the only income inputs. The original interaction is the movement from a familiar number to a possible address, not a copy of a hospitality website.
4. **Next question:** “What leaves my salary?” opens a short tax breakdown; “Why this place?” exposes matched preferences and sourced housing assumptions. Annual figures stay annual. Monthly salary payment counts are explicit.
5. **One more thing:** a place is revealed only after it satisfies the stated constraints. The next option stays within that same valid set.

## Interaction contract

A short vertical reveal can create anticipation without gambling language, a spin button, confetti, fake randomness or repeated unresolved names. Reduced motion shows the final result immediately. Focus moves to the result heading only after an explicit action; live updates do not steal focus. The place photograph, name, a grounded explanation and one map link carry the result. Missing imagery has a composed text treatment, never an unrelated photograph.

Preference controls are independent accessible pressed buttons. Selected tastes are constraints, not decorative signals: a place without evidence for a selected preference cannot be described as a match. No result is an honest state with one adjustment action. A 35% housing allowance is a visible assumption, not an affordability certification. The remainder after housing must not be called disposable income when other living costs have not been deducted.

## Language and trust

`regressar-copy.ts` supplies typed pt-PT, en-US and French functional copy. Labels are short. No foreign gross salary is requested; no foreign tax is inferred. Article 12-A is a conditional scenario, never a default eligibility claim. Explain the versioned annual Portuguese tax method separately from temporary withholding changes. Show exchange-rate source and date, and stop a foreign-currency conversion when no verified rate exists.

Do not transmit the entered salary to analytics, URL parameters, map links or persistent storage. Completion, preference and map-open signals may use the existing consent-aware event interface without raw financial values. A map deep link includes the place identity only.

## Visual craft

Use the existing Playfair Display and Manrope typography, warm ivory, restrained dark green, broad photographic space and generous line spacing. Prefer horizontal rules and typographic alignment over a grid of square cards. Keep primary actions large enough for touch and every control keyboard reachable. Motion should last only long enough to make the transition legible; calculation itself stays immediate.

## Research boundary

This direction follows the operator’s Jobs × Belmond brief and the repository’s `content/METHOD.md` and `content/DESIGN_AND_DATA.md`. It does not claim live inspection of any third-party hospitality website, official partnership, or independently verified foreign tax system. Fiscal evidence and city-source verification belong to the corresponding implementation reports.
