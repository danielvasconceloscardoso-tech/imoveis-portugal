# Issue 4 — Portugal, as a place to live

**Feeling:** “I can see where I could live, and the next answer is already here.” The actual MapLibre map is the experience. Preserve its geographic identity; improve its natural terrain, composition and useful detail. This is an original direction grounded in `content/METHOD.md` and `content/DESIGN_AND_DATA.md`, not a copy of another brand.

## Composition and behaviour

- **One landscape.** Keep the coast and terrain continuously visible. Use muted sage land (`#dce3d2`), an Atlantic blue-gray (`#789ca6`), restrained deeper water (`#537d8d`), ivory surfaces (`#f7f4ed`) and dark green ink (`#243a33`). Reduce embossed hillshade and administrative boundaries at national zoom. Roads, local borders and dense labels appear as the camera approaches. Subtle sky and 3D support orientation; start around 28–35° pitch on desktop and 18–24° on phones, subject to actual viewport checks. Keep the existing engine and self-hosted assets.
- **An arrival, already on the map.** First entry gets an automatic 1.5–2.5 second introduction over living geography. An inset authentic photograph may occupy less than half the desktop canvas; on phones use a shallow, captioned image band. “Portugal” is the only large headline. A brief localized line and one action suffice. Skip and the primary action reveal the map immediately. Direct links, returning visitors and reduced-motion preferences bypass the introduction. Never use an intro timer to conceal a broken map or delay an intentional action.
- **A place before a report.** The balanced card reads: authentic photograph → place name → one existing, verified narrative line → one useful price answer → expandable next questions → one primary action. Keep source and period close to price facts. Exact-place photography only; an unavailable image gets an intentional typographic opening. Do not fill gaps with another municipality's photograph or unsourced sensory prose. Preserve photo attribution and sponsored identification where applicable.
- **Quiet, usable controls.** Playfair Display for names and primary answers; Manrope for navigation, facts and controls. Starting sizes: arrival 40px phone/52px desktop; card title 32–36px; body 15px/23px; price 24px/30px. Use 44px action targets, fine rules, restrained shadows and visible focus. Search must remain legible in Portuguese. A low horizontal region filmstrip replaces the tall desktop stack and recedes when a place card opens. Preserve pan, zoom, search, layers, save/share and direct links.
- **Space for geography.** At 390×844 and 430×932, the initial sheet should leave the selected geography visible above it; target 56–62svh maximum with internal scrolling and a reachable close control. At 1440×900 use a roughly 360–400px side panel and frame the selected point in the uncovered canvas. In short landscape viewports use a compact side panel instead of a tall portrait sheet. Keep attribution readable and outside controls.
- **Motion serves orientation.** Interface transitions take 200–300ms with a calm ease-out. Camera travel ends at the selected place without a flourish or orbit. Pointer/key interaction cancels automatic travel. Reduced motion uses immediate camera placement and no animated arrival. Loading, missing imagery and fetch errors remain intentional, actionable states.

## Evidence and research boundary — 5 November 2026

Inspected `MapV3.astro`, `site/src/styles/mapv3.css`, and actual issue3 before/after screenshots (390px US/LU and 1440px US). Observable problems include heavy relief/borders, overlapping circular project photographs, a tall desktop region stack, and truncated Portuguese search text. These observations motivate the direction above; they do not establish the final implementation's quality.

The official [MapLibre large-data guide source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js/main/docs/guides/large-data.md) was retrieved successfully (HTTP 200). It recommends simplifying styles, reducing unnecessary GeoJSON, loading data by URL, clustering points and using zoom-dependent detail. Apply those principles to keep the natural map responsive; avoid adding many decorative layers or loading all detail at arrival.

Live reference requests to [Belmond](https://www.belmond.com/), [Claridge's](https://www.claridges.co.uk/), [Apple Maps](https://www.apple.com/maps/), [FT HTSI](https://www.ft.com/htsi), [Hodinkee](https://www.hodinkee.com/), [Mapbox scroll-fly-to](https://docs.mapbox.com/mapbox-gl-js/example/scroll-fly-to/), [MapLibre large-data documentation](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/guides/large-data/), [Aman destinations](https://www.aman.com/destinations), [Google Earth](https://earth.google.com/web/) and [Four Seasons Private Jet](https://www.fourseasons.com/privatejet/) all returned **“Tunnel connection failed: 403 Forbidden”** through the configured proxy.
