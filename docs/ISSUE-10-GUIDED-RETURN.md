# Programa Regressar — guided return

## Implementation brief

Make Programa Regressar the starting point. A visitor should understand what to do next without entering a salary: understand the programme, check the relevant conditions, then prepare a conversation with official support. Keep the income simulator optional. Preserve the existing annual-tax assumptions, private local calculations, preference matching and municipality data. One clear primary action per section; no advisor, appointment, entitlement or government-partnership claims.

The hero now introduces the programme and offers three clear steps. The eligibility section sits before the financial form and is available independently. The salary comparison remains a separate optional section. Schools, hospitals, housing power and the interactive map remain available after a salary simulation.

## Official-support handoff

Visitors can reach the support section directly from the hero without calculating income or completing eligibility screening. Three established institutional origins distinguish programme guidance, IEFP employment/return support and AT tax confirmation. Links open the institution's website; the interface explicitly directs visitors to its published Contacts menu. These are not freshly verified direct-contact pages, a named-advisor directory or a booking service.

The browser prepares a brief containing practical questions, an explicitly selected country and an indicated return year. It excludes salary, budget and the four tax-screening answers. No country is inferred from edition defaults. Copying is user initiated; if clipboard permission is denied, the same readonly text is focused and selected for manual copying. Nothing is emailed, submitted, stored or forwarded by Imóveis Portugal.

Current official-site verification remains blocked by the environment's destination policy. A supported network-draft save was attempted but returned `draft_not_editable`; persistence was not confirmed. The proposed host list is retained in `site/artifacts/issue10/network-proposal-guided-return.json`. No proxy bypass, contact guess or credential request was used.
