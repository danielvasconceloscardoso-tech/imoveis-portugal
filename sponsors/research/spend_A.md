## Notes

**Method.** Each figure was read in the report PDF (pdftotext, then the note table), "p.N" is the page number of the PDF file (as a PDF viewer counts it), not the folio printed on the page. Each PDF was deleted after extraction. Revenue figures come from the same report's management summary.

**Banks (2025).** Portugal-level advertising lines run from 6.2 to 17.3 M€, or 0.33% to 0.55% of banking income:
- Santander Totta 6.2 M€ (0.33%); BCP Portugal 7.4 M€ (0.36%); novobanco 8.6 M€ (0.55%); CGD 17.3 M€ (0.50%).
- BCP group including Poland is 36.5 M€ (0.96%), but Poland drives that number. Use the 7.4 M€ individual figure for Portugal.
- CGD, novobanco and BCP raised the line in 2025 (+13% to +23%). Santander cut it by 10%.
- Banks book these lines under "Gastos gerais administrativos" or "Outras despesas administrativas". The labels vary: "Publicidade e edição de publicações" (CGD), "Publicidade e publicações" (novobanco), "Advertising" in the English versions (BCP, Santander).
- Sponsorships are usually not in this line. CGD reports "investimento na comunidade (incluindo patrocínios)" of about 20 M€ separately in the management report (PDF p.8, narrative text, not a note line). That figure is community investment, not advertising.

**Insurers (2025).** Only Fidelidade was verified:
- Fidelidade: "Publicidade e propaganda" 13.5 M€ for the insurance company and 34.5 M€ for the group. The group figure includes Luz Saúde and foreign operations.
- Insurers report it inside "custos de exploração por natureza › Fornecimentos e serviços externos". The other insurers file this note in their Relatório e Contas, but their PDFs were not reached in this run.

**Hospital groups.**
- CUF (2025): "Publicidade e propaganda" 4.3 M€ consolidated at CUF, S.A., about 0.44% of 970 M€ operating income. 2.9 M€ of that is brand marketing booked at the CUF, S.A. holding.
- Luz Saúde (2023 only): "Publicidade" 1.8 M€, about 0.27% of operating income.
- Hospital groups spend clearly less than banks in absolute terms. Their share of revenue is in the same band.

**Caveat for pricing.** These lines capture paid media and production. They usually exclude marketing staff and sponsorship/community budgets (CGD shows this) and digital fees booked under "serviços especializados". Treat them as a floor for the total brand budget.
