## 3. Notes per category

**Two kinds of numbers, never mix them.** (a) Filings give net cost actually booked (TAP, NOS, Jerónimo Martins, Ryanair, easyJet). (b) MediaMonitor (Marktest) gives investment at rate card ("a preços de tabela", list prices before the discounts negotiated with media). Rate-card figures are many times net spend: the top 10 advertisers alone total about €4.7bn at rate card, while the whole Portuguese net ad market was forecast at just over €0.9bn for 2025 (Magna) and the IAB Europe estimate for Portuguese digital is €612m. Use MediaMonitor only for ranking and share of voice, not as a budget.

**Airline.** TAP books "Publicity" of €28.9m in 2025 (€28.6m in 2024), inside a €216.4m "Commercial, marketing and communication costs" caption that is mostly commissions, booking fees and sales charges. The TAP report also notes that an advertisement ban from the EU-approved restructuring plan remained in force (p. 160 context; refers to advertising the state aid / acquisitions, check before using in a pitch). Ryanair and easyJet only report bundled lines (Ryanair's includes distribution and a fine provision). Group figures are worldwide, not Portugal.

**Telecoms.** NOS is the only Portuguese telco with a clean public line: "Marketing and advertising" €40.2m in 2025, up 17% from €34.2m. MEO (Altice Portugal, private) and Vodafone Portugal (private subsidiary) do not publish a comparable line online; MediaMonitor ranks MEO #6, NOS #7, Vodafone #9 in 2025 at rate card (€450.6m / €441.3m / €411.2m). Telecoms = 3 of the top 20 advertisers (Dec 2025).

**Retail / home setup.** Jerónimo Martins reports group "Advertising costs" of €179m (2025), but Pingo Doce/Portugal is not broken out and most of the group is Biedronka in Poland. Sonae (Continente, Worten) is Portugal's #1 advertiser at rate card (Continente €630.4m, 2025); Sonae's own annual-report advertising line could not be read (site blocks scripted access). Retail is 7 of the top 20 advertisers (Dec 2025). Lidl (#4, €452.1m rate card) is not on the target list but is a reference point.

**Wine.** Sogrape, Symington, Fladgate (The Fladgate Partnership) and Esporão are private and do not publish marketing spend; not found in this pass.

**Market.** Portuguese media spend is TV-heavy at rate card (TV 89.3%, internet 4.9% in 2025 MediaMonitor), but on a net basis digital is much larger (IAB Europe: €612m digital, +10.2%, 2025). Portugal has no official national digital ad-spend study of its own (IAB Europe estimate is the reference). Magna (IPG Mediabrands) forecast the total market above €900m for 2025 (+6%); MediaPost reports Magna's Dec 2025 update is likely its last public forecast.
