## Finance: the two camps (in each `why_fits`)

- **Neobanks and brokers.** These are Trade Republic, bunq, N26, XTB, eToro, DEGIRO, Trading 212, Lightyear, Openbank, Nubank, Inter&Co and C6. Pitch them the letter block, the Friday item and the Partner level, aimed at a younger audience. Trade Republic is the strongest lead: it opened a Portuguese branch with a PT50 IBAN on 16 Jul 2026, has about 200,000 clients in Portugal and is aiming for 500,000. After it come Trading 212, which named its first Portugal country manager in Sep 2026, and N26, which plans to "localise" its business and add MB WAY (Oct 2026).
- **Traditional and private banks.** These are Banco Finantia, Bison Bank, Banco do Brasil and Bradesco. Pitch them Official Partner, "Apresentado por" and the sponsored story on borrowing as a non-resident. The Portuguese banks were already on the list, so I added only the missing ones.
