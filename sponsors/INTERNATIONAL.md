**Chambers and embassy.** A "Portuguese-Israeli Chamber of Commerce (CCLI)" appears in an undated Portugal Resident article, as newly formed and holding a lunch in Porto for about 50 people. No website was found, so Daniel or the partner must confirm it exists. The Embassy of Israel in Lisbon has a public site but no economic or trade page was found; Israel's missions often have an economic section, but that is an inference for Lisbon.

**Audience size.** Few Israelis live in Portugal: 569 in 2022 (SEF, via Times of Israel, 2 Sep 2023). Many hold the passport: 20,975 Israelis applied for Portuguese citizenship in 2022 and 21,263 in 2021, and about 60,000 Israelis held Portuguese citizenship in 2022 (same article, fetched). The Israeli reader is therefore mostly a passport holder or investor abroad, not an emigrant. The rules for the Sephardic route changed after 2022: check the current status before writing about it.

**What an Israeli buyer needs to know** (facts base):
1. Income from property in Portugal may be taxed in Portugal, and Israel gives a credit for the Portuguese tax (treaty of 2006, arts. 6 and 23; `il-pt-treaty-property`).
2. Non-residents pay a flat 7.5% IMT unless an exception applies (`pt-imt-nonresident-flat`, `pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions`).
3. Residence: the real-estate Golden Visa is closed to new applications since 7 Oct 2023 (`pt-ari-realestate-ended`); D7 is for people living on their own income (`pt-visa-own-income`); IFICI is a 20% rate for 10 years for eligible activities (`pt-ifici-20pct`).
4. Not verified: Israel's own reporting rules on a foreign home, and whether Israeli banks lend against it (gov.il refuses automated access; `content/faq/UNVERIFIED.md`).

## USA

**Capital and sponsors.** No US-capital residential developer with a verified Portuguese project turned up. US investment shows in the luxury segment instead: Sotheby's Portugal reports US investment in luxury homes up 82% in Q2 2025 year on year, with Lisbon at 53% of demand, the Algarve 21%, Porto 12% and Madeira 11% (Essential Business, 8 Oct 2025, fetched). Lone Star, a US fund, owned Vilamoura from 2015 and sold it in 2021 (search results only), so it is not a sponsor target. Kronos Homes (already in the pipeline as tier C) reports growing US interest (search result). Sotheby's is an agency (D9).

**Chamber and embassy.** AmCham Portugal is the door: about 300 member companies, a monthly newsletter, more than 40 events a year, and 49 sponsors in Platinum, Gold and Silver tiers (its own site, fetched). It publishes no prices and no real-estate committee. The U.S. Commercial Service in Lisbon serves US exporters, so the draft asks only for an introduction (search result; the staff names on trade.gov disagree between pages).

**Audience size.** AIMA counts 19,258 US citizens living in Portugal in 2024, up 36% from 14,129 in 2023 (Portugal Resident, 27 Oct 2025, fetched). A higher figure of 20,959 appears in The Portugal News from US embassy data; a claim of more than 50,000 has no official source and is not used. US readers who live in the US are the larger group and have no count in this run.

**What a US buyer needs to know** (facts base):
1. A US person files an FBAR when foreign accounts exceed 10,000 USD in total at any time in the year (`us-fbar`), and Form 8938 above 200,000 USD at year end for a single filer living abroad (`us-8938-abroad`).
2. Gains on Portuguese property can be taxed in Portugal, the US keeps the right to tax its citizens, and the foreign tax credit (Form 1116) avoids double tax (`us-pt-treaty-gains`).
3. The same 7.5% IMT and the same Golden Visa closure apply.
4. Not verified: whether US banks or Portuguese banks lend to a US-resident buyer on different terms (no official source; the FAQ quotes only Banco de Portugal's limits).
