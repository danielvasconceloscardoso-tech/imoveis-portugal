# Rate card v5: price what a customer is worth to the partner (CEO proposal, 30 Oct 2026)

**The rule.** Every price is set from what one customer we bring is worth to the partner, then charged as a flat, upfront fee for a period or an inventory. Flat and upfront keeps it advertising, not mediation (Lei 15/2013, art. 2) and respects D6; sizing it on their unit economics is what makes it a real price instead of a favour. Founding Partner terms (D40) still apply: the first 10 get 40% off year 1, price locked for year 2.

Benchmarks are market practice, not promises; the partner's own numbers decide.

## 1. Developers: per unit, not per building

- A developer pays 3–5% commission on every sale (€10,000–17,000 on a €350,000 home) and spends 1–3% of the project's sales value on marketing. "€5,000 per building" is €100 per apartment: a rounding error, and it tells them we don't know our worth.
- **Price per unit listed**, flat, for 12 months or until the unit is sold, whichever comes first; the pin, the showcase, the Concierge, the weekly letters in six editions, the sales-team view and the assessment included.

| Price band of the unit | Per unit | A 55-unit building |
| --- | --- | --- |
| up to 250,000 € | 400 € | 22,000 € |
| 250,000–500,000 € | 700 € | 38,500 € |
| above 500,000 € | 1,200 € | 66,000 € |

- Minimum 10,000 € per project. Exclusivity in the municipality for the period: +50%.
- Founding Partner: −40% year 1. A 55-unit building at 700 € = 38,500 € list, 23,100 € founding.
- **Marble Terrace:** the 5,000 € already quoted stands as the founding reference customer; the agreement states the list price (38,500 €) and the 12-month term, so renewal and the next developers start from the list.

## 2. Golf clubs and resorts: the price of one member

- A top club's international member is worth tens of thousands over the years: annual memberships of 3,000–10,000 €, joining fees that at the top resorts can exceed 20,000 €, plus green fees, lessons and the house they buy nearby.
- **Golf Collection partner:** 24,000 €/yr, by invitation, maximum 8 (the Top 100 Golf Courses Portugal ranking as the invitation list). Includes the course card on the map and the golf layer, the Golf collection sponsor slot, one feature a quarter in all six editions, the "Living on the fairway" series.
- **Single course listing:** 12,000 €/yr.

## 3. Lawyers: one client pays the year

- A purchase mandate is typically 1,500–3,000 € in fees, more with the NIF, the power of attorney and the deed. Fee-sharing is prohibited, so the price is a flat card per edition.
- **Partner lawyer card:** 3,600 €/yr per edition, exclusive per edition and language (6 editions × 1 lawyer). One partner in two editions: 6,000 €.

## 4. Banks and mortgage intermediaries

- A 250,000 € non-resident mortgage earns the bank 15,000–30,000 € of margin over its life, plus the insurance.
- **Finance partner per edition:** 12,000 €/yr, exclusive: the mortgage step in Passo a passo, the simulators' "ask a partner bank" action, the Concierge's finance card, one feature a quarter.

## 5. Insurance, tax advisers, movers of money

- Home and life cover for a mortgaged home: 600–1,500 €/yr per household, for decades. **Insurance partner:** 6,000 €/yr per edition.
- A non-resident's annual Portuguese tax return and advice: 800–2,000 €/yr recurring. **Tax partner card:** 3,600 €/yr per edition, exclusive.
- Money transfers: flat only (D45). **Transfer partner:** 6,000 €/yr across editions.

## 6. Municipalities: the IMT argument

- IMT and IMI are municipal revenue (Lei 73/2013, art. 14). One buyer we bring pays the municipality 8,000–17,000 € of IMT on a 300,000–400,000 € home, then IMI every year. One buyer pays for the partnership several times over.
- **Município parceiro:** 9,000 €/yr: the municipality page (programmes for returning residents, incentives, contacts), the featured place card, the index breakdown with the municipality's own data note, one feature a year in all editions. Direct award is possible below 20,000 € (CCP, art. 20(1)(d)); needs D4.
- **Portugal 100 presenting sponsor:** 25,000 €, one, exclusive, labelled (a bank, an insurer or a region).
- **Portugal 100 badge licence** for commercial use: 1,500 €/yr.

## 7. Everything around the house: the collections

- A new-build buyer furnishes and fits out for 20,000–50,000 €. Airlines sell the flights, every month.
- **Collection sponsor** (Comprar do estrangeiro · Vender e herdar · Golfe · Vinho · Mar · Madeira): 12,000 €/yr per collection, exclusive, labelled. Kitchens, furniture, airlines, wine estates, hotels.

## 8. Readers and professionals

- **Paid call** with the qualified advisor: 150 € / 45 min (unchanged).
- **Guides:** 29–49 € (unchanged); **Portugal 100 report:** 39 €.
- **Pro data membership** (the index dataset quarterly, early access, custom weights, parish level where available): 2,400 €/yr per seat; banks, insurers, developers, asset managers; agencies only if 8y opens.
- **Development assessment** (the badge): 490 € per assessment, 990 €/yr to keep it; included for listed developments.

## 10. The step-up

Prices are for the pre-launch and launch period. The rate card states that list prices rise 50% when the six editions pass 10,000 subscribers combined, and that Founding Partners keep their price for year 2 (D40). The step-up is written into every agreement from the first one.
