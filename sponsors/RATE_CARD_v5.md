# Rate card v5: price what a customer is worth to the partner (CEO proposal, 30 Oct 2026)

**The rule.** Every price is set from what one customer we bring is worth to the partner, then charged as a flat, upfront fee for a period or an inventory. Flat and upfront keeps it advertising, not mediation (Lei 15/2013, art. 2) and respects D6; sizing it on their unit economics is what makes it a real price instead of a favour. Founding Partner terms (D40) still apply: the first 10 get 40% off year 1, price locked for year 2.

Benchmarks are market practice, not promises; the partner's own numbers decide.

## 1. Developers: per unit, not per building

- A developer pays 3–5% commission on every sale (10,000–17,000 € on a 350,000 € home), spends 1–3% of the project's sales value on marketing, 5,000–15,000 € on the brochure and renders, 20,000–50,000 € on a launch event, 1,000–3,000 € a month on portal placements. "5,000 € per building" is the price of the brochure for 55 apartments: 100 € a door.
- What they buy from us is the brochure that reaches six countries, is never out of date, asks the buyer the qualifying questions and reports back. Priced against what they already spend, a 55-unit project at list is one launch event, or two and a half commissions, for twelve months.
- **Price per unit listed**, flat, for 12 months or until the unit is sold, whichever comes first; the pin, the showcase, the Concierge, the weekly letters in six editions, the sales-team view and the assessment included.

## 2. Golf: sell to resorts as developers, list clubs for free

- A golf club does not pay to be found: Google Maps already does that, and a round of golf is worth 100–200 €. What a golf **resort** sells is houses: Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Monte Rei, Palmares, West Cliffs, Dunas Comporta, Pinheirinho are developers with a course attached. They buy the developer product (§1), per unit, with the golf layer and the index's golf indicator as the hook.
- All 79 courses stay on the map for free, from our own list: readers need them, and a resort that already appears on the golf layer with its readership numbers is the easiest call we will make.
- **Golf Collection partner** (24,000 €/yr, by invitation, maximum 8) is a year-2 product, sold with a full year of golf-layer readership numbers behind it. Not before.

## 3. Lawyers: one client pays the year

- A purchase mandate is typically 1,500–3,000 € in fees, more with the NIF, the power of attorney and the deed. Fee-sharing is prohibited, so the price is a flat card per edition.
- **Partner lawyer card:** 3,600 €/yr per edition, exclusive per edition and language (6 editions × 1 lawyer). One partner in two editions: 6,000 €.

## 4. Banks: the client is worth more than anything else on this card

- The reader is someone moving money and a life to Portugal. The first thing they need, before the NIF, is a Portuguese bank account; the richest of them move 1–5 M€ and finance a 1–3 M€ home. A 5 M€ private-banking client is worth about 50,000 €/yr to a bank; a 3 M€ mortgage, 30,000–45,000 €/yr in margin for twenty years. One client pays five years of any price below.
- These banks already buy diaspora media: the "residentes no estrangeiro" units (CGD, Millennium bcp, Novobanco, BPI, Santander) advertise on RTP Internacional and in Contacto and Lusojornal. We are diaspora media with the buyer at the moment of decision. That is the pitch, not page views.
- **Banco parceiro, per edition:** 12,000 €/yr, exclusive in the edition's finance slot: the sponsor slot on the finance pages, the Concierge's finance card, the mortgage step in Passo a passo, the simulators' "simulate with our partner bank" action, one feature a quarter. All six editions: 48,000 €/yr (the Private Partner of D40). The editorial comparison of banks lending to non-residents stays editorial, with sources; exclusivity buys the slot and the card, never the comparison.
- **Private banking partner (US, Brazil, Israel editions):** 24,000 €/yr per edition, exclusive: the "Private" series (moving wealth, opening the account, residence, the 1 M€+ purchase), and the Concierge's Private card shown when the reader's budget passes 1 M€. For Luxembourg and Switzerland, the same product to banks with Portuguese or Brazilian desks.
- **Credit intermediaries** (Banco de Portugal register, non-resident specialists): 4,800 €/yr per edition, up to two, non-exclusive, for the mid-market mortgage.
- Founding Partner −40% applies. Sold now: these units decide on the segment, and they have the budget line already.

## 5. Insurance, tax advisers, movers of money

- Home and life cover for a mortgaged home: 600–1,500 €/yr per household, for decades. **Insurance partner:** 6,000 €/yr per edition.
- A non-resident's annual Portuguese tax return and advice: 800–2,000 €/yr recurring. **Tax partner card:** 3,600 €/yr per edition, exclusive.
- Money transfers: flat only (D45). **Transfer partner:** 6,000 €/yr across editions.

## 6. Municipalities: the IMT argument

- IMT and IMI are municipal revenue (Lei 73/2013, art. 14). One buyer we bring pays the municipality 8,000–17,000 € of IMT on a 300,000–400,000 € home, then IMI every year. One buyer pays for the partnership several times over.
- Municipalities do not buy page views; they buy returning families, investment and a story for the local press. They buy through procurement and only from an entity with tax clearance (D4). So, before launch, we sell them nothing and give them two things: their own index card with every indicator and its source (they will want to argue with it, which is the relationship), and a free municipality page listing their published incentives for returning residents, with a request to verify it. The Portugal 100 reveal in December is the moment they notice us.
- **From Q1 2027, "Município parceiro" pilot: 4,900 €** for 12 months, priced under the simplified direct award threshold (CCP, art. 128: one signature, no formal contract): the verified municipality page, the featured place card, the index note, one feature in the editions where their emigrants live. Renewal at 9,000 €/yr, above which the direct award under art. 20(1)(d) still applies.
- First targets: Gondomar (the partner group's base), then the Minho and Trás-os-Montes municipalities whose emigrants fill the Luxembourg and French editions, pitched with that edition's readership numbers, not the total.
- **Portugal 100 presenting sponsor:** 25,000 €, one, exclusive, labelled (a bank, an insurer or a region).
- **Portugal 100 badge licence** for commercial use: 1,500 €/yr.

## 7. Everything around the house: the collections

- A new-build buyer furnishes and fits out for 20,000–50,000 €. Airlines sell the flights, every month.
- **Collection sponsor** (Comprar do estrangeiro · Vender e herdar · Golfe · Vinho · Mar · Madeira): 12,000 €/yr per collection, exclusive, labelled. Kitchens, furniture, airlines, wine estates, hotels.

## 7b. The feed: featured posts and campaigns (the Gentleman's Gram / Visit Madeira model)

- Curated lifestyle pages earn from placements: a brand pays per post, priced on followers and fit (lifestyle accounts clear roughly 10 € per 1,000 followers per post); tourism boards (Visit Madeira, Visit Portugal, the regional promotion agencies) pay creators and media for content in their source markets and repost what fits their image. Our feed is that page with a number on every image: the place, the index score, the price per m². Nobody else can post that.
- **Featured post** (one image or carousel, labelled "Patrocinado"): 300 € pre-launch, repriced every quarter at 10 € per 1,000 followers, minimum 300 €. **Featured reel** (drone or film, 20–40 s): 2× the post price. Posted on all six editions' accounts and in that week's letters.
- **Golf spot of the week** (post + reel + letter feature): 1,500 € pre-launch; included for Golf Collection partners.
- **Concelho da semana** (post + reel + the index card + letter feature + the municipality page): 2,500 € pre-launch, 4,900 € from Q1 2027 (CCP art. 128).
- **Developer launch** (reel + stories + letter slot + the showcase): 2,500 €; included in the per-unit listing.
- **Tourism-board campaign** (regional promotion agencies, AP Madeira, Turismo de Portugal source-market budgets): 4–8 posts, 2 reels, a collection cover, a letter feature in the editions they target: 7,500–15,000 € per campaign; procurement, so D4 first.
- Rules: every paid post labelled (Código da Publicidade art. 8; Instagram's paid-partnership tool; France's "collaboration commerciale" wording in the FR captions); facts and sources as everywhere (D12); reposted creator content only with written permission, logged.

## 9. How any of this gets sold with no audience yet

1. **Sell the segment and the moment, not the audience.** Every partner on this card sells to people moving money and a life to Portugal; we hold those people at the moment they decide where and with whom. That is true on day one with 300 subscribers, and the price is set on what one such client is worth to them.
2. **The annual agreement is the default; the 90-day pilot is the fallback** for a hesitant developer: flat, for the build we deliver and a monthly report, renewing at list −40% unless cancelled. We promise reporting, never results.
3. **Order of calls:** the "residentes no estrangeiro" and private-banking units of the Portuguese banks; developers with unsold stock in the North and the Algarve and the golf-resort developers; credit intermediaries; lawyers and tax advisers; insurers. Municipalities, collections and pro data follow the Portugal 100 in December.
4. **Outreach to Portuguese companies does not wait for 8q** if the operator confirms the reading: marketing email to legal persons in Portugal is opt-out, not consent (Lei 41/2004, art. 13.º-A, n.º 3), on legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f), recital 47). The stricter regimes (Switzerland, Israel) matter for later.

## 11. The step-up

Prices are for the pre-launch and launch period. The rate card states that list prices rise 50% when the six editions pass 10,000 subscribers combined, and that Founding Partners keep their price for year 2 (D40). The step-up is written into every agreement from the first one.
