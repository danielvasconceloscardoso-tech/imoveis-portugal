# Letter monetization v2 (prompt 54 §3), 2 Nov 2026

One letter for everyone, every Tuesday and Friday, in PT, EN and FR (D74), in the voice of `content/VOICE_AND_WORLD.md` (D73). This plan applies `sponsors/NEWSLETTER_PLAYBOOK.md` to it.

The prices start from the CEO's numbers in prompt 53 §5. Where the research argues for a change, the change is marked **proposal**. **All prices are for the CEO to approve** (CLAUDE.md, How to work 5; task 45b). Letter products only: the per-building developer placement (D63), the partner agency's sponsorship and social extensions are priced separately in RATE_CARD_v5.

## 1. Inventory

- 104 letters a year, about 8.7 a month.
- **Each letter carries one sponsor element**, plus the Friday weekend item.
- **Per month:**
  - one block on every letter, sold by the letter or by the month, exclusive by category;
  - one "Apresentado por" (the month's title sponsor);
  - at most one sponsored story;
  - up to four Friday weekend items.
- **Per year:** four to six themed series of six letters each, and the Portugal 100 reveal (late November).

Rules for the prices:
- **Thresholds.** A price changes on the first day of the quarter after the list passes a threshold, using the confirmed subscribers on the operator's report.
- **Raises.** When the next quarter is 80% booked, raise prices and open a waiting list; never add a second sponsor element.
- **No discounts.** Give inventory instead: an extra Friday item, a social post.
- **Minimum.** Three months, with the step-up written into the first contract.
- **Exclusivity.** By category, per letter and per month, at +50% for a whole quarter.

**For the CEO (task 54a):**
1. "Apresentado por" at 4,000 € a month is about 48,000 € a year. That is 4 to 6 times the Gold package in CLAUDE.md (8–12k € a year), so the two need one ladder. Proposal: Gold = 6 "Apresentado por" months or 12 block-months, Silver = 3 block-months, Bronze = one series.
2. At 1,000 subscribers no benchmark supports 250 € per letter: it works out to about 556 € per 1,000 opens, against $15–75 in the market (playbook §3). It sells only as a Founding Partnership: the brand next to the index and the Portugal 100, the price locked, the monthly report. The media kit shows a price per 1,000 subscribers only from 5,000.
3. Paid recommendations (beehiiv Boosts, SparkLoop) pay per subscriber. Is that "per lead" under D6? Proposal: we don't sell them. We may buy them for growth once a sponsor has paid.

## 3. The quality rules we adopt

1. **One sponsor element per letter.** On Fridays, the weekend item may be a second one, and is labelled the same way. Never a dedicated sponsor send.
2. **The label cannot be missed.** "Patrocinado", "Apresentado por {brand}", "A história, apresentada por {brand}" (Código da Publicidade art. 8, hard rule 4), in the same type as the letter, at the top of the element.
3. **We write it, they approve the facts.** Our writers draft every sponsor element in the letter's voice (Morning Brew). The sponsor checks names, numbers and its offer, never our editorial.
4. **The right to refuse, with a written test** (Not Boring):
   - Would we write about this company unpaid?
   - Is it useful to someone buying or living in Portugal from abroad?
   - Is its offer legal and plain, with no "guaranteed returns"?

   We publish the test and refuse what fails it.
5. **Never sold:**
   - per lead or per click (D6);
   - any real-estate agency other than the partner (D9);
   - the index score or a Portugal 100 place (D69);
   - paid entry into any ranking or award (the 2008 Wine Spectator lesson);
   - a sponsor's sight of editorial before it is published, except its own element.
6. **Category caps** (FT HTSI): at most two sponsors from one category in a month, and no more than five campaigns live at once.
7. **Every sponsor gets the monthly report** (prompt 51): reach, clicks, readers by country, requests and the advisor's reply time, the same figures on their private page. The report is what renews (playbook §4).

## 6. Three scenarios for 12 months (Dec 2026 to Nov 2027)

Computed by `sponsors/build/letter_scenarios.py`. Letter products only, at the prices above. The Founding −40% applies to the first ten sponsors. Event figures are the estimated margins in §4.

| Scenario | Subscribers in Nov 2027 | Letter revenue, list prices | After the Founding discount, plus event margin | Months 1–6 (list) | Months 7–12 (list) | Per subscriber (Nov 2027) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conservative | 6,000 | 35,762 € | **21,457 €** | 13,950 € | 21,812 € | 3.6 € |
| Base | 10,000 | 116,302 € | **94,737 €** | 40,703 € | 75,599 € | 9.5 € |
| Ambitious | 25,000 | 300,896 € | **280,753 €** | 91,664 € | 209,232 € | 11.2 € |

**Assumptions, written out:**
- **Conservative.**
  - Subscribers: 800 by the end of December, 3,000 by May, 6,000 by November. This is below the PLAN target of 5,000 by 24 Jan.
  - Blocks: 25% of block-months sold in months 1–6, 40% after.
  - Other sales: four "Apresentado por" months, two series, Friday items from month 3, two sponsored stories, no events.
  - All sponsors at the Founding price.
- **Base.**
  - Subscribers: 5,000 by March and 10,000 by November.
  - Blocks: 40% sold in months 1–3, 60% in months 4–6, 75% after.
  - Other sales: ten "Apresentado por" months, four series, three or four Friday items a month, five sponsored stories, a Douro dinner (May) and a golf day (October).
  - 70% of revenue at the Founding price.
- **Ambitious.**
  - Subscribers: 5,000 by January, 10,000 by May, 25,000 by November.
  - Blocks: 60% sold, then 90% from month 4.
  - Other sales: nine "Apresentado por" months, six series, four or five Friday items, ten sponsored stories, four events.
  - 40% of revenue at the Founding price.

**What the numbers say:**
- **The base case** earns about 9.5 € per subscriber in a year. That is above Morning Brew, The Hustle and 1440 at about 5–7 € (D, playbook §3), because a premium niche sells higher units. It needs the base sell-through to hold.
- **The conservative case** is below one Gold sponsor a month. It still covers the year's costs (domains and free tools, hard rule 5).
- **The ambitious case** depends on per-reader prices holding at the premium end: about 133 € per 1,000 opens at 45% opens. If the market prices us as an ordinary niche ($30–75), the year 2 figures halve.

**The levers, in order:**
1. sell-through of the blocks (a calendar sold in advance);
2. the "Apresentado por" months to banks in December–January;
3. subscribers.
