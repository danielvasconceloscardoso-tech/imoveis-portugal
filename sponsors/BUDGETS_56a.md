# What they really spend (task 56a), 2 Nov 2026

**The rule (a proposal for the CEO):** an Official Partner costs **1% of the median advertising line, Portugal scope, of the sourced companies in its category**, rounded to 5,000 €. Partner is 30% of that and Featured 10%.

The figures come from the 2025 annual reports' advertising lines unless stated. These lines are a **floor**: sponsorship sits outside them. CGD also reports about 20 M€ of "investimento na comunidade (incluindo patrocínios)".

**The market:**
- Digital advertising in Portugal was **612 M€ in 2025**, up 10.2% (IAB Europe AdEx, via ECO, 23 Jul 2026).
- The total market was forecast above **900 M€ for 2025** (IPG Mediabrands/Magna, December 2024 forecast).
- MediaMonitor's 2025 ranking (Continente #1 at 630 M€, MEO #6, NOS #7, Vodafone #9) is at **rate card**, about 10× real spend: NOS 441 M€ at rate card against 40.2 M€ in its accounts. Use it to rank companies, never as a budget.

**What it means:**
- Banks and hospital groups spend less on advertising than v6 assumed, so their anchors fall.
- TAP and NOS spend more, so theirs rise.
- **Founding −40%** and the **90-day pilot at 25%** still apply. The banking pilot is 20,000 €, about 0.15–0.3% of one bank's line.
