## Advertising rules noted per company

- **Wine, port and spirits:** Código da Publicidade art. 17 (no minors, no link to driving or performance, the legal warning) and the Loi Évin in France. The short notes for Switzerland, Brazil, the US and Israel must be checked with counsel before any alcohol campaign runs there.
- **Health:** DL 238/2015 (ERS): no claims of results, no comparisons, provider registered with the ERS. The ERS number is noted where the provider shows it.
- **Finance:** Banco de Portugal and CMVM rules: APR in credit examples, risk warnings for investing.
- **Insurance:** we advertise; the quote happens on the insurer's side (Lei 7/2019).
- **Cars:** fuel consumption and CO2 in car advertising (EU Directive 1999/94/EC as applied in Portugal: to confirm with counsel).
