# Partnerships v6: sold top-down, as annual category partnerships (CEO proposal, 2 Nov 2026)

**Why v5 was wrong.** It priced us bottom-up like a small newsletter (price per letter, per 1,000 readers). That puts us in a brand's "newsletter test" line, a few thousand euros. The money sits in a different line: the annual partnership and brand budget, where a bank, an airline or a hospital group spends six figures to own a moment and an audience. We are not a newsletter with a map; we are the platform that owns the moment someone decides to move money and a life to Portugal. Sell that.

## The architecture

1. **One Official Partner per category per year, exclusive across every channel:** the map, the Concierge, the letter, the social accounts, the events, the Portugal 100, the annual data report. Categories: Banking · Private banking · Digital banking and investing · Mortgage · Insurance · Health · Education (schools) · Higher education · Airline · Wine · Spirits and coffee · Mobility (cars) · Energy and home · Hotels · Golf · Telecoms · Luxury.
2. **Three levels inside a category** (only one company can hold Official; others can buy the lower levels if the Official partner's exclusivity allows it, priced at a fraction):
   - **Official Partner** (exclusive, all channels, 12 months, title of the category: "Parceiro oficial de banca").
   - **Partner** (non-exclusive presence: featured on the map and in the Concierge, a quarter of the letter inventory).
   - **Featured** (map and Concierge only).
3. **Portugal 100 Presenting Partner** (one company, all categories): the annual ranking, the reveal week, the event, the printed edition.
4. **Developers** stay on per-unit pricing (rate card v5 §1).

## How they buy (so the price can be met)

1. **Founding partner per category:** the first company to sign in each category gets −40% in year 1, price locked for year 2, and its name on the launch. That's the honest discount for being first; it's also the urgency.
2. **A 90-day pilot at 25% of the annual price**, credited against the year if they convert. Big companies test before they commit; this lets them say yes inside a quarter's budget.
3. **The pitch is the moment, not the reach:** "Every person in this audience is deciding where to bank, insure, school, fly and live in Portugal in the next 12 months. One company per category is with them at that moment." Backed by the platform walkthrough, the prototype letter with their category, the map with their branches, schools or hospitals already on it, and the monthly report they will receive.
4. **Sell to the right person:** the marketing or brand director with the annual partnership budget, not the digital buyer with a CPM sheet.
5. **Scarcity is real:** one Official partner per category, 17 categories, ten founding places.

## What a year adds up to

Five Official founding partners at the low end of their ranges, after −40%: about 300,000 €. Plus the Portugal 100 presenting partner and the developers.
