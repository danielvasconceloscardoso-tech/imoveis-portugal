## 4. What is included

The pin on the map, the card in the Concierge, the project page with the availability of each block, requests passed to the qualified advisor of the partner agency, the weekly letter in six editions, the sales-team view, the assessment against the published criteria and the monthly report. No fee per request, visit or sale. All advertising is labelled as such.

## 5. Step-up clause

List prices rise 50% when the six editions pass 10,000 subscribers combined. A Founding Partner keeps its founding price for year 2, even if the step-up has already happened. From year 3 the rate card in force applies.

## 6. Next step

The platform sends the sponsorship agreement with these figures. The booking lapses on the date above if the agreement is not signed. This form creates no obligation to pay or to deliver for either party.
