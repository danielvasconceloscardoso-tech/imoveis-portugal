# Sponsorship agreement

**Between:**

**[Firma, Lda]**, a Portuguese private limited company (sociedade por quotas), with registered office at [Sede], company and registration number [NIPC], share capital [...] €, represented by its [Gerência], duly authorised, which operates the Imóveis Portugal platform (the "Platform");

**and**

**[Developer]**, with registered office at [...], company number [...], represented by [name and capacity of the Developer's representative], duly authorised (the "Developer"),

this sponsorship agreement is entered into on the following terms and on Annexes I and II, which form part of it.

## Recitals

A. The Platform operates Imóveis Portugal, a digital data and services platform for people who live outside Portugal and want to buy a home in Portugal, with six editions (Luxembourg, France, Switzerland, Brazil, the United States and Israel), an index per municipality, simulators, a map, guides and a weekly letter sent to users registered with double opt-in.

B. The Developer is developing the project identified in Annex I and wishes to sponsor its presentation on the Platform.

C. The Platform does not carry out real-estate brokerage (Portuguese Law 15/2013 of 8 February). Readers' requests about buying a home are handled by the qualified advisor of the partner agency, a licensed real-estate brokerage company (AMI licence no. [...]), acting in its own name and under its own licence.

## Clause 1 · Purpose

1.1 The Developer sponsors the presentation of the project on the Platform and pays for it a flat fee for the placement and the services described in Clause 2 and Annex II.

1.2 This agreement is an advertising and services agreement. The Developer is the advertiser within the meaning of article 5(a) of the Portuguese Advertising Code (Código da Publicidade, Decree-Law 330/90 of 23 October). The Platform does not promote or negotiate the sale of any unit, does not represent the Developer or the buyer, and receives no payment linked to a sale.

## Clause 2 · What the Platform delivers

2.1 During the term, the Platform provides the Developer with:

1. **the pin** of the project on the Platform's map of Portugal, in all six editions;
2. **the card** of the project in the Concierge and in search results, when the place the reader looks for matches the municipality or its area;
3. **the project page**, with photographs, typologies, the availability of each block, the price or the words "price on request", and the assessment panel (clause 2.3);
4. **the request flow:** the "Request a private visit" button and its form, which pass each request to the qualified advisor of the partner agency, with the typology, budget, means of payment and timeline the reader gave;
5. **the weekly letter** of the six editions: [number] appearances during the term, in the editions and on the dates set in Annex II, and the project launch (a short video, stories on the Platform's social accounts, a feature in the letter and the page);
6. **the sales-team view:** a private, unindexed page where the Developer's sales team sees the project as the reader sees it, the availability table it supplied and the month's activity figures, with no personal data;
7. **the monthly report**, delivered within the first ten working days of the following month, with the content described in Annex II.

2.2 The Platform will provide the services with due care. It does not guarantee any number of visits, requests, contacts or sales. Audience figures it reports are those measured by its own tools and always state the source and the period.

2.3 The project is assessed against the criteria published by the Platform (Annex II). Each criterion is marked met, not met or "to be confirmed", with the date checked. The dated badge is awarded only when every criterion is met.

## Clause 3 · Price and payment

3.1 The price is a flat fee per unit listed, according to the unit's price band, as set out in Annex I, for 12 months from the go-live date or until the unit is sold, whichever comes first. VAT is added at the legal rate.

3.2 The price is paid upfront and in full within [8] days of the invoice, which the Platform issues on the date of signature. Go-live follows payment.

3.3 No part of the price depends on requests, visits, contacts, reservations or sales. The Platform charges no fee of any kind per request, per contact, by commission or on success. A unit sold before the 12 months end is no longer shown, with no refund and no further charge.

3.4 The Developer may list further units by written addendum, at the price per unit that applies to it on that date.

3.5 **Founding Partner.** Where Annex I states the founding price, it is the list price less 40% in year 1, and it is kept for year 2 on renewal.

3.6 **Step-up.** List prices rise 50% when the six editions pass 10,000 subscribers combined. The step-up does not apply to the current term or to a Founding Partner's year 2. Other renewals use the rate card in force on the renewal date.

3.7 Late payment entitles the Platform to default interest at the legal rate for commercial transactions and to suspend its services until payment.

## Clause 4 · Labelling of advertising

4.1 All content paid for by the Developer is identified as advertising, unmistakably and in every medium, as required by article 8(1) of the Portuguese Advertising Code: on the Platform with the label "Sponsored" (or its equivalent in the edition's language), in the weekly letter with "Sponsored" next to the content, on social networks with each network's paid-partnership tool and, in French captions, the words "collaboration commerciale". The Developer may not ask for the label to be removed or toned down.

4.2 The Developer warrants that the information, prices, areas, images and documents it supplies are accurate, current and capable of proof (article 10 of the Advertising Code), and contain no promise of yield or appreciation. The Developer approves each piece of content in writing before publication.

4.3 The Developer is liable to the Platform for any loss resulting from inaccurate information or from material for which it does not hold the rights, including where joint liability arises under article 30 of the Advertising Code.

## Clause 5 · Independence

5.1 The index per municipality, the Portugal 100 ranking, the method, the scores, the simulators, the guides and the assessment of the project are never affected by the sponsorship. The Developer may not review, change or see in advance any of them.

5.2 The Platform may publish data about the project's municipality, including unfavourable data, without this being a breach.

5.3 The assessment uses the word "assessed". The Platform never presents the project as "recommended" or "the best".

## Clause 6 · Data protection

6.1 Each party complies with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Portuguese Law 58/2019 of 8 August, and is responsible for the processing it carries out.

6.2 **Routing in force.** A reader who asks for a visit or for information gives consent naming the recipient, and the request is sent only to the qualified advisor of the partner agency, who handles the buyer and the contact with the Developer. The Platform sends the Developer no personal data of readers.

6.3 **Direct routing to the Developer.** It can be enabled only by written addendum. In that case the Developer receives a request only after the reader has given specific, informed consent naming the Developer. The Developer then becomes an independent controller of that data, uses it only to answer the request, does not add it to marketing lists without the data subject's own consent, and does not pass it to third parties.

6.4 The monthly report and the sales-team view contain only aggregate figures and qualified summaries of requests (typology, budget band, means of payment, timeline, edition), with no name, contact detail or other element that identifies the reader.

6.5 The Platform processes the contact details of the Developer's representatives and sales team only to perform this agreement.

## Clause 7 · Exclusivity

7.1 This agreement grants no exclusivity, unless Annex I states it and the 50% supplement is paid.

7.2 With exclusivity, the Platform presents no other sponsored project in the same municipality during the term. Exclusivity does not extend to the index, the data or the guides about the municipality.

## Clause 8 · Materials and rights

8.1 The Developer supplies the materials on the onboarding checklist (photographs with usage rights, the availability table or an automatic feed, the sales contact and languages, the documents for the assessment, the logo) within 48 hours of signature.

8.2 The Developer grants the Platform a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use those materials on the Platform, in the weekly letter, on the six editions' social accounts and in the monthly report, during the term. Letters and posts already sent or published may remain in the archives.

8.3 The Developer keeps availability current and reports each sale or reservation within two working days.

8.4 The Platform may refuse or withdraw material that breaks the law, third parties' rights or its content rules (sources, no promises of yield).

## Clause 9 · Term and renewal

9.1 The agreement takes effect on signature. The term of each unit runs from the go-live date in Annex I and ends after 12 months or on the sale of the unit, whichever comes first. The agreement ends when the term of the last listed unit ends.

9.2 Renewal is by written agreement, on the terms of Clause 3.

9.3 Where Annex I states the 90-day pilot, the pilot renews for 12 months at list price less 40%, unless either party gives written notice at least [15] days before the pilot ends.

## Clause 10 · Termination

10.1 The agreement may end by written agreement.

10.2 Either party may terminate for a material breach by the other that is not remedied within 15 days of written notice.

10.3 If termination is due to the Platform's breach, the Platform refunds the part of the price for the period not delivered. If it is due to the Developer's breach, or the Developer withdraws, the price paid is not refunded.

10.4 The Platform may immediately suspend any content that proves inaccurate or unlawful until it is corrected.

## Clause 11 · Liability

Each party's liability for loss arising from this agreement is limited to the amount paid under it, except in case of wilful misconduct or gross negligence. Neither party is liable for loss of profit or for sales not made.

## Clause 12 · Confidentiality and references

12.1 The terms of this agreement and the reports are confidential, save where the law requires otherwise.

12.2 The Platform shows the Developer as a partner on the "Partners" page or in other material only after recording its written consent, with the date and the document.

## Clause 13 · Notices

Notices are given in writing to the email addresses stated in Annex I.

## Clause 14 · Governing law and jurisdiction

This agreement is governed by Portuguese law. The courts of the district (comarca) of [Sede] have jurisdiction over any dispute, to the exclusion of any other.

## Clause 15 · Final provisions

15.1 This agreement and its annexes are the entire agreement between the parties on its subject. Any change must be in writing.

15.2 The agreement may be signed with a qualified electronic signature. Where a Portuguese version also exists, the Portuguese version prevails.

## Annex II · Specification of the services

**Monthly report.** Reach per edition (letter subscribers, sends, opens, site visits), views of the project page, the pin and the card, requests per edition with their qualified summaries and no personal data, and the municipality's index card. Every figure states its source and period; where there is no data yet, the report says "no data yet".

**Assessment criteria (version 1, published).** Visits by video call; a sales contact who answers in the buyer's language (Portuguese and at least English or French); availability published and kept current; the documents a buyer's lawyer needs available on request (licence, energy certificate, plans); a process that works by power of attorney, from reservation to deed; prices, or "on request" with a written reply within a stated time; no per-request fee anywhere in the relationship.

**Onboarding checklist.** See the document "Developer onboarding checklist", which forms part of this annex.
