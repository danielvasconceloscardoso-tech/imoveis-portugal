# Developer onboarding checklist

What we need within 48 hours of signature, and what goes live on day 1, day 3 and day 7. Send everything to [email da Firma], with the project name in the subject line.

## Within 48 hours: what the developer sends

### 1. Photographs, with usage rights

- <span class="box"></span>8 to 15 high-resolution images (exterior, interiors, views, surroundings), at least 2000 px on the long side.
- <span class="box"></span>Written confirmation that the developer holds the rights and allows their use on the platform, in the weekly letter, on the six editions' social accounts and in the monthly report (agreement, clause 8).
- <span class="box"></span>The credit to show with each image (photographer or render studio).
- <span class="box"></span>Which images are renders, so they carry the words "illustrative image".

### 2. Availability table, or an automatic feed

- <span class="box"></span>One row per unit: block, reference, typology, area, floor, price or "on request", status (available, reserved, sold).
- <span class="box"></span>Format: a spreadsheet (XLSX or CSV) or an address serving the data as JSON, XML or CSV that the platform reads every day.
- <span class="box"></span>Who updates it and how we are told: each sale or reservation reported within two working days.

### 3. Sales contact and languages

- <span class="box"></span>A sales-team email address (preferably a shared one, such as sales@) and a phone number.
- <span class="box"></span>The languages the team answers in (Portuguese and at least English or French) and its response time.
- <span class="box"></span>The time slots for visits by video call.
- <span class="box"></span>Who approves each piece of content in writing before publication.

### 4. Documents for the assessment criteria

- <span class="box"></span>Building permit or occupancy licence.
- <span class="box"></span>Energy certificate (or pre-certificate).
- <span class="box"></span>Floor plans of the typologies listed.
- <span class="box"></span>A description of the power-of-attorney process, from reservation to deed.
- <span class="box"></span>Confirmation of visits by video call and of the time for a written reply to price requests.
- <span class="box"></span>Confirmation that there is no per-request fee anywhere in the relationship.

The documents are used only to check the published criteria. Each criterion is marked met, not met or "to be confirmed", with the date.

### 5. Logo

- <span class="box"></span>SVG, or PNG with a transparent background, in positive and negative versions.
- <span class="box"></span>Brand guidelines, if any.

### 6. Invoicing

- <span class="box"></span>Company name, tax number, registered office and billing email. Payment is upfront, on invoice; go-live follows payment.

### 7. Optional: reference on the "Partners" page

- <span class="box"></span>Written consent for the developer to appear as a partner. Without a recorded consent, the name does not appear.

## Day 1: live

1. The project's pin on the map, in all six editions, labelled "Sponsored".
2. The project page, with photographs, typologies and the availability of each block.
3. The card in the Concierge and in search, for the municipality and its area.
4. The "Request a private visit" button, with requests passed to the qualified advisor of the partner agency.
5. The link to the sales-team view, sent to the sales contact.

## Day 3

1. The assessment panel published, with each criterion and the date checked.
2. The project page in the languages of the six editions (Portuguese, French, English), after the developer's approval.
3. The texts for the weekly letter and the launch sent to the developer for written approval.

## Day 7

1. The project in the weekly letter of the six editions (the letter goes out on Thursday), labelled "Sponsored".
2. The launch on the six editions' social accounts: a short video and stories, with each network's paid-partnership tool.
3. The dated badge, if every criterion is met.
4. The first availability check with the sales team.

Before the platform launches on 1 December 2026, the day-7 letter and social appearances move to the first week after launch.

## After that

The monthly report arrives within the first ten working days of each month. Availability is updated whenever it changes. Each unit stops being shown when it is sold or after 12 months.
