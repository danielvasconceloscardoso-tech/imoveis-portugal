# Outreach v3: three touches per segment and language (prompt 15 §2)

Drafts only. Nothing is sent before Daniel confirms the legal grounds for B2B email per country (task 8p) and the operator says "go". Limits: at most 30 emails a day from the brand mailbox, plain text, one-to-one, an opt-out line in every email. Log every touch in `sponsors/pipeline.csv` (touch1_date, touch2_date, touch3_date, reply, call_date, outcome); `/status` reads them.

Order: (a) warm, via the partner group; (b) tier A from pipeline.csv (Private tier first, then the banks added in prompt 13); (c) bilateral chambers and diaspora associations (sponsors/INTERNATIONAL.md).

Rhythm: **day 0** email · **day 3** LinkedIn connection note (sent by hand by the operator, no pitch) · **day 7** short follow-up with the founding offer and the 1 December deadline. One ask only: a 15-minute call.

Placeholders: `[Nome]` / `[Name]` / `[Nom]` (found on LinkedIn at the moment of sending, never stored), `[Empresa]`, `[evidência]` (from the evidence column), `[Assinatura]` (brand signature, no personal name).
