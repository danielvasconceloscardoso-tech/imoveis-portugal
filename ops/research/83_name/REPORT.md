# Prompt 83: a .com for Maré. Report to the operator (12 Nov 2026)

Research only. Nothing was bought, registered or created. No trademark office, TMview, WIPO or INPI page was opened by anyone. Four agents: namer (80 candidates), domain checker, trademark and handle checker, independent fact-checker. Their notes are beside this file.

How to read "free": the .com registry answered "no such domain" twice, an hour apart (80 names checked; 40 free, 40 taken, 8 of the taken ones for sale). That is "probably free": only a registrar's price on the day confirms it.

## 2. Existing "Maré" brands that matter

1. **mare** (Germany): a magazine and a book publisher under the bare word. If it holds an EU mark for publications, that covers Portugal, France and Luxembourg. The main open question.
2. **MEO Marés** (Portugal): the festival formerly MEO Marés Vivas, renamed in February 2026. Events sit in the same class as publishing.
3. **Maré, by Grupo Marquise** (Brazil): a property line announced in December 2025, first project in development. Real estate under the bare word, in one of our markets.
4. **Mid Atlantic Real Estate Journal, "MAREJ"** (US), at marejournal.com: real-estate trade press. Close to any "mare + press word" name.
5. **Maré Viva** (Espinho) and **Maré de Notícias** (Rio): press titles using the word.

The bare word is common and weak: many users, so hard for anyone to stop us, and hard for us to protect. The handle `mare` is already taken on X and TikTok, so the handle will have to match the longer domain.

## 3. What only you can check, by hand (about 30 minutes)

1. **TMview** (https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/, free, no account). Offices: EUIPO, Portugal, France, Benelux, Switzerland, UK, Brazil, US, WIPO. Classes 16, 35, 36, 41. Status: filed and registered only.
   - First the bare word, exact: MARE, MARÉ, MAREE, MARÉS, MAREA.
   - Then "begins with MARE" in class 41, then 16, 35, 36.
   - Then the names: MARE DE PORTUGAL / MAREDEPORTUGAL; PORTUGAL MARE / PORTUGALMARE; MAREVOLUME / MARE VOLUME; MARE PORTUGUESA / MAREPORTUGUESA; MARELETTER / MARE LETTER.
   - Worry about: a live word mark, identical or one letter away, in one of those classes and territories. Do not worry about: dead marks, logos where "mare" just means sea, hotels and restaurants.
2. **INPI Portugal** marks search: MARÉ, MARE, MARÉS with "contains", then the names. The address we hold (https://servicosonline.inpi.pt/pesquisas/main/marcas.jsp) was not opened; if it fails, start from inpi.justica.gov.pt, "Pesquisas", "Marcas".
3. **.pt**: the registry's own search, for the name you choose. From here we can only see that none of the free names is in use.
4. **Handles**: instagram.com/<name>/ and linkedin.com/company/<name>/ for the name you choose; all four networks for mareportuguesa.
5. **Price**: the registrar's price for the name on the day. A normal .com is about 10 € a year; a "premium" price means walk away.
6. In Portugal a periodical's title is also registered with the media regulator. A separate check, for when Maré is published under its own name.

The full step-by-step, with a term table for all 15 shortlisted names, is in trademark.md, section 1.

## 4. What to buy first, and the cost

One domain, about 10 € a year: maredeportugal.com, after the five-minute bare-word search in TMview. portugalmare.com for another 10 € would keep the nearest alternative out of other hands. The trademark waits until there is a company or a first sponsor. Both decisions are yours.

## What the fact-checker corrected

1. 20 of the 80 candidates work in one language only, so they miss the "easy in three languages" rule; three of them were in the namer's first five free names. The ranking above takes that into account.
2. The .pt column proves nothing beyond "not in use". One note said the registry "publishes no lookup service"; that was not established.
3. Handles were checked for the bare word and eight names only.
4. For the .pt question the domain checker tried one lookup address of the .pt registry and its WHOIS port. Both attempts failed inside our own network and reached nobody. The prompt asked for that check; it is disclosed here because the first note left it out.
5. MEO Marés: 120,000 is the capacity over three days, not last year's attendance. The Marquise "Maré" line is announced, not launched.

Could not be checked by anyone: any trademark register, real .pt availability, Instagram and LinkedIn, and whether a free .com sells at the normal price.
