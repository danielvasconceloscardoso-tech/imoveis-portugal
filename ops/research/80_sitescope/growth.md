## 2. Paid or organic?

**Leaning organic or a favour (low confidence).** The evidence on each side:

- **Points to organic:**
  - Sitescope's own post (reshared on the CEO's LinkedIn) presents it as "a short video with Robert, Albert and Jakub about what we're building at Sitescope", which reads as a joint piece, not an ad buy [G11].
  - The company is listed as unfunded with about 5 employees (Tracxn, July 2026) [G12]. A cash budget for creators is unlikely, though not impossible.
  - Featuring local startups fits his scout/investor/"startup house" persona: the content doubles as deal flow and community building [G3][G6].
- **Points to paid:**
  - He does commercial brand work (the brand list on his site) [G1].
  - A small Warsaw startup is exactly the kind of client that pays a micro-creator a few hundred euros.
- **What would settle it:**
  - Is there a "Paid partnership with sitescope.ai" label under his handle, or #reklama / #współpracareklamowa / #ad in the caption? I could not see the post: **UNCONFIRMED**. The operator can check this on a phone in 10 seconds.
  - Polish law makes the label more telling. The Polish consumer authority, UOKiK, recommends two layers of disclosure for any paid or in-kind collaboration: a label in the post itself and the platform's paid-partnership tool, with the brand named [G13][G14]. Meta's branded-content rules also require the label when there is any compensation [G15].
  - So if the reel has no label, it is either organic or a non-compliant paid post. A missing label does not prove it was organic.

## 3. Why the reel went viral

Figures:
- Operator: ~10.9K likes, 782 comments, 9.6K shares.
- Company, self-reported, under 24 h: 200,000+ views, 370+ comments, 4,000+ shares, 5,500+ saves [G11].

Whether the company's figures are the same reel at an earlier point is UNCONFIRMED. The company file treats them as probably the same [company.md].

Ratios:
- Shares to likes = **0.88**.
- Comments to likes = 7.2%.
- In the 24 h snapshot: shares to views ≈ 2.0% and saves to views ≈ 2.75%.

No official benchmark for shares/likes exists [G26]. On most Reels, likes far outnumber shares, so near 1:1 is exceptional.

The mechanics:
1. **Sends drive reach.** Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, calls "sends per reach" (how many viewers sent the post to a friend in a DM) one of the most important ranking signals, especially for reaching people who don't follow the account [G27][G28]. A reel shared almost as often as it is liked gets pushed to non-followers, and every wave produces more sends. Shares on Reels grew 67% year on year against 17% for likes in Metricool's 24M-post study [G29], so the platform keeps rewarding this.
2. **It is useful to a third person.** A buildability check on a plot is something almost everyone in Poland knows someone needing (a parent with a działka, a friend building a house, an architect). The natural reaction is "send to X", not "like". The 5,500+ saves (more than the shares in the first 24 h) point the same way: people bookmark a tool to use later [G11].
3. **National pride.** The "polish startup" label turns a B2B product into "one of ours". Polish viewers share it as a point of pride, which reaches people who will never buy it.
4. **The hook is a thesis.** "Startups like this are the foundational layer…" is the vocabulary of the AI-stack debate ("foundation layer" versus apps) [G30]. It flatters the viewer as someone who understands where value sits, and it invites agreement or disagreement in the comments. 782 comments on 10.9K likes is high. What the comments actually say ("link?", "does it work in my gmina?", sceptics) is **UNCONFIRMED**; I could not see them.
5. **It feels real.**
   - A real office and a real map on a TV, with a hand pointing: the product is shown, not pitched, with no slides and no stock footage.
   - Word-by-word captions let people watch with the sound off and keep the eye on the screen.
6. **Length: UNCONFIRMED.** Office-visit reels of this kind usually run 20–45 s. I could not measure this one.

**Lesson in the numbers:** 200K+ views, yet the brand account has about 2,300 followers and 3 posts (per the operator; not verified by search). The **creator kept most of the audience**. A reel only works for us if it sends people to one owned destination: the newsletter signup.

## 6. How we would copy the format (20–30 s, faceless, anonymous)

**Setup.**
- Our 3D map and purchase-cost simulator, on a TV or large monitor in a neutral room.
- Film the screen with a phone; a hand and pointer are the only "person".
- Anonymity checklist:
  - No rings, tattoos or watches on the hand.
  - No voice: captions plus music, or a neutral synthetic voice.
  - The screen shows no OS username, browser tabs, bookmarks or email.
  - No location metadata in the file.
- Alternative: a clean screen recording composited onto a TV mock-up. A real TV films as more authentic.

**Shot list (about 26 s).**

| t | Shot | On-screen caption (word-by-word) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Tight on a hand pointing at the 3D map, which is already flying into Lisbon/Porto (motion in frame 1) | Hook (below) |
| 2–6 s | Tap a municipality; development pins appear | "Every development. One map." |
| 6–11 s | Cut to the simulator: type a price (e.g. 300 000 €), choose buyer status "non-resident" | "Price on the listing: 300 000 €" |
| 11–17 s | Lines fill in one by one: IMT, stamp duty, notary/registry, bank costs; the total counts up | "What you actually pay: [simulator total]" |
| 17–22 s | Switch the edition flag (LU → FR → CH) to show it works for each country | "From Luxembourg, France, Switzerland…" |
| 22–26 s | Brand end card: newsletter signup URL / "link in bio" | "Free weekly guide. Link in bio." plus a small disclaimer: "Informação, não aconselhamento." |

- The figures must be **whatever our sourced simulator outputs** on the day of filming. No hand-typed numbers (D12: every fact traceable).
- Sources go in the post caption.

**Hook options** (one per variant; test 2–3):
- PT: "Ferramentas como esta são o que faltava a quem compra casa em Portugal a partir do estrangeiro." (direct echo of "Startups like this are…")
- PT: "300 000 € de casa em Portugal. Quanto pagas a mais? Vê."
- FR: "Acheter au Portugal depuis la France : le prix affiché n'est pas le prix final."
- PT-BR: "Comprando imóvel em Portugal do Brasil? O preço do anúncio não é o que você paga."
- EN (USA/IL): "Buying in Portugal from abroad? The listing price isn't the price."
- Banned: "paraíso", "oportunidade única", any return or price-rise promise.

**Captions.**
- Big word-by-word text, centre-safe area (clear of Instagram UI at top and bottom), brand colours.
- One language per edition (CH gets PT and FR versions).

**Post caption template:**

> 1 line restating the hook
> 3 bullets of what the map and simulator show
> "Envia a quem está a pensar comprar casa em Portugal."
> Sources: [AT/IMT, etc.]
> Information, not advice
> #publicidade, if sponsored

- The explicit "send to…" line drives sends per reach, the signal that made sitescope's reel travel.
- Optional: on-screen labels "Feito em Portugal" / "Portuguese tool" to borrow the national-pride mechanic of "polish startup".

**Distribution.**
- Export to the weekly Meta Business Suite file for operator approval (rule 2). Never auto-post.
- Point the link in bio at the edition's newsletter signup, so the reach turns into subscribers, not followers we don't own.
- Use a comment-keyword DM automation only if a free tool and operator approval allow it; otherwise use the link in bio.
