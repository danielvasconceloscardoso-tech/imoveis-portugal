# Prompt 80: what Imóveis Portugal learns from sitescope.ai (recommendation)

Strategist, 8 Nov 2026, from the four research files in this folder. Links are copied from them; UNCONFIRMED marks are kept; my estimates say "estimate". Nothing is built. Independently fact-checked on 8 Nov 2026 (table at the end); corrections are in the text.

## 1. What sitescope is

- A Warsaw company registered on 7 Aug 2025 with 6,000 PLN of share capital ([company.md, Compabase](https://compabase.com/companies/krs/0001187643)). Tracxn lists it as unfunded with about 5 staff (UNCONFIRMED, [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/sitescope/__nlAkx7kcR0c2ZQqdpu0vPqPax3eIDaxz30nE8ICgB8A)).
- It sells a Polish plot report with 36 sections (zoning plan, land register, risks, prices) in the browser and as a PDF. An "Ask AI about a plot" Studio (offered by demo) and an API ("in development") are announced, not self-serve. Development capacity is "coming soon" ([company.md, reports.sitescope.ai](https://reports.sitescope.ai/)).
- No public price list (Reports has a checkout step whose price was not seen; Studio and API go through "Book a demo"), and no named customers or press found; its only visible traction is one viral video (company.md).

## 2. What we learn

1. **Product: one question, answered on a map, with the source behind each answer.** The live product looks like GIS aggregation; the one section that would need plan reading (development capacity) is "coming soon", and the AI workspace (Studio) is shown by demo only ([company.md, reports.sitescope.ai](https://reports.sitescope.ai/)). The pitch is "what can be built" plus "return to the source documents" ([company.md, sitescope.ai](https://sitescope.ai/)). *Lesson:* our map and simulator already answer one question ("what will I really pay"); put the source beside each figure; no AI claim needed.
2. **Content: the creator kept the audience.** The company reports (self-reported, not independently verified) 200,000+ views, 4,000+ shares and 5,500+ saves in under 24 h ([growth.md, founder's LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/albert-bigoszewski-332b48106/)). The brand account still has about 2,300 followers and 3 posts (operator's observation, not verifiable by search, [BRIEF.md](/home/user/wt-p80/ops/research/80_sitescope/BRIEF.md)). On the operator's figures, shares were 0.88 of likes (9.6K / 10.9K, growth.md). Instagram's head names sends per reach as one of its top ranking signals, with watch time and likes ([growth.md, SEJ](https://searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389)). *Lesson:* make each reel worth sending to one person, and point it at the newsletter signup.
3. **Go-to-market: their B2B route needs a sales team.** "Book a demo", and an API planned for portals, banks, insurers and public administration ([company.md, sitescope.ai](https://sitescope.ai/)). The scaled comparables all sell professional seats: LandInsight at £2,500–£6,000 per licence per year ([market.md, G-Cloud](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud/services/595409603965228)), TestFit Site Solver from $15,000/yr since Aug 2026, up from $10,000 ([TestFit](https://www.testfit.io/testfit); [PricingSaaS change log](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/testfit/diffs/2026W33)). None of the comparables found earns from private owners (market.md, §1c pattern). *Lesson:* for three part-time founders, the money is in the audience the tools need, not in the tool.
4. **Market: in Portugal the tool already exists, and it is cheap.**
   - **Parci** (parci.eu) claims 300 of 308 municipalities ([market.md, Parci](https://parci.eu/)). Its plans are €29/€149/€499 (entry, recurring use, teams; billing period not seen), plus a €49 one-off report without subscription (vendor's own pages: [Parci blog](https://parci.eu/blog/promotores-ia-analisar-terrenos-parci); [Parci](https://parci.eu/)). No funding was found.
   - **Litehaus** (score.litehaus.co) publishes "Índices PDM" pages per municipality, Gondomar included, and sells land reports from 29 € + VAT per parcel ([data.md, Litehaus](https://score.litehaus.co/land/pt/gondomar/indices-pdm); [Litehaus](https://www.litehaus.co/pt/)). On 29 May 2025 ECO reported a round of 1.5 M€ (headline; the round is reported elsewhere as a 1.46 M€ pre-seed) ([ECO](https://eco.sapo.pt/2025/05/29/litehaus-levanta-15-milhoes-para-acelerar-construcao-de-casas/); [idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/news/node/70017)). Litehaus describes itself as a home-building platform (3D-printed and modular homes); the land reports are a separate product line.
   - **ATLAS** by Segmento Urbano (an architecture practice) was presented at SIL, 23–25 April 2026 ([market.md, idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/news/node/75042)).
   - Others exist: foral.ai says it covers all 308 municipalities (UNCONFIRMED), and market.md lists IPR Atlas (UNCONFIRMED).
   - **Same or different?** Different: other names, domains, products and backers, and no file links them. Parci is subscription SaaS; Litehaus is a funded home-building platform. The shared 29 € entry looks like a market price. Any ownership link: UNCONFIRMED.
   - *Meaning:* building would make us at least the fourth tool in a €29–49 market. They are flat-fee **sponsors** to sell to. None appears to be an estate agency (UNCONFIRMED), so D9 should not apply.
5. **Demand: the emigrant land question is real and has no owner in the media.** 506,451 citizens on the mainland had identified properties in BUPi by 16 Apr 2026 ([market.md, Diário de Viseu](https://www.diarioviseu.pt/2026/04/18/bupi-com-42-de-area-identificada/)). Free registration ended on 30 Sep 2026; since 1 Oct each georeferenced plot (RGG) costs €15 up to the ninth and €10 from the tenth ([market.md, idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/news/imobiliario/habitacao/2026/09/30/77929-registo-gratuito-de-terrenos-no-bupi-termina-e-passa-a-custar-15-euros); [DN Madeira](https://www.dnoticias.pt/2026/9/30/507248-madeira-tem-37-dos-terrenos-identificados-no-bupi/)). About 1.8M Portuguese-born people live abroad ([market.md, Jornal de Negócios](https://www.jornaldenegocios.pt/economia/mundo/detalhe/numero-de-emigrantes-portugueses-revisto-em-baixa-sao-18-milhoes-e-nao-21-milhoes)). Forums ask what inherited family land is worth and whether it can be built on ([expat.com FR](https://www.expat.com/fr/forum/europe/portugal/812018-connaitre-la-valeur-dun-terrain-au-portugal-svp.html); a Fórum da Casa thread cited by market.md could not be checked: UNCONFIRMED, [Fórum da Casa](https://forumdacasa.com/discussion/6572/predio-rustico/)). How many emigrants hold inherited land is UNCONFIRMED.

## 4. What we build, if anything: three steps

**Step 1: Gondomar only (golden sample, rule 17).**
- On the Gondomar place card: click a point and see its soil class and category. The data is DGT's CRUS, CC BY 4.0 ([data.md, dados.gov.pt](https://dados.gov.pt/en/datasets/carta-do-regime-de-uso-do-solo-portugal-continental-1)), downloaded once by hand and cached, with no live calls to government servers. The `crus_gondomar` collection is UNCONFIRMED.
- Flags for REN, Natura 2000 and heritage zones, and for the two 2023 preventive-measure areas ([data.md, Aviso 13612/2023](https://www.cm-gondomar.pt/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/2023.07.17-Aviso-DR-Suspensao-Parcial-do-Plano-Diretor-Municipal-de-Gondomar.pdf)).
- Links to the articles of the regulation in force, Aviso 3337/2018 ([DRE](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/aviso/3337-2018-114859410); [municipal PDF](https://www.cm-gondomar.pt/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/PDM_Gondomar_2018.pdf)), and the "last checked" date.
- The data.md disclaimer (only a PIP or a certidão binds the câmara); never "you can build X m²". About 6–8 days (data.md, estimate).
- Free or paid: the operator decides in that prompt (rule 17 defaults to paid). I recommend free: competitors charge from 29 € + VAT for more.

**Step 2: sponsors (flat fees only, D6), then public bodies as media buyers.**
- Sell a "Terreno de família" section (article, newsletter slot, the Gondomar layer as the demo) to one architecture practice and one tool (Parci, Litehaus or ATLAS) as flat-fee sponsors. Developer campaigns start at 1,500 € (CLAUDE.md).
- Partner rather than build: a vendor could supply its indices, credited, so we never maintain 308 regulations.
- Public bodies: BUPi has run emigrant campaigns on general-interest TV and local press (Easter 2023, [Justiça](https://justica.gov.pt/Noticias/Campanha-BUPi-para-os-emigrantes-chega-a-televisao)), and buys its media through SGMJ procurement; the TED notice cited covers the summer and Christmas 2023 campaigns aimed at residents in Portugal, not emigrants ([TED](https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/687385-2023/pdf)). The ~€140k figure is UNCONFIRMED. Programa Regressar has supported almost 37,000 people, about 17,000 applicants plus 20,000 family members ([idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/news/node/71353)).
- The fit is a **campaign placement** (a media buy), not a software licence, which means tenders and a sales team.
- At the 9 Nov Regressar meeting (prompt 79): listen for the land question, pitch nothing.

**Step 3: exit value.**
- A buyer (portal, agency network, media group) pays for subscribers per edition, recurring sponsor contracts and consented data signals.
- A zoning layer is copyable (at least three Portuguese tools have one). It adds exit value only as a proven sponsor category and an emigrant-land segment, counted in aggregate (judgement).

## 7. What we do not do

- **No nationwide build**, and no buildable-m² claims. There is no national dataset of indices, so they must be extracted from PDF municipality by municipality ([data.md](https://dados.gov.pt/en/datasets/carta-do-regime-de-uso-do-solo-portugal-continental-1)).
- **No paid creator before a sponsor pays** (rule 5). No founder on camera (rule 1).
- **No per-report or per-lead fees** from tools or architects (D6). Buyer requests still go only to the partner agency (D14).
- **No scraping** of BUPi geometry or scripted calls to government servers (data.md, BRIEF).
- **No contact with sitescope**: Poland only (company.md), though reportedly at Web Summit Lisbon, 9–12 Nov, with the Warsaw delegation (attendance UNCONFIRMED: LinkedIn snippets only, growth.md; dates: [HaDEA](https://hadea.ec.europa.eu/events/web-summit-2026-2026-11-09_en)).

## 8. Risks

- **Legal: a zoning class can be suspended or out of date.** CRUS on dados.gov.pt was last updated 21 Sep 2023 ([dados.gov.pt](https://dados.gov.pt/en/datasets/carta-do-regime-de-uso-do-solo-portugal-continental-1)). Urbanisable-land norms can lapse where a PDM was not adapted, and Gondomar's 2nd revision is in progress ([data.md, CM Gondomar](https://www.cm-gondomar.pt/atividade-municipal/planeamento-e-ordenamento/plano-diretor-municipal/)).
- **Licences:** CRUS is CC BY 4.0, so DGT must be credited (exact credit wording to check on the listing). The terms for ICNF, Património Cultural and APA data are UNCONFIRMED (data.md).
- **Competitors:** the Portuguese tools could start content of their own and compete for the same audience.
- **Evidence:** sitescope's figures are self-reported; whether the reel was paid is UNCONFIRMED (growth.md).
- **Anonymity and brand:** a filmed reel can leak a username or location (growth.md checklist); no price-rise story (banned returns promise).
