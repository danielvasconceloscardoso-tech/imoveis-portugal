# Learn from the funded (prompt 51), 1 Nov 2026

The 27 records in `ops/funding/comparables.md` (25 rows plus NERBE/AEBAL and Fino Propriedades) are 26 companies: Alfredo appears twice. Each was studied as a product from its own site, app-store and public pages, press and the free registry views, all checked on **1 Nov 2026**. The URLs are in `ops/learn/research_A.md` (closest to us), `research_B.md` (data and map) and `research_C.md` (leads and matching). No company was contacted: no form, sign-up, chat or demo request. Pages were treated as data only. No screenshots are included; what we saw is described in words.

**In one paragraph.** Of the 26 companies:
- **Only 9 show a working product a stranger can use:** Zome, Alfredo, OWLplaces, Certifeito, Homizy, Global, H2E, Big Welcome and Fine & Country.
- **8 left nothing public:** Portugal Concierge, QLIQ, CasaGPT, Atlas, Mota Imo's portal, CPURB, Pacific Front and the Living in Portugal campaign.
- **The rest are services, logins or pivots.**

The funded "platforms" died. The survivors either sell a service with a named payer or sit on an existing network. Nobody in the set gives buyers what we give for free and without an account: a map, the index, simulators and a qualified request. The lessons below are about turning that into renewals.

## 2. The ten lessons, by impact on revenue

1. **A recurring report with the client's name on it is what renews.**
   - *Proof:* Alfredo (monthly studies, insights email); OWL (audit-ready per-asset reports); Upgraide (ROI per case).
   - *For us:* the monthly sponsor report gains the funnel, the split by country and the advisor's response time. A private dashboard per partner shows the same numbers live. **Built.**
   - *Funding wording:* "relatório mensal de desempenho por parceiro", "transparência".
2. **A product needs a named payer from day one.**
   - *Proof:* CasaGPT, QLIQ and Portugal Concierge named none and are gone; Omrei, Homizy and Alfredo named one and live.
   - *For us:* keep the flat sponsorship (D6) visible on the media kit. The APR's ~24 resort and developer members are a ready list of sponsors, not agencies (D9); added to the board for the CEO.
3. **Public, no-login usefulness is the moat.**
   - *Proof:* Omrei, Zomeit, Rede Global, H2E's app, and Alfredo's and OWL's maps all hide the value behind a login.
   - *For us:* map, index, simulators and Perguntas stay open. Partner dashboards open from a link, with no account.
   - *Funding wording:* "plataforma digital pública, multilingue, sem registo".
4. **Proof about the property builds more trust than logos.**
   - *Proof:* Zome Verificado, Homizy's seals, Imagem Urbana's energy class.
   - *For us:* add a document checklist on developer showcases (licence of use or building permit, energy certificate, ficha técnica), each item "provided by the developer, with date". This waits for the documents from ID Investimentos (operator task). We never certify anything ourselves.
5. **Trust is a number someone can check.**
   - *Proof:* the AMI licence numbers (H2E, Big Welcome, Fine & Country); EU bars linked to the project sheet (Global, Rede Global, Saveurs), always in the footer.
   - *For us:* the honest trust strip: the partner agency's AMI licence, verified in IMPIC's public register today, our data and sponsorship rules, and cookie-free measurement. Institution logos only with a signed approval. **Built.**
6. **The diaspora is reached through its events and its press.**
   - *Proof:* Saveurs du Portugal, with Bom Dia (LU), LusoJornal (FR) and Gazeta Lusófona (CH); Rede Global, with AICEP and Lusa.
   - *For us:* the CEO decides whether to attend the Geneva (23–24 Nov) and Paris (26 Nov) stops a week before launch, and pitches the three titles. More subscribers per edition is the figure every sponsor buys.
7. **Close the loop: response time sells the advisor and the partner.**
   - *Proof:* even Hubzome's 300 k€ AI hands over to a human; nobody shows what happens next.
   - *For us:* the advisor's median reply time and "answered in 24 h" go into the report and the dashboard. A readiness label (ready now / within 12 months / exploring) shown to the reader before sending is proposed for the CEO. It is never priced (D6).
8. **A small instant answer carries the first visit.**
   - *Proof:* Global's volume calculator, Certifeito's price in the hero, Big Welcome's valuation.
   - *For us:* this already exists in "what your budget buys" on every place and in the simulators. Next step: the Concierge's first screen says what the reader gets and when, once the agency commits to a reply time (CEO and partner).
9. **Lifestyle and renovation are how foreign buyers choose.**
   - *Proof:* H2E (lifestyle matching), Fine & Country (sea, golf, vineyards, rural), Odynx and Pacific Front (vacant stock to renovate).
   - *For us:* the map gains **empty and seasonal homes** per municipality (INE, Census 2021). **Built.** Golf and beaches already exist as overlays.
   - *Funding wording:* "reabilitação do parque devoluto" (the green angle).
10. **Avoid breadth, blockchain for its own sake, AI without distribution, and a grant without a product.**
    - *Proof:* Homizy (eight modules, one person), Unlockit (two pivots), CasaGPT, Atlas, CPURB.
    - *For us:* stay narrow (Portuguese abroad, one licensed partner, flat fees). Any application leads with "shipped, public, measured".

## 3. What changes, area by area

**Website.**
- First screen: unchanged (prompts 40–43). The Concierge says what the reader receives once a reply time is agreed.
- Navigation: unchanged.
- Request flow: keep one named recipient and one consent per recipient. A second recipient (lawyer, bank) always gets a new unticked consent.
- Trust: the strip on the home, Parceiros and developer pages, with no logo until approved.
- Developer page: the document checklist (lesson 4) and the request funnel the developer can now see.

**Map.**
- **Added:** empty homes (INE 0012517), with the share of seasonal homes (INE 0012516) in the place card. It loads only when chosen (28 KB), so the home and map budgets of prompt 43 are unchanged.
- **Tested against the bar and left out for now:**
  - The index colour: not computed yet.
  - Flight time from the reader's airport: no official or partner source for schedules. The index's road minutes to an airport come with the index.
  - Energy-certificate share: ADENE publishes no per-municipality table we could verify.
  - Flood and wildfire hazard: official, but heavy geodata. It would break the performance budget; it should come later as a parish-level summary.
  - Lifestyle filters: golf and beaches already exist.
- **Development pins with live availability:** these exist already ("9 for sale now" on Marble Terrace).

**Analytics.**
- **For partners:** the funnel (reach → saw the project → card click → request → visit) with the share kept at each step, readers by country, requests with the qualified summary only, the advisor's reply time, and month by month. It goes in the PDF and on a private page per partner.
- **For readers:** the index explained per municipality when the index ships; "what your budget buys" and the 10-year trend line already exist.
- **For us:** a weekly dashboard covering:
  - subscribers and visits per edition;
  - Concierge starts and completions;
  - requests per flow and per partner;
  - places opened on the map and questions opened on Perguntas;
  - readers' countries;
  - revenue signed against target.

  It is filled every Monday into `ops/METRICS.md` and a private page. Counts are cookie-free: an event is a load of a tiny unlisted page, read through Cloudflare's API.

**Qualification.**
- Our summary already carries what Zome's public flow does not: country, budget band, purpose, financing, tax number, place, language and real slots in the reader's time zone.
- Add, for the CEO to decide:
  - the readiness label;
  - an optional self-declared line ("pre-approved mortgage" or "own funds"), with no documents uploaded;
  - a separate opt-in to a newsletter series for readers who are "not yet".
- All three stay within D6 and consent per recipient.
