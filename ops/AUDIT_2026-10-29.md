## The ten changes that matter most

1. **Home: from ten blocks to five.** Today 10 blocks and 13 screens on a phone (Vanguard: 5 blocks; Claridge's opens on one image and one line). Keep the film and the bar ("Começar" opens the Concierge), the map, Passo a passo, the questions and the weekly letter; everything else goes to an article or out.
2. **One type scale.** We use 15–16 different text sizes on the home page and 11–14 elsewhere; Claridge's uses 7–10, Doutor Finanças 8–11. Six sizes for everything: 13, 15, 17, 21, 28, 40 (display 64 on desktop only, for the hero).
3. **One spacing scale.** 8, 16, 24, 40, 64, 104. Every gap on every page comes from it.
4. **The same navigation on phone and desktop.** Today the phone shows 4 header links plus a 6-tab bar (without Artigos) plus a menu; the desktop shows 13 links. Same items, same order, same words on both: Passo a passo · Mapa · Simuladores · Artigos · Perguntas · Parceiros, plus Concierge. One pattern per device, everywhere: inline on desktop, one menu sheet on the phone; the bottom tab bar goes.
5. **Perguntas: one line per question.** 2,495 words on one page today (Doutor Finanças hides answers behind the question). The answer opens in place, two or three sentences, then "Saiba mais" to the article.
6. **Passo a passo: one question, one action per screen.** The first block has 66 links today. Each station: a plain question, two or three positive sentences, "Saiba mais", and one partner link matched to the topic.
7. **Articles: the answer first, written for a foreigner.** Average 1,270 words, 15 words per sentence, Portuguese terms not always explained, some AI tells. Target phrase in title, H1, slug, description and first sentence; the answer in two sentences; H2s as the questions people search; FAQ data; 600–1,200 words.
8. **Sources: one line, opened on demand.** Every page ends with 100–400 words of sources. They stay (D12), folded into "Fontes (n)" that opens in place.
9. **Weight.** Home 2.6 MB on a phone, 1.9 MB of it the film (kept as it is); everything else must stay under 1.2 MB. The Parceiros page loads 2 MB of map code on desktop for one small embed: load it only when the reader touches it. JavaScript per page today 30–76 KB, within budget.
10. **Parceiros is two pages in one.** Readers get the partners who help them; the sponsor pitch (audience, packages, "before the launch") moves to the page for partners, linked once.
