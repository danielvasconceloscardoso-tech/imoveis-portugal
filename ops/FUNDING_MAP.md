Effort hours are our estimates. "Not stated" means the source gives no figure. Nothing was applied for; no application was drafted (§4 not run, by instruction).

**In one paragraph.** There is no large grant for us before Q2 2027, and possibly none in 2027. The Portugal 2030 lines that fit a platform (SICE Qualificação and Internacionalização) need at least 200,000 € of eligible spend, last closed in January 2025 and March 2026, and are not in the published call plan to August 2027 as individual calls; the PRR is in its closure phase. **Patterns.**

1. **The line that funded platforms like ours most is gone.** PRR C16-i02 (Vouchers para Startups, a flat 30,000 €; e-commerce internationalisation, 12,500–42,500 €) funded at least nine property and proptech start-ups in 2023–25, four of them in 2025 (rows 2–11). It is closed and the PRR is in its closure phase; no successor is named.
2. **Bigger money needs co-financing and R&D framing.** PRR "IA nas empresas" paid 16,000–300,000 € (about 74% for Hubzome); PT2030 R&D calls paid 64–75% on 300–480k €; SI Internacionalização pays about 40%, 50% in Norte, capped at 150,000 €. The index method is the only part of our work that could be framed as R&D.
3. **Diaspora and foreign-buyer marketing is funded through associations, at 80–85%** (APR, AEP's diaspora network three times, Saveurs du Portugal in FR/LU/CH). Our route there is the 2027 SICE joint-projects call as a participant with AEP or a chamber, not our own application.
4. **Small early money (2015 "vales", 75% on 20,000 €) has no 2021–27 successor in Norte.**
5. **The wording that wins:** "plataforma digital" or "SaaS" with a named technology (AI, big data, matching, geospatial data), "transparência do mercado imobiliário", named export markets (Frontfiles: Brazil, Argentina, Canada, Israel), "multilingue", and a green angle for vouchers (fewer trips, renovation, energy). Reviewers reward a live product with first users, named markets and measurable digital transition.
6. **The incumbents took no public money.** Casafari, Idealista, Imovirtual/OLX, Confidencial Imobiliário, Uniplaces and Homeit appear in none of the lists under those names (name matching only): the sector's winners were privately funded; public money goes to small, young firms.

## 4. Risks, line by line

**SME Fund.** It reimburses part of a fee we pay first; vouchers 2–4 are valid one month (plus one), so the filing must be ready when the voucher arrives, and the brand must be chosen by then. Budget is limited and may run out before 4 Dec. Whether it counts as state aid is not stated on the INPI page.

**Estágios +Talento.** We pay the intern in full each month and claim IEFP's share; 40% comes late. The window can close early "until the budget is reached", as the hiring measures did in October. An intern is not staff: we must provide a tutor and a real training plan, and IEFP can audit and recover the support if the internship ends early or the plan is not followed. 80% only if we then hire on an open-ended contract.

**Emprego +Talento / +Emprego.** The job must be net new and kept for 24 months, with no dismissals 3 months before; if the person leaves and is not replaced, or headcount falls, IEFP recovers the money. 20% arrives only after 24 months. It cannot be combined with Social Security exemptions for the same job. The 2026 money ran out in October; 2027 may run out faster. Partners and their spouses are excluded.

**Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs.** The money goes to the manager, not the company, and covers living costs abroad for up to six months away from the business at launch time. Matching a host can take months.

**BPF loans.** Debt with a personal and company repayment duty; the bank decides, and banks want accounts we do not have yet. The guarantee's grant equivalent counts as state aid.

**Portugal 2030 (any line, if it reopens).** Grants reimburse costs we pay first; only up to 10% comes as an advance. We must pay at least half of the project (70% for Inovação Produtiva) and hold 25% equity by the first payment. Costs incurred before the application are not eligible. Logos, the operation sheet and an A3 poster are mandatory whatever the size; results are audited for years after, with clawback if indicators (jobs, turnover, exports) are missed. Minimum spend of 200,000–300,000 € is out of reach before revenue.

**Startup status and tax lines.** No cash. SIFIDE and RFAI only reduce IRC when there is profit; SIFIDE is in force for 2026 only. The startup route by venture-capital round is closed to real-estate intermediaries, so any wording of our object as property brokerage would cost the status.

**Equity (Portugal Ventures, 28DIGITAL investment).** Dilution, investor rights over an exit within 12 months, and long processes for small tickets.

**De minimis (Regulation (EU) 2023/2831).** At most **300,000 € over any 3 years** per single undertaking, counted from the date the right to the aid is granted, whatever its form (art. 3). A single undertaking includes every enterprise linked by majority voting rights, board control, dominant influence or a shareholder agreement (art. 2(2)): if a partner controls another company that is linked to ours, its de minimis aid counts against the same ceiling. The register applies from 2026. Everything in this plan stays far below 300,000 €, but each application asks for a sworn declaration of the past three years' aid, which the accountant must prepare from each partner's other companies.
