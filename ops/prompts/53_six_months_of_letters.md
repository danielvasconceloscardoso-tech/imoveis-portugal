# Prompt 53: six months of letters, the sponsor prototypes, and the plan to sell them

CEO, 2 Nov 2026, 15:15. Goal: 52 letters ready (Tuesday and Friday, Tue 1 Dec 2026 to Fri 28 May 2027), built on a researched bank of the best of Portugal, plus sponsor-ready prototypes the partners can negotiate on, plus the monetization plan. Follow `content/VOICE_AND_WORLD.md` (D73) to the letter, including the banned AI patterns, and prompt 52 for the format. Nothing is sent.

## 1. The bank: everything worth writing about (`content/letter/BANK.csv` + `BANK.md`)

Find at least 300 entries the operator would want to read about: premium and cool, insider not tourist, true and checkable. For each entry: name, type, place, municipality, one-line why it matters, two checked facts with sources and dates, best month to feature it, image route (our licensed library, the venue's press kit with permission, or a commission), possible sponsor category.

Cover, at minimum:
1. **Art and street art:** Vhils, Joana Vasconcelos, Bordalo II, Killa Was Here, Paula Rego, the new galleries in Porto and Lisbon, the azulejo.
2. **Museums and foundations:** Gulbenkian, Serralves, MAAT, CCB/MAC, Casa da Música, Museu do Oriente, Museu Coleção Berardo [current status], Casa das Histórias, the Douro Museum, Madeira's museums.
3. **Architecture:** Siza, Souto de Moura, the projects worth a detour, the houses that changed a place (Comporta's cabanas, the new Porto riverfront).
4. **Golf resorts and courses** with their designers and stories.
5. **Tables:** every Michelin star in the current edition with the chef's story; the beloved places (JNcQUOI, Ramiro, Taberna da Rua das Flores, Café Santiago, the tascas); the chefs people follow.
6. **Hotels** people talk about and the stories behind them.
7. **Wine and spirits:** the Douro story, the quintas that receive visitors, Alentejo, Vinho Verde, Madeira, Colares, the winemakers.
8. **Football and sport:** the three clubs, the derbies and their dates, Ronaldo's Funchal, surf (Nazaré, Ericeira, Peniche), padel, sailing, the Estoril Open, cycling.
9. **Traditions and festas** with their dates in the six months.
10. **Events worth flying for** between December and May, with dates.
11. **Famous people and Portugal:** only from published reporting, attributed (who bought, who opened what, who moved), never rumour as fact, no addresses.
12. **Places by municipality,** so each letter can tie to the map and the index.
13. **Stories with a twist the reader didn't know** (the Gulbenkian arrived because of the war; Pombal demarcated the Douro in 1756; the Ronaldo airport): at least 50.

## 2. The calendar (`content/letter/CALENDAR_DEC26_MAY27.md`)

52 slots, Tuesday and Friday. Tuesday: a place and its story (with "A casa" from the map or a partner). Friday: the weekend (a table, an exhibition, a match, a festa, an event). Built around real dates: São Martinho is gone, so start with Christmas lights, Madeira New Year, Nazaré big-wave season, Carnaval (Torres Vedras, Ovar), almond blossom in the Algarve, Easter, the Estoril Open, the start of the Santos Populares season. **One letter for everyone (operator, 2 Nov, D74):** the same content for every reader, whether in Luxembourg, France, Switzerland, Brazil, the US or Israel, sent in the reader's language (PT, EN, FR). Balance the year across Portugal, not across countries.

## 3. The 52 letters

1. One letter per slot, written in PT-PT first (the operator reviews these), then the same letter in EN and FR. No edition-specific versions; nothing that assumes where the reader lives (say "de onde estiver", not "do Luxemburgo").
2. Each letter: the destaque with its photo and number, A casa, Este mês or the weekend item, O número, the partner slot, Como lá chegar (three lines, positive), A sua pergunta.
3. Run every draft through a checker that fails on the banned patterns of VOICE_AND_WORLD (contrast flip, demotion punchline, kicker, mic-drop, unsourced superlative, banned words, triplets), on sentences over 25 words, and on any fact without a source.
4. Deliver as `content/letter/2026-12-01_lu-pt.md` etc., plus a contact sheet PDF per month.

## 4. Sponsor prototypes (what the partners show to sponsors)

1. **Six sample letters with the partner slot filled,** one per sponsor category: bank (account and mortgage from abroad), developer (a project and its homes), golf resort, insurer, currency transfer, lawyer or tax adviser. The sponsor shown is a **clearly fictional placeholder** ("Banco Exemplo", "Empreendimento Exemplo"), never a real brand or logo; marked "Exemplo" in the corner.
2. **The formats on one page each:** the partner block in the letter; "Apresentado por" (one sponsor presents a whole edition for a quarter: header line, the block, one destaque a month on its theme, all labelled); the Friday "fim de semana com" (a resort, a hotel or a restaurant group sponsors the weekend item); the series sponsor (a 6-letter series, e.g. "O Douro em seis cartas", "Golfe de dezembro a maio"); the social extension (the same content in the feed).
3. **A one-page sponsor deck per category** (PT and EN): the reader, the format, the calendar slots that fit them (e.g. golf in the Algarve weeks), the founding price, the step-up, the labelling rule, the reporting they get.
4. Every prototype labelled "Patrocinado" as the live product will be (Código da Publicidade art. 8).

## 5. The monetization plan (`sponsors/LETTER_PLAN.md` + PDF, at most 4 pages)

Write it from the CEO's structure:
1. **Inventory (one letter for everyone, D74):** in six months, 52 partner blocks, 26 Friday weekend items, 6 monthly "Apresentado por" and 4 to 6 themed series.
2. **List prices until 10,000 subscribers** (the Founding Partner −40% for the first ten applies on top): partner block 250 € per letter or 1,500 € a month (eight letters, category exclusive); "Apresentado por" 4,000 € a month; a six-letter series 2,500 €; Friday weekend item 400 € per letter. Then per reader: 60 € per 1,000 subscribers per letter for the block, ×2.5 for "Apresentado por", reviewed every quarter. (Final numbers: the operator, task 45b.)
3. **Who to sell to first, by calendar fit:** banks' non-resident units for December–January (the year-end decision season), Algarve golf resorts for the golf weeks, developers for their launches, Madeira hotels for New Year, insurers and currency transfer all year.
4. **Packaging:** sell calendars, not slots (a sponsor buys "the six Algarve letters"); bundle the letter with the social extension and the site placement; exclusivity per category per edition at +50%.
5. **Proof without audience yet:** the prototypes, the open and click benchmarks of the category (cite public industry figures), the monthly report they will get, the subscriber growth plan; the founding price as the risk discount.
6. **Negotiation playbook:** price on the value of one client to them; scarcity that is real; give inventory, never discounts; three-month minimum; step-up in the first contract; close on the booking form the same week; what to say when they ask for audience numbers, for performance pricing (never per lead, D6), or for editorial control (never: the label and our independence are the product).
7. **Targets:** six months of sold slots in the first 90 days after launch: realistic, ambitious, with the number of signatures each requires.
