To run: "Read ops/prompts/24_sellside_renovate_referrals.md and execute it." Work on a branch and open a PR. Nothing is published or merged without the operator's "go". PT, EN and FR natively; brand and voice rules as in the latest prompts.

## 1. Sell-side (D46)
1. A journey stage and page per country: "Herdei / tenho uma casa em Portugal: vender, arrendar ou manter?", with the verified rules (inheritance registration, capital gains for non-residents and at home, IMI) and the partner for each step.
2. **"Quanto vale a casa da família?"**: a request form (Tally, give the operator the fields: location, typology, area, condition, situation such as inherited or co-owned, country of residence, consent naming Habinedita Gondomar, AMI 13250). The site shows the INE median €/m² for that municipality as context, clearly labelled as statistics and not a valuation (property valuation is a regulated activity); the agency replies with its own assessment.
3. Link it from the map place cards, the price-trend section and the inheritance FAQ answers.

## 2. Renovate and rent (D46)
1. A stage per country after the purchase: renovating from abroad, furnishing, renting out (long-term or local accommodation: rules, registration, tax in Portugal and at home).
2. Partner slots (no prices on the site): architects, builders, interior and furnishing, rental management.

## 3. Referral partners in unregulated categories (D45)
1. Money transfer, NIF and tax representation, translation, moving, furniture, rental management: referral links allowed, each labelled "Publicidade · ligação de parceiro" (Código da Publicidade, art. 8.º), never presented as editorial advice.
2. A config file for referral links (partner, category, URL with tracking parameter, label), empty until the operator adds the partner programmes.
3. Never for real estate, credit or insurance (licensed partner group only).

## 4. Imóveis Portugal Data (D47)
Prepare the structure only: a "Data" page describing the investors and developers database as a product for professionals (company-level fields only), with an enquiry form. No personal contact data in the repo or on the site.
