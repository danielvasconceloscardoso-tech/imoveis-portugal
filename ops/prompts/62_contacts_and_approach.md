1. **No personal data in the repo.** pipeline.csv keeps company-level rows only (company, category, priority, status, next step, date). Names, roles, e-mails and phones of people go only in the .xlsx sent to the operator, never committed (add it to .gitignore).

No scraping of LinkedIn or any site whose terms forbid it; for LinkedIn give the search to run by hand ("Diretor de Marketing" + company).
3. Each row: company, category, priority, person (if public), role, generic or professional e-mail, phone, source link, date checked. Prefer role addresses (marketing@, comercial@) when no named contact is published.
4. B2B e-mails under Lei 41/2004 art. 13-A(3): opt-out in every message; GDPR art. 14 notice in the first message (who we are, why we have the contact, how to object). Task 45c (confirm with the operator) still applies.

## 3. The approach (one page, Portuguese, for the sales team)
1. **Developers:** warm first. The partner group's own relationships, then a visit or call, never a cold mass e-mail. Bring the demo on a phone: their building on the map, the Concierge, a sample letter featuring it. Close with the booking form at 250–350 € per unit plus the optional launch package.
2. **Brands:** e-mail from the brand address (5 lines: who we are, the reader and the moment, what they get, the founding offer and its deadline, a 20-minute call), then call on day 3, follow up on day 7, last note on day 14. Parallel e-mail to the media agency.
3. Write the four messages (developer, digital bank/BYD, traditional bank, school) and the call script (60 seconds: who we are, the reader, the offer, the ask). Short, plain, no AI slop, in the voice of content/VOICE_AND_WORLD.md for a business reader.
4. The founding offer: first 10 partners −40% in year 1, price locked for year 2, paid upfront for 12 or 6 months (D79).
5. Never present the platform as a press outlet (D71) or as a mediator; never promise leads or results.
