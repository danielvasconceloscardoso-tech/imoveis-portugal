# Prompt 58: competitors and likely buyers, and how to approach them (due Fri 6 Nov 2026)

CEO, 2 Nov 2026, 23:00. Operator's request: a competitive analysis of the companies that compete with us today and those likely to buy us (D76: exit above 1 M€), and the most professional way to approach them. **One document, English, at most 8 pages, plus one CSV** (rule 15). Due Friday 6 November, end of day.

## 1. Who to cover

1. **Likely buyers:** idealista (and idealista/news), Imovirtual (OLX Group), Casafari, Confidencial Imobiliário, Doutor Finanças, ComparaJá, the Portuguese media groups (Impresa, Global Media, Medialivre, Público, ECO), a bank's non-resident unit, and the international portals active in Portugal (Kyero, Green-Acres, JamesEdition, Rightmove Overseas).
2. **Direct competitors for our reader:** content and lead sites for foreigners and the diaspora buying in Portugal (Portugalist, Portugal Homes, The Portugal News property, Global Citizen Solutions' guides, relocation advisers with content arms, the Facebook and WhatsApp diaspora groups as an informal channel), and any newsletter or Instagram account of note in the same space.

## 2. For each (one row in `sponsors/research/competitors.csv`, one short paragraph in the document)

1. What they do, who their reader is, how they make money (from public sources: their site, annual accounts at the Portuguese business register or Companies House, press).
2. Size signals: revenue where public, traffic estimates from public tools where available (cite the tool and date), social followers, newsletter if any.
3. Where they overlap with us and where they don't (diaspora, one letter for everyone, the Descobrir map, the five-star concierge, flat sponsorship instead of leads).
4. What they would want from us (audience, data, a lead channel they don't have, brand, team) and what they have paid for similar assets (acquisitions with reported prices only, cited).
5. Risk to us: could they copy us in a quarter? What protects us?

## 3. The approach (the part the operator cares about most)

1. **Not now as sellers.** We approach buyers first as partners (content partnership, data sharing, a sponsorship) so that by the time of the exit they know the business and its numbers. Write the partner pitch per buyer type in five lines each.
2. **Who at each company:** the role (head of partnerships, business development, M&A or corporate development, publisher), never a personal name; the public channel to reach them.
3. **The sequence and timing:** what we must be able to show before each type of conversation (subscribers, renewals, revenue, months of history) and the month we can realistically start.
4. **What a professional first message looks like:** one short email in Portuguese for the Portuguese companies and one in English for the international ones, plain and human (the operator's style: short, no hype), signed by the company.
5. **What to prepare for an eventual sale:** the five numbers every buyer asks for first, and how we keep them clean from month one.

## 4. Report

The document and the CSV by Friday 6 November. One line on anything you could not verify. Nothing is sent to any company.
