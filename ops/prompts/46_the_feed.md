# Prompt 46: the feed (D70). Instagram as a curated page with a number on every image

CEO, 30 Oct 2026, 13:30. Operator's direction: our social accounts work like Gentleman's Gram and Visit Madeira: a curated, beautiful feed that brands and tourism boards pay to appear in. Ours has one thing theirs lacks: a number on every image. Applies to prompt 30's content machine from the next batch. Social accounts are created by the operator (task 30b); nothing is posted without the operator's go.

## 1. The standard

1. **One image, one place, one number.** Every post: a photograph or 20–40 s film at the level of the site's hero (sunlit, no filters, no text walls), the place name, and the number: the index score once live, until then the median price per m² and its 10-year change. Small, bottom left, in the identity's type. Nothing else on the image.
2. **Caption:** at most three short lines in the edition's language, the source, one action ("Ver no mapa"). No hashtag walls (playbook rules stand).
3. **Cadence:** one post a day per account, one reel every two days, stories daily from the same material. Same post across the six editions' accounts, captions localised.
4. **Sources of images, in order:** our own films and licensed photos; footage commissioned per region once a sponsor has paid (drone and photo day rates, budgeted at 300–800 €); creator content reposted only with written permission (DM screenshot logged in `social/permissions.csv`) and credited. Never Google Images, never stock with faces.

## 2. The growth loop

1. **Collab posts** (Instagram's co-author feature) with every partner that posts: a golf resort, a developer, a tourism board. One collab puts us in front of their followers; this is the cheapest reach we will ever buy.
2. **Tag and hashtag the boards** (Visit Portugal, Visit Madeira, Visit Algarve, Porto e Norte, Visit Alentejo, Centro de Portugal, Visit Azores, Visit Lisboa) on every relevant post; they repost what fits. Keep a log of reposts in `ops/METRICS.md`.
3. **Series that invite a buyer:** "Concelho da semana" (place + score + what 500,000 € buys there), "Golf spot of the week", "Madeira in December", "Comporta at 7 a.m.", "What your budget buys" (one number, six cities). Each series has a fixed visual frame so the feed reads as one magazine.
4. **The feed and the letter are one issue:** the week's cover (prompt 45 §2) is Monday's post.

## 3. Paid formats (rate card v5 §7b)

1. Featured post, featured reel, Golf spot of the week, Concelho da semana, Developer launch, Tourism-board campaign. Every paid item labelled "Patrocinado" in the caption's first line, with Instagram's paid-partnership tool on; FR captions carry "collaboration commerciale".
2. Build the formats as templates in `social/templates/` (image frame, number placement, caption skeleton per language) so a paid post takes minutes, not hours.
3. A one-page social media kit, `sponsors/media-kit/social.pdf`: the six accounts, the formats, the prices, three example posts, the labelling rule. Updated automatically with follower counts once the accounts exist.

## 4. Deliver

1. Rework the next weekly batch (content/inbox, prompt 30) to this standard before anything else is produced; weeks already written are re-cut to the frame, not rewritten.
2. Report: ten example posts (PT, EN, FR) as a contact sheet PDF, the templates, the media kit. No posting.
