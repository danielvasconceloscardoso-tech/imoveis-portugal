# Prompt 50: the funding map. Portuguese and EU money, and who funded ideas like ours

CEO, 1 Nov 2026, 23:00. Find every public or quasi-public source of money (grants, vouchers, tax credits, co-investment, hiring support) that a new Portuguese Lda like ours can get in the next 12 months; prove it with who has already received it for similar projects; rank by euros per hour of effort and by time to cash; prepare the first three applications so a partner only signs. Assume: a Portuguese Lda incorporated in November 2026 in the Porto area (Norte region), two managers, no employees yet, a data and services platform for buyers abroad (web portal, data, advertising; not a press title, D71), six markets, launch 1 December 2026.

## 1. The sources to cover (verify each on its own site; add any you find)

1. **Portugal 2030 through Norte 2030 and COMPETE 2030:** the SI Inovação Produtiva, SI Qualificação and Internacionalização lines, the simplified "Vales" (incubação, empreendedorismo, internacionalização, digitalização, I&D) as they exist in the 2021–2027 period; which accept a company under two years old; calls open now and the published calendar of calls (Balcão dos Fundos, the programme's "avisos"); eligible costs (software and data platform development, content in five languages, international marketing, fairs, certification, IP and trademark filing); grant rate for a small company in Norte, ceilings, advance payments; the publicity obligations (Regulation (EU) 2021/1060, art. 47–50 and Annex IX) that make the logos appear.
2. **PRR (Recuperar Portugal):** any lines still open to startups in 2026 (Startup Portugal vouchers, digital transition, "Empresas 4.0"); the end dates.
3. **Startup Portugal:** startup recognition under Lei 21/2023 and what it unlocks; Startup Voucher, Vale Incubadora or their 2026 successors; the incubator network in Norte (UPTEC, ScaleUp Porto, Startup Braga, Startup Guimarães) and the vouchers they administer.
4. **Banco Português de Fomento:** co-investment with business angels and venture funds, the guarantee lines for SMEs (via the mutual guarantee societies), any InvestEU-backed instrument for startups.
5. **Portugal Ventures:** its calls (Ignition, proptech or digital themes), ticket sizes, what they take.
6. **IAPMEI and IEFP:** the SME certificate (prerequisite for most of the above); IEFP hiring incentives (the 2026 equivalent of "Compromisso Emprego Sustentável", internships "Estágios Ativar"), relevant once we hire the first person.
7. **Tax:** SIFIDE II (R&D tax credit; whether the index method, the data pipeline and the simulators qualify as R&D; ANI's certification), the RFAI and the IRC benefits for small and interior companies, the startup tax regime under Lei 21/2023 (stock options).
8. **EU direct:** EIC Accelerator (realistic? say so), Horizon Europe calls on data or housing, Digital Europe, the EIT KICs (EIT Digital, EIT Urban Mobility) and their startup programmes, the Interreg Atlantic Area and SUDOE programmes (projects with partners in France and Spain), Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs. Creative Europe and any journalism lines: note that they would require us to act as a press title, which conflicts with D71; list them only for the record.
9. **Diaspora-linked:** whether the PNAID, the GAID or the "Voltar" programme (OE2026) fund or co-fund projects that inform or support returning or investing emigrants; municipal programmes in Norte that pay for diaspora outreach.
10. **Regional and municipal:** Câmara do Porto, Gondomar and the AMP (Área Metropolitana do Porto) startup and internationalisation support; Porto Digital and Porto Innovation Hub.

## 2. Who has already been funded for something like us (the proof)

Search the beneficiary data: **Kohesio** (the EU database of cohesion-funded projects, kohesio.ec.europa.eu, filter Portugal and the 2014–2020 and 2021–2027 periods), the Portugal 2030 and Portugal 2020 lists of approved operations, the PRR beneficiary lists (Recuperar Portugal transparency portal), Portugal Ventures' portfolio, Startup Portugal's voucher beneficiaries.
2. Search terms (PT and EN): plataforma imobiliária, portal imobiliário, proptech, dados imobiliários, índice de preços, internacionalização de plataforma digital, diáspora, comunidades portuguesas, emigrantes, investimento estrangeiro, marketplace, simulador, mapa interativo.
3. For each comparable (aim for 15–25): company, programme and line, year, approved amount and grant amount, what the project was, region. Note proptechs and data platforms (Casafari, Imovirtual, Idealista Portugal, Confidencial Imobiliário and others) where public records show funding.
4. Write the patterns: which lines fund platforms like ours, typical amounts, typical wording of the project, what reviewers seem to reward.
