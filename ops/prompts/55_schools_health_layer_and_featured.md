# Prompt 55: schools, universities and hospitals on the map (free), and the "featured" product (paid)

CEO, 2 Nov 2026, 16:45. Families choose the school and the hospital before the house. Show all of them on the map as reference points, free, from official data; sell "featured" status to those who want more (D75). Ship per D67.

## 1. The layer (free, for everyone)

1. **Data, official only:**
   - international and private schools from the Ministry of Education's school directories (DGEEC / the school network data on dados.gov.pt) plus each school's own site for curriculum (British, American, French, German, Swiss, IB) and ages;
   - universities and business schools from DGES (higher-education institutions);
   - hospitals and clinics: SNS hospitals (already in the index) and private hospitals from the ERS registry of health providers.
   Each point: name, type, curriculum or specialty, languages, address, website, source, date checked.
2. **On the map:** a "Família" layer (schools, universities) and a "Saúde" layer (hospitals), off by default, one tap to turn on; small neutral icons; the place card shows "Escolas internacionais a 15 min: 3", "Hospital com urgência: 12 min".
3. **In the Concierge:** "Têm filhos em idade escolar?" as an optional question; if yes, the suggestions show schools near each place.
4. **In the index:** no change to the official index; "O seu índice" gets an optional "escolas" weight.

## 2. Featured (paid, labelled "Parceiro")

1. A featured school, university or hospital gets: its logo on its point, a richer card (photos, curriculum, admissions dates, open days, languages, fees as the institution chooses to state them), a "Pedir informação" / "Marcar visita" action through the Concierge (consent naming the institution, unticked), placement first in its area's list, and the monthly report.
2. Featured never changes the order of the free data elsewhere, never removes competitors from the map, and never touches the index (D69).
3. Health: wording within the rules on advertising health services (DL 238/2015, ERS); no claims of results, no comparisons.

## 3. The sales kit for these three categories

1. A one-page deck per category (PT, EN): the reader (families and professionals moving to Portugal), the layer, the featured card, the letter products (sponsored story, a series in August–September for schools, the Friday item for open days), the social extension, the price, the report.
2. A sample featured card for one fictional school and one fictional hospital ("Exemplo"), never a real institution without its consent.

## 4. Report

The layer live per D67, the deck PDFs, the count of points per type and region, one line on anything you could not verify.
