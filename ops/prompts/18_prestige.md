# Prompt 18 — Prestige: look like the obvious place for 500k € and 10 M€ buyers, and for the partners who serve them

To run: "Read ops/prompts/18_prestige.md and execute it." Work on a branch and open a PR. Nothing is published or merged without the operator's "go". PT, EN and FR, natively.

Decision D36: **looking amazing and having amazing partners matter more than doing more things.** The brand must feel prestigious, so that a 500k € buyer and a 10 M€ buyer both feel welcome, and Swiss wealth planners, Portuguese tax planners, Luxembourg banks, Brazilian advisers and prime developers feel they should be on it. Nothing that costs the team time (events, webinars, roundtables, calls by the team) is promised or built unless Daniel approves it.

## 1. Budget scenarios, not one number

1. The map (prompt 16) and the launch data story show **four scenarios side by side: 300,000 €, 1 M€, 5 M€ and 10 M€**, each in the reader's currency too.
2. For each scenario, where in Portugal that budget makes sense, with photos: e.g. Porto and Braga apartments; Lisbon and Cascais; Comporta and the Algarve's Golden Triangle (Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Vilamoura); Douro quintas; Madeira.
3. Figures: INE municipality medians (with date and source). INE medians don't describe prime enclaves (Quinta do Lago sits inside Loulé's median), so for 5 M€ and 10 M€ use **real published listings or developer price lists** as examples, labelled with the source and "exemplo" (or "patrocinado" if from a partner). Never invent a price or a property.
4. Launch data story = "O que 300 mil, 1 milhão, 5 milhões e 10 milhões de euros compram em Portugal", replacing the single 500k € story everywhere (prompts 15 and 16, press kit, social).

## 2. Prestige across the site

1. Visual: the Comporta and Monocle rules from prompt 10 everywhere: generous white space, large premium photography across the price range (from a Porto apartment to a Comporta house), restrained type, no busy elements.
2. Copy: calm confidence. Every budget is served with the same respect; no "cheap" or "luxury" labels; the information scales (taxes, financing and structuring matter more as budgets grow).
3. The "Private" section (D33) for 1 M€+: wealth and tax structuring questions (for example residence options, IFICI, inheritance planning, holding structures), each answered with the verified rule and the matching partner. Information only (editorial policy).
4. Trust markers, all real: licences of every partner shown (AMI, Banco de Portugal, ASF, Ordem dos Advogados), sources under every figure, sponsored labels. No invented logos, clients or audience numbers.

## 3. Partner pages that make the right firms want to pay

1. **"Para parceiros" rewritten for decision-makers** at private banks, wealth managers, tax planners, law firms, money-transfer firms and developers in LU, CH, FR, BR, US and IL: who reads us (people abroad preparing a purchase in Portugal, from 300k € to 10 M€+), why now (launch 1 Dec, founding partners), what they get (placements only: profile, category answers, simulator and map positions, newsletter slots, Private section), prices (price list v3 and the Private tier, both still *proposal* until Daniel confirms tasks 8s and 8t).
2. One page per partner type (private banks and wealth, tax and legal, money transfer, developers), each in the language that type reads (FR and EN for Swiss and Luxembourg finance, PT for Portuguese firms, PT for Brazilian advisers, EN for US and Israel).
3. Media kit PDF per partner type, same design.
4. "Our partners" page that looks premium even with few partners: the partner group first (agency, credit, insurance, all licensed), then founding partners as they sign.

## 4. Remove time-consuming promises

Search the site, media kits, outreach drafts and plans for events, webinars, roundtables, live sessions, private reports or team-delivered services not decided by Daniel, and remove them. The paid calls stay (delivered by the partner agency, D19).
