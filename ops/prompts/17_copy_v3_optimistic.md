# Prompt 17 — Copy v3: optimistic, realistic, obstacle → solution

To run: "Read ops/prompts/17_copy_v3_optimistic.md and execute it." Work on a branch and open a PR. Nothing is published or merged without the operator's "go". PT, EN and FR, each written natively.

Daniel's verdict on the current copy: some lines sound written by someone who doesn't know the reader ("todos os agostos", "casa tratada de fora", "O preço é o início. Por cima paga…", "245 000 € e 20 000 € por cima", "quem compra"). Nobody talks like that. The tone must be **optimistic while staying realistic**, and every obstacle the reader has must meet its solution: the information, then the partner who solves it.

## 1. Voice rules v3 (replace the matching parts of content/VOICE.md)

1. **Optimistic and realistic.** Buying from abroad is doable and we show how. Never dramatise costs or risks; never promise outcomes or returns.
2. **Obstacle → information → partner.** Each section, FAQ answer, newsletter item and e-book chapter names a real obstacle in the reader's words ("Não posso ir a Portugal", "O banco empresta a quem vive fora?"), answers it with the verified rule or number, and ends with the partner who solves it (lawyer, credit partner, tax adviser, agency, money transfer, developer).
3. **Talk to the reader directly** ("você", "o seu", "pode"). Never "quem compra", "o comprador", "os utilizadores".
4. **Costs as information, not as a warning:** "Custo total com impostos, escritura e registo: X €", or "Custos de compra". Never "por cima", "a somar", "o preço é o início".
5. **Banned phrases:** "todos os agostos", "tratada de fora", "o preço é o início", "por cima paga", "por cima", "quem compra", plus the AI list from prompt 11. Add them to the voice check in the scorecard (prompt 15).
6. No clichés about emigrants (August, saudade, the village); respect the reader as a capable adult with a plan.
7. Headlines: short, positive and concrete ("Comprar casa em Portugal a partir do Luxemburgo é mais simples do que parece").

## 2. Apply everywhere

1. Home page: use content/copy/HOME_CEO_DRAFT.md (v2) as written for PT, adapting the edition name per country; write EN and FR natively from it.
2. Rewrite COPY_DECK.md, the FAQ answers, the newsletter format and week 1 drafts, the e-book texts, the Private section and the one-stop page under these rules, in all three languages.
3. Search the whole site and content for the banned phrases and remove every one (`grep` in PT, EN and FR equivalents).
4. The one-stop "home from abroad" page (prompt 13) gets a name that follows these rules (e.g. "Comprar a partir de fora, passo a passo").
