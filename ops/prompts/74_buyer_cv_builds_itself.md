# Prompt 74: Short Concierge, full buyer CV: the profile builds itself

Operator, 4 Nov 2026: "The concierge is not preparing the CV of the qualified buyer. That's one of our key features. What can we do to have both?"

The CV is the qualified summary (D54) and "O meu Portugal" (D79). Prompt 69 cut the Concierge to three steps and moved the qualification questions to an optional screen after booking, so most requests now arrive without a CV.

Run after prompt 73. Goes live under D67. Follow content/METHOD.md (functional = plain words). Record it as the next free D number.

## The principle
Booking stays at three taps. The CV builds itself from what the buyer already did, the buyer confirms it in seconds, and we ask only for what is missing. The concierge already knows; he does not interrogate.

## 1. Fill the CV from the path (D54, in the browser)
Take what the reader has already done:
- **edition and language:** the country they live in;
- **simulator:** price, use (to live in or holiday/investment), with or without credit, down payment, total cost, IMT;
- **map:** places and developments opened or saved (♥), and how long they spent on each;
- **Concierge:** what they need, the budget, the developer or place they came from;
- **Maré game:** the concelho chosen for the prize, if any.

## 2. Show it on the confirmation screen, and ask only the gaps
1. **Under "Marcado", a card titled "O seu perfil de comprador":** each known line pre-filled, editable with one tap. For example:
   - "Vive em: Luxemburgo";
   - "Orçamento: < 300 mil €";
   - "Para viver";
   - "Com crédito, entrada 20%";
   - "Interesse: Gondomar, O'rizon".
2. **Then at most three one-tap questions,** only for what is missing:
   - "Quando quer comprar?" (< 6 meses / 6–12 meses / Mais tarde);
   - "Já tem NIF?" (Sim / Ainda não);
   - "Pensa passar a residente em Portugal?" (Sim / Não / Talvez). This one drives the 7.5% IMT refund; show the reader's own refund figure beside the answer.
3. **"Pronto" sends it.** Skipping is allowed; whatever is filled travels with the request.

## 3. The buyer keeps the CV too ("O meu Portugal", D79)
1. **A one-page summary they can open, save as PDF and reuse** with a bank or lawyer: budget, full costs, IMT and refund, timeline, NIF, shortlist.
2. **In the confirmation email,** with one tap to complete any missing line before the call.

## 4. The consultant and the developer
1. **The consultant gets the CV with every request:**
   - the fields;
   - a completeness figure ("Perfil 80%");
   - where it came from (simulator, map, answers).
2. **A developer gets it only if the buyer ticks an unticked box naming that developer** (hard rule 4, D54, D79).
3. **For the sponsor report:** the number of qualified buyers per developer per month, where qualified means 4 out of 5 key fields filled (country, budget, use, timeline, financing).

## Data
1. **No personal data in the repo** (hard rule 3).
2. **The CV lives where D79 says**, with its consent text.
3. **Update the privacy policy** in the same pass if a new field is stored.

## Acceptance (on main, phone, PT, EN and FR)
1. A reader who used the simulator and opened Gondomar books in three taps, and the confirmation shows a pre-filled profile with at least five lines and no more than three questions.
2. The consultant's email carries the CV and the completeness figure.
3. The buyer can open and save their one-page summary.
4. A developer receives nothing without the named consent.
5. The checks and the build pass; the Ana and James agents (prompt 70) book a call and find the CV quick and useful.
