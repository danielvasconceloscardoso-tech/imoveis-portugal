# Prompt 47: look professional in ten days. Brand check, company pack, sales papers, credibility

CEO, 1 Nov 2026, 21:30. Three developers have said they will pay. Before the first invoice we need a company, a protectable brand, the papers a developer signs, and the signs of credibility a sponsor looks for. The operator decides three things (jurisdiction, the manager of record, the brand path); everything below runs now, and the parts marked **[name]** are re-run once the name is chosen. Deliver PDFs for the documents; ship to the site per D67 only what §5 says.

## 1. Brand check [name]

1. **Criteria,** scored 0–3 each: distinctive and protectable as a word mark (not descriptive in PT, FR, EN, DE or HE); pronounceable and spellable in all five; `.pt` and `.com` free or buyable, plus `.lu`, `.fr`, `.ch`, `.com.br`, `.us`, `.co.il` availability noted; Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and LinkedIn handles free; no conflicting trademark in classes 35, 36, 41, 42 at INPI (PT), EUIPO and INPI (BR); no dominant Google collision (a newspaper, an agency, a bank); fits the concept (a number on every place, Portugal seen from abroad, the magazine).
2. **Shortlist to check,** with "Imóveis Portugal" as the control: Farol · Miradouro · Morada · Solar · Raiz · Mirante · Atlas Portugal · Lugar · Varanda · Portugal 100 · Casa Cem · Latitude 39. Add up to eight of your own that pass criterion 1.
3. **Checks:** domains via RDAP (rdap.org, rdap.verisign.com) and DNS.pt's whois; handles via HTTP HEAD on each platform (if the platform blocks it, say so and list them for a manual check); trademarks via TMview and the INPI search; collisions via WebSearch in the five languages; descriptiveness per the EUIPO guidelines (a sign descriptive in any EU language is refused).
4. **Deliver** `ops/BRAND_CHECK.pdf`: the scored table, the three finalists with a one-line case each, and for each finalist the exact domains and handles to buy and the trademark classes to file. Note the registration conditions for `.com.br` (Brazilian taxpayer number or representative) and `.co.il`. No purchase.

## 2. Company pack (Portugal, Lda; the CEO's recommendation, operator decides)

1. **Steps for the operator**, one page: Empresa na Hora (same day, about 360 €; certificate of admissibility if a custom name), minimum capital, the manager of record, the registered office, the NIF, the IVA regime, Segurança Social registration, the bank account, the certified accountant. List the current CAE codes for web portals, publishing of periodicals, advertising and other information services (CAE Rev. 4), and say which is primary.
2. **Invoicing:** the certified-software obligation and its turnover threshold; the free route via the Portal das Finanças while under it; ATCUD and QR code; an invoice template per rate card v5 (per-unit listing, pilot, cards), with the sponsorship wording (D6), no per-lead line ever.
3. **Consumer rules for the paid calls and guides:** the Livro de Reclamações eletrónico (how to join, the footer link), the distance-selling information and the 14-day withdrawal wording for digital content and services (DL 24/2014), the price display.
4. **Legal pages** per edition, rewritten with placeholders for the entity: the legal notice per DL 7/2004 art. 10 (name, registered office, registration number, NIF, contact), the privacy policy (controller = the entity; recipients named per consent: partner agency, lawyer, bank, developer), cookies (none), the publicity labelling policy, the disclaimer (D12). PT, EN, FR.
5. **Memo for the operator (facts, no conclusion):** whether the site and the letters are a periodical under the Lei de Imprensa (Lei 2/99: arts. 5, 9, 15, 19), which would require ERC registration, a named director and a ficha técnica; what the ERC publishes about registered online publications; the cost and timing.
6. **Guardrails for the finance and insurance cards (facts):** the credit-intermediary regime (DL 81-C/2017) and insurance distribution (Lei 7/2019): what counts as presenting or proposing a contract versus advertising, so the partner-bank card and the "simulate with our partner" action stay advertising (flat fee, the simulation runs on the bank's side, no data passed by us without the reader's own action).
7. **Trademark pack [name]:** the INPI national filing and the EUIPO filing for the chosen name as a word mark and the logo as a figurative mark, classes 35, 36, 41, 42, fees and forms; Brazil and the US as the next step. Note that "Imóveis Portugal" can only be filed as a figurative mark and offers weak protection.

## 3. Sales papers for the three developers (and the next twenty)

1. **Founding booking form,** one page: the project, the municipality, the blocks and units, the price band, the list price per unit and the founding price, the 12-months-or-until-sold term, the go-live date, the step-up clause (list +50% at 10,000 subscribers; founding price kept for year 2), signature. Non-binding until the agreement is signed; it reserves one of ten founding slots.
2. **Sponsorship agreement,** PT and EN, from the CEO's structure: flat fee for a placement and services, paid upfront on invoice; what we deliver (pin, card, showcase, request flow to the qualified advisor, letters in six editions, the sales-team view, the monthly report); labelling (Código da Publicidade art. 8); independence (the index and the assessment are never affected); data protection (the developer receives a request only after the reader's consent naming it, if that routing is ever enabled); no per-lead fee, no exclusivity unless bought; term, renewal, termination; governing law Portugal. The operator reviews it before use.
3. **Onboarding checklist** for a developer: what we need in 48 hours (photographs with rights, the availability table or a feed, the sales contact and languages, the documents for the assessment criteria, the logo), and what goes live on day 1, day 3 and day 7.
4. **The monthly sponsor report,** generated from Cloudflare Web Analytics and the request log: reach per edition, views of the showcase, requests and their qualified summaries (no personal data), the index card of the municipality. `/sponsor-report` produces it as PDF.
5. **"Parceiros" page:** the developers, the lawyers, the banks and the institutions, each labelled, each with its consent record in `content/partners/consents.csv` (who, what, date, document). Nothing appears without a row.

## 4. Credibility: who can stand behind us, how fast

1. **Build the table** `ops/CREDIBILITY.md` (and a PDF): for each body, what it is, what it gives (a membership badge, a licence to use a mark, a listing, a grant with publicity obligations), the conditions, the cost, the time to a visible logo, the link to apply, and whether it is honest to show before launch. Cover at least:
   - Portuguese business associations and the bilateral chambers: AEP and ACP (Porto), AIP, ANJE; the Luso-French, Luso-Swiss, Portugal–Brazil, Portugal–US and Israel–Portugal chambers; the Portuguese business associations in Luxembourg;
   - incubators in Porto and the north (UPTEC, ScaleUp Porto, Startup Braga) and the Startup Portugal network;
   - IAPMEI: the SME certificate, "Portugal Sou Eu" (whether it is open to services), PME Líder conditions;
   - Portugal 2030 / COMPETE 2030 / Norte 2030: the "Vales" and internationalisation lines open to a company under two years old, the calls open now or next, the publicity obligations that make the logos appear, and the realistic timeline from incorporation to a logo on the site;
   - the diaspora institutions: GAID (Gabinete de Apoio ao Investidor da Diáspora), PNAID and its partner network, the Secretaria de Estado das Comunidades, the Conselho da Diáspora Portuguesa; what a media or content partnership with each could look like;
   - AICEP and Turismo de Portugal and the regional promotion agencies: co-marketing and source-market programmes, not endorsements;
   - embassies and consulates: what they can do (list community media, share events) and cannot (endorse a commercial site).
   Rank the table by weeks to a legitimate logo. State plainly that EU funds' emblems may only be shown by a beneficiary of an approved operation.
2. **Press kit for the Index and the Portugal 100 launch** (`ops/press/`): a press release in PT, EN and FR (the data is the story: 308 municipalities, ten years, the method), a data note with five findings and their sources, six images with the number on them (prompt 46 standard), the brand files. Spokesperson: "a porta-voz da Imóveis Portugal", no names. The media list per country (ECO, Expresso, Público, Jornal de Negócios, Idealista News; Contacto, Lusojornal, Bom Dia; Le Temps; Estadão, Folha; the Portuguese-American press; Globes, Haaretz English) with newsroom contact pages only.
3. **"Instituições" slot in the footer and on the Parceiros page**, empty until a consent row exists; the design ready so a logo goes live the day it is allowed.
