# Prompt 75: Premium destinations first, not municipalities

Operator, 4 Nov 2026: "Why focus on Loulé and random spots? People know the premium spots, not those random places that just fill space and are trashy." And: "Not just the map. The article descriptions etc. describe random-ass spots."

Buyers think in destinations (Comporta, Vilamoura, Quinta do Lago, Cascais, Foz), not in concelhos (Loulé, Alcácer do Sal, Grândola). Our map, cards and simulator are organised by the 308 concelhos because that is how INE publishes prices. The data can stay that way underneath; what the reader sees must not.

Run after prompt 74. Goes live under D67. Follow content/METHOD.md. Record it as the next free D number.

## 1. The destination list (show it to the operator before building)
1. **Research the Portuguese destinations international buyers and the prime-property press actually name:** Knight Frank, Sotheby's, Savills, FT How To Spend It, Condé Nast Traveller, the NYT.
2. **A starting point to check, not a final list:**
   - Lisboa (Chiado, Príncipe Real, Lapa), Cascais, Estoril, Sintra;
   - Comporta, Melides;
   - Porto, Foz do Douro, the Douro Valley;
   - Vilamoura, Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo, Lagos;
   - Madeira (Funchal).
3. **For each destination, give:**
   - its concelho and freguesia (for the data);
   - one line on why buyers know it;
   - the source.
4. **Send the list to the operator and wait for his yes** before section 2.

## 2. What the reader sees
1. **Map, opening view:** the destinations as named pins with a photograph. The 308 concelhos remain as the data layer and in search, but they are not what the eye lands on.
2. **Destination cards:** they replace the concelho card wherever a destination exists (Vilamoura, not Loulé; Comporta, not Alcácer do Sal). Same pattern as prompt 68: arrival, the developments, one-line answers, one more thing.
3. **Prices:** use the most local official figure available, the freguesia if INE publishes it, otherwise the concelho. Label it in small type ("INE, freguesia de Almancil" or "INE, concelho de Loulé").
4. **Simulator, "Onde":** destination names only ("Comporta", "Vilamoura", "Quinta do Lago", "Cascais", "Foz do Douro"…), with no concelho subtitles.
5. **Prompt 72's gems move to their destinations:**
   - Vilamoura: the marina, the Els Club and the PGA Tour;
   - Falésia: on the Vilamoura card or its own pin;
   - Portimão (F1) and Senhor Frango: as places to go from the Algarve destinations, not as cards of their own.
6. **Developments sit in their destination:** O'rizon and Marble Terrace stay on Gondomar's card and reachable from the map; no development is hidden by this change.
7. **Concelhos that are not destinations stay searchable and reachable from a development,** but get no pin of their own on the opening view.

## 3. Everywhere else, not just the map
1. **Audit every piece of content that names a place,** in PT, EN and FR:
   - articles and their titles, descriptions, front matter and meta descriptions;
   - the home page, edition pages and examples in the simulators and step by step;
   - the FAQ examples and the Maré issues;
   - the place pages, social exports and guides.
2. **Lead with the approved destinations.** Wherever a random concelho is used as an example or a headline, replace it with a destination people know, or remove the mention.
3. **Articles built around a place that is not a destination:**
   - rewrite them around the nearest destination where it reads naturally;
   - otherwise unlist them (`draft: true`, not deleted, out of the sitemap and lists);
   - Gondomar stays because O'rizon and Marble Terrace are there.
4. **Report a table:** the piece, the place it named, and what it names now (or "unlisted").

## Acceptance (on main, phone and desktop, PT, EN and FR)
1. The opening view shows the approved destinations, named and photographed. No municipality names like Loulé, Alcácer do Sal or Grândola appear unless the reader searches for them.
2. Tapping Vilamoura opens a Vilamoura card with Arrow's developments, the gems and the right price source.
3. The simulator's "Onde" shows destination names only.
4. No article title, description or example names a non-destination concelho, except where a development sits.
5. The James, Mike and Ana agents (prompts 70 and 72) find the places they already know within 10 seconds.
6. The checks and the build pass.
