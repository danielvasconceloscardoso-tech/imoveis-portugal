## The bar
"Imagine Claridge's and Steve Jobs created a newsletter." In practice:
1. **A calm four-minute coffee, on a phone,** that makes the reader look forward to the next issue.
2. **One idea per screen.** Photography an Apple keynote would use. Type and spacing with the restraint of a Claridge's menu card. Nothing decorative.
3. **Every issue teaches** a well-travelled, well-read reader something they did not know about the place: who comes from there, what is made there, what it costs to live there, where to eat. Gems and insights, not tourism copy.
4. **Every issue has a game** the reader plays in one tap and is rewarded for the same day.
5. **It holds full attention from the first line** and leaves the reader in awe.

Test: would the editor of How To Spend It or Monocle be jealous of it? If not, it is not done.

3. **The copy:**

4. **Polls answered "on Friday", and any streak that takes weeks to pay out.**
5. **O'rizon in Maré.** Prompt 66 and D91: no newsletter mention until Shalu signs.

## Step 5: The 1 December issue, Gondomar: the story plan

## Step 6: The copy

2. **Method:** Mariana van Zeller's. Pick one thread and follow it to the source with primary data, names, places and numbers. Here that thread is the gold: from the bench of a one-person workshop, to the hallmark at the assay office, to a shop window in Hong Kong. Then the people and businesses that make the place.
3. **Rules:**
   - 50/50 property and lifestyle;
   - talk to the reader (VOICE.md rule 3);
   - no history lessons, no flourishes, no invented people; real business stories are welcome;
   - Gondomar only.
4. **Research the place properly before writing.** Sources: the Câmara's honours (medalhas de mérito), Wikipedia PT, Infopédia, Gulbenkian, Serralves, local press (JN), INE and Jornal de Negócios. List people, companies, brands, artists, chefs and athletes from Gondomar, each with a source, and pick the best.
5. **Leads already verified** (check them again):
   - **Zulmiro de Carvalho:** sculptor, born in Gondomar in 1940. St Martin's in London on a Gulbenkian grant (1971-73), where he met Anthony Caro. Monumental works in South Korea and Macau, a piece in the CAM Gulbenkian collection, founding president of the Júlio Resende foundation.

## Step 7: The game: a rush every Tuesday and Friday, prizes the same day
The operator wants readers to feel a rush, with prizes that are free for us (no cash cost) and worth having for them.
1. **Research first.** Search online for the games and rewards that work best and say why:
   - newsletter games and referral programmes (Morning Brew, The Hustle, Axios, The Skimm);
   - NYT Games and Wordle, Duolingo streaks, the Robinhood free-stock lottery, scratch cards, mystery boxes;
   - airline and hotel loyalty tiers.
   
   Extract the mechanics: instant feedback, variable rewards, near-misses, scarcity ("the first 50"), streaks, levels, rankings by edition, sharing a result.
2. **Prizes free for us,** at least ten ideas, ranked. For example:
   - digital: a market analysis of the concelho the reader chooses, generated from our data; early access; the reader's name in the next issue;
   - given by partners in exchange for exposure: a filigree piece from a Gondomar workshop, a Topázio piece, a dinner, a hotel night, a session with a partner;
   - status: a reader level that unlocks something.
   
   Partner prizes are arranged by the CEO: list who to ask and what they get.
3. **The rules of play:**
   - the reader answers in the email with one tap;
   - the page that opens carries the run on;
   - the prize is unlocked the same day, never "on Friday";
   - a run of five correct in one sitting is the baseline. Improve on it with what the research shows.
4. **Manners:** the rush comes from the stakes and the reveal, not from noise. No confetti, no red alerts, no fake countdowns.
5. **The questions are about the place and the market in the issue,** so playing teaches. The opening riddle (step 5) is question one. The photo challenge can be one of them.
6. **Signals and data (DESIGN_AND_DATA.md):**
   - every answer, run, level and prize claimed feeds the consented profile;

   - update the privacy policy in the same pass;
   - verify beehiiv's merge tag for the subscriber id, and that one tap works in Gmail, Apple Mail and Outlook;
