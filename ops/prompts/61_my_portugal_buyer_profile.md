# Prompt 61: "O meu Portugal", the buyer profile (D79)

CEO, 2 Nov 2026, 23:25. The buyer builds their Portugal plan with us, at their pace, and feels it is theirs. Light, optimistic, never intrusive. For us it is the most valuable asset we have: qualified, consented buyers (prompt 60).

## 1. The feeling (operator, 2 Nov)
1. The buyer owns the process. They decide what to share, when, and with whom.
2. **Nobody contacts them unless they chose it:** no calls, no partner emails, no "a consultant will contact you". Contact happens only when the buyer taps a named partner in that moment ("Falar com o advogado"), and they pick the channel and the time.
3. Optimistic tone: it's their plan for a life in Portugal, not a form. Never "complete your profile", never a score, never a red warning.

## 2. How it fills (no long form)
1. It assembles itself from what they already do: the quiz (regions), the budget simulator (budget), the Concierge (timing, financing, who comes), the dossier (purpose, country). The buyer confirms with one tap.
2. One question at a time, always skippable, with the reason shown in the Concierge voice.
3. Each answer gives something back at once: budget → the homes that fit and the full cost; country → the tax at home; financing → illustrative scenarios; timing → their calendar of steps.
4. A light refresh: once a quarter the letter asks one tap ("Ainda é para 2027?").

## 3. What the buyer sees
1. "O meu Portugal": the regions they love, the homes they saved, their budget and full cost, their calendar, their team (the partners they chose).
2. "Os meus passos": NIF, budget, financing check, regions, lawyer, visit, documents; ticked as they go. Progress, never a score.
3. Badges that help them: "NIF obtido", "Pré-análise bancária feita", "Visita marcada". Shared only if they share.
4. Download as PDF, share a link with a partner they chose, edit, delete.

## 4. Data
1. We keep the data on our own platform (Cloudflare D1 or KV, encrypted, EU), never in the repo (hard rule 3). Partners get only what the buyer shares, when they share it.
2. Consent: one unticked consent for the profile; one per named partner when the buyer shares (GDPR arts. 6(1)(a), 7, 13). Aggregated, anonymised data for our research and products (Recital 26). The right to erasure and portability stays (arts. 17, 20): offer it plainly.
3. Credit and mediation steps go to the licensed partner group, disclosed to the buyer (DL 81-C/2017; Lei 15/2013).

## 5. Pricing (D79)
Developers pay per unit (rate card v5). Every other partner signs an annual or 6-month agreement paid upfront. No quarterly packages: remove any from the rate card, the partnerships file and the papers.

## 6. Ship
Portuguese first, per D67. Show the operator the screens (mobile) before the data layer goes live. Reply in the chat in plain text, one line per step, with the live link.
