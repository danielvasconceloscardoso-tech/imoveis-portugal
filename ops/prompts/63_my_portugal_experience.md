# Prompt 63: "O meu Portugal", the experience (supersedes the screens in prompt 61; data and consent rules there still apply)

CEO, 3 Nov 2026, 00:20. Operator: the lightest, most fun way to build the buyer profile, Apple-like, with the feeling of a private assistant (Alfred to Bruce Wayne): it anticipates, never asks twice, never bothers. Every tap gives the reader something beautiful back; the profile fills itself as a side effect.

## 1. Principles
1. One thing per screen, big type, real photographs, one thumb. Motion 200–300 ms, soft springs, dark mode, light haptics where the browser allows.
2. No forms. Taps, swipes and sliders only. Typing only for the e-mail, once, when they choose to save.
3. Every answer is rewarded at once: a photo, a fact, a number, a home.
4. Value before identity: they play and explore without an account. "Guardar o meu Portugal" comes when they have something worth keeping. Sign-in by magic link (no password).
5. Quiet by default: no calls, no partner messages, no pushes. Only the letter, if they want it.

## 2. The moments (each one fills a field)
1. **"A vista da sua janela"**: four full-screen photos (mar, rio, vinha, cidade); tap one → lifestyle.
2. **"Onde se vê"**: swipe region cards (right = gosto, left = passo), each with a photo and one line in the voice of content/VOICE_AND_WORLD.md → ranked regions (up to three).
3. **"O que o seu orçamento compra"**: a slider; as it moves, the homes and the full cost change live in each chosen region → budget band.
4. **"O primeiro Natal em Portugal"**: pick the season and year on a simple strip ("verão de 2027") → timing.
5. **"Quem vem consigo"**: icons (a dois, família, sozinho, amigos); if família, optional ages → household and schools.
6. **"Casa para quê"**: viver, férias, um dia voltar, investir → type of purchase.
7. **"A sua equipa"**: cards appear only when relevant (a bank card after the budget, a lawyer card after a saved home, a school card after the kids' ages). Tapping one is the only way a partner ever hears from them, through the Concierge, at the time they choose → partners needed.

## 3. The assistant
1. It notices and offers, one line at a time, never more than once: "Guardou três casas no Porto. Quer ver as escolas a dez minutos?" / "O Douro está em vindima em setembro. Marco no seu calendário?"
2. It remembers: never asks what it already knows from the quiz, the map, the simulator or the Concierge.
3. Tone: a great hotel concierge. Brief, warm, discreet. No exclamation marks, no emojis.

## 4. What they see
1. **O cartão**: one beautiful card that fills as they go (region photo, budget, season, household, their team), shareable as an image ("O meu Portugal: Porto, verão de 2027"). Later, an Apple/Google Wallet pass once a sponsor has paid (hard rule 5).
2. **A mesa**: saved homes, places and roteiros laid out like a mood board.
3. **Os passos**: their path as a quiet timeline (NIF, banco, advogado, visita, escritura), ticked as they go. Progress, never a score.
4. Edit or delete anything, download it as a PDF, at any time.

## 5. Data and consent (from the operator, 3 Nov)
1. Fields stored only in the consented profile (prompt 61 §4) and in the anonymised statistics store (prompt 60). Never in the repo.
2. Partner sharing only when the buyer taps a named partner card (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)); no list of partners anywhere. Statistics anonymised and aggregated (Recital 26). Privacy policy lists recipient categories (art. 13(1)(e)), names on request. One unticked consent when they save the profile.
3. Measure (anonymously): completion of each moment, drop-off, the share rate of the card.

2. Then build per D67, Portuguese only. Reply in plain text, one line per step, with links.
