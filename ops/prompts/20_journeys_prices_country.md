## 2. "O seu percurso": one journey per country, with checkpoints

For each country, one page that walks the reader from idea to keys, then to renting, selling and passing it on. Each checkpoint: the questions people from that country actually ask, the verified answer (facts.csv, primary sources), and a **partner slot for that step and that country**.

Checkpoints (adapt wording per country):
1. Budget and place: the map, price trends and yields by area
2. Financing: bank in Portugal or at home, how much can be borrowed, moving the money (currency)
3. Documents: NIF, whether a Portuguese bank account is needed (verify: legal requirement vs practice), proof of funds, power of attorney
4. The purchase: CPCV, deed, notary or Casa Pronta, buying without travelling
5. Taxes when buying: IMT (including the non-resident rate and exceptions), stamp duty, registration
6. Owning: IMI, AIMI, insurance, management
7. Renting it out: tax in Portugal (CIRS art. 72.º) and at home under the tax treaty
8. Selling: capital gains in Portugal (non-residents) and at home under the treaty
9. Residence and structuring (1 M€+ budgets): residence options, IFICI, inheritance and holding questions, Private section

Example for Luxembourg: "Preciso de conta num banco português?", "Quanto pago de impostos quando vender?", "Também pago impostos no Luxemburgo?", "E se arrendar?" Each answer ends with the matching partner slot: Luxembourg tax adviser, Portuguese lawyer, bank, money transfer, international wealth adviser, the agency.

Where a fact can't be verified from a primary source, leave the question with "Pergunte a um parceiro" and list it for Daniel.
