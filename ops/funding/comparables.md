# Comparables: who has already been funded for projects like ours

Prompt 50 §2. Research done 2026-11-01 (all sources checked that day). Only records seen in an official list or database are included. Amounts are copied from the record. "Eligible" means the eligible cost; "grant" means the fund approved (EU plus national public share where the list says so). "not stated" means the record has no figure.

## Patterns

1. **The line that funds platforms like ours most often is PRR C16-i02, run by IAPMEI and Startup Portugal.**
   - **Vouchers para Startups.** A flat **30,000 €** grant per startup, and no eligible cost appears in the list. Paid records show 25,000 € paid first and the rest at the end. There have been three calls: 10/C16-i02/2022, 16/C16-i02/2023 and 21/C16-i02/2025. Web sources say the 3rd call is called "Novos produtos digitais/tecnológicos", has 30 M€ in total and is for SMEs under 10 years old; we did not open the PDF.
   - **Internacionalização via E-commerce.** Awards fall in bands: about **12,500 €** (12,450, 11,775), 30,000 € and **42,500 €** (40,428.75; 42,499.50).
   - We found at least 9 real-estate and proptech startups funded under these lines (rows 2–11). PRR ends in 2026, so the open question for §1 is the successor line.
2. **PRR C05-i14.01, "IA nas empresas" (2025–26 calls), pays more.** The range is **16,000–300,000 €**. Hubzome got 300,000 € on 407,500 € eligible (74%). There are at least 5 real-estate cases (Listoo 161,847.75; Omrei; Pacific Front; Hubzome; Vogue Homes 217,407.57).
3. **The PT2030 lines are larger but co-financed.**
   - **R&D and innovation calls (MPr-2024-12, MPr-2025-4)** pay about 64–75% on 300–480k € eligible. They suit the index, data pipeline and simulators if those are framed as R&D.
   - **SI Internacionalização PME (MPr-2024-7, MPr-2025-16)** pays about 40% of eligible cost in most records. In the Norte 2025 records it pays 50%, and one record (Sailing 360) is capped at 150,000 €.
   - **Regional SME calls (ALGARVE-2023-14)** pay about 40%.
4. **Small, early money in 2014–2020 came from "vales"** (20k eligible → 15k grant, 75%) and from **SI2E or +CO3SO** job-creation grants (about 10–110k €, 50–85%). These lines are closed. Their PT2030 equivalents need checking in §1.
5. **Collective internationalisation for diaspora and foreign-buyer marketing goes to associations, not to firms.** Examples: APR's "Living in Portugal", AEP's Diáspora network funded three times in a row, and Saveurs du Portugal in FR/LU/CH. Rates run at about 80–85%. For us, the route is a partnership with such an association (for example a chamber or AEP), not an application of our own.
6. **The wording that wins:**
   - "plataforma digital" or "SaaS", together with a named technology: AI, LLM, big data, machine learning, matching, webSIG or geospatial data, digital twin;
   - "transparência do mercado imobiliário";
   - **named target markets**: Frontfiles listed Brazil, Argentina, Canada and Israel; Biscayne listed 19 countries;
   - "multilingue", "escalabilidade", "concorrentes internacionais";
   - for the vouchers, a **green angle** (fewer trips and visits, renovating vacant housing, energy certificates).
7. **What reviewers seem to reward:**
   - an existing product with first users ("cerca de 20 clientes");
   - clear export markets;
   - measurable digital transition;
   - for PT2030 R&D, a technical novelty claim tied to an EU framework (OWL cites Level(s));
   - for PT2030 internationalisation, a structured plan: prospecting, brand, fairs, digital marketing, certification.
8. **Big incumbents have no grant record.** Casafari, Idealista, Imovirtual/OLX, Confidencial Imobiliário (also searched as "Imometric"), Uniplaces, Homeit, Housers and Spotahome do not appear under those names in the P20, P30, PRR or Kohesio beneficiary lists. Their growth was privately funded, and public money in this sector goes to small, young firms.

## What we could not verify, and unreachable sources

- **recuperarportugal.gov.pt** fails TLS through the session proxy (`curl: (60) unable to get local issuer certificate`). We used the PRR open data on dados.gov.pt and transparencia.gov.pt instead.
- **portugalventures.pt** loads its home page, but the portfolio and search pages time out or return 503. Its full proptech portfolio and ticket sizes are unverified. Unlockit is confirmed only through AICEP.
- **WebFetch** can't reach portugalglobal.pt or eaglestone.eu: their hosts don't resolve in this session. curl reached portugalglobal.pt.
- **Startup Portugal** publishes no separate list of voucher beneficiaries. The PRR C16-i02 records above are the beneficiary data.
- **Name matching.**
  - The negative result for the big incumbents rests on company names; their legal names could differ.
  - "Hubzome" is Zome: hubzome.pt redirects to zome.pt, whose privacy policy names HUBZome, Lda (Braga); zome.pt calls zomeit.com its consultants' portal (checked 2026-11-01, ops/learn/research_C.md). Shared shareholders not verified (paywalled).
- **Inferred call names.** The PT2020 call typologies (SI-47 = I&DT, SI-52 = internationalisation, SI-53 = qualification) are inferred from the codes. The title of the 3rd voucher call comes from a web search snippet. We did not open the call PDF.
- **Coverage.**
  - Kohesio's keyword search does not index beneficiary names, and its 2021–2027 data is partial.
  - The PT2030 list stops at 30-09-2026.
- **No eligible cost in PRR.** The PRR lists give the approved support only, not the eligible cost, except where a summary states it.
