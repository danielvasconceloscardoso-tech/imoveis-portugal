# Funding research: sources 1 to 5 (prompt 50, §1)

Checked 2026-11-01. Company profile used: Portuguese Lda, incorporated November 2026 in the Porto area (NUTS II Norte, AMP), two managers, no employees, data and services platform (web portal, data, advertising; not a press title, D71), six markets, launch 1 Dec 2026.

Method: official sites only. The Portugal 2030 call list was read through the portal's own call API (`portugal2030.pt/wp-json/avisos/query`, the source behind https://portugal2030.pt/avisos/). Call PDFs were downloaded from the same portal. "Effort" figures are our own estimates, marked "est.". Every other figure comes from the cited page.

## Summary for the CEO

1. **Portugal 2030 has nothing we can apply to before Q1 2027, and possibly nothing for all of 2027.** The two lines that fit a platform like ours are SICE Qualificação (digital, trademarks, certification, marketing) and SICE Internacionalização (fairs, international marketing, web platforms). Both had their last individual call close in 2025 or early 2026, and neither appears as an individual call in the published Plano Anual de Avisos (Sep 2026 to Aug 2027). Their minimum eligible spend is €200k. SICE Inovação Produtiva closes on 6 Nov 2026 and needs at least €300k of eligible spend. SI Empreendedorismo (Norte 2030), the only start-up line, closed in March 2025 and needs an R&D-origin spin-off.
2. **The PRR is closing.** The startup vouchers and incubator vouchers are closed, and the Government has moved Recuperar Portugal into a closure phase that only finishes projects already approved.
3. **The realistic sources now are not grants.** They are BPF mutual-guarantee loans (Impulsar Portugal, "Novos Negócios" sub-line, up to €1M per company with an 80% guarantee, open until 31 Dec 2028) and startup status under Lei 21/2023 (for tax and programme access). Equity from Portugal Ventures (tickets up to €6M) or BPF co-investment only comes once a private investor leads.

## 1. Portugal 2030: Norte 2030 and COMPETE 2030

**Who pays for a small company in Norte (programme split).** The split differs by line:
- **Inovação Produtiva:** Norte 2030 pays for operations of €3M or less located in Norte. COMPETE pays above €3M or for operations in several regions.
- **Qualificação and Internacionalização (individual):** COMPETE 2030 pays for every operation in Norte, Centro and Alentejo.

These rules are quoted from each call's "Delimitação entre Programas" section (calls MPr-2026-6, MPr-2024-4 and MPr-2025-16).

**Annual call plan.** The Plano Anual de Avisos (published 14 Sep 2026) covers Sep 2026 to Aug 2027: 172 calls, about €2.03 billion. It lists only 4 NORTE2030 calls, none of them for company investment.

### 1a. SICE – Inovação Produtiva (MPr-2026-6, "Territórios Baixa Densidade e Outros Territórios")
- **What it is.** Grant (subvenção, real costs) for productive innovative investment. Funded by Norte 2030 (€7.5M fund, of which €4.5M is for "outros territórios") alongside COMPETE and the other regions.
- **Amount and rate.** Porto/AMP is "Outros Territórios": the maximum rate is 30% for micro and small companies. Base rate 30 pp. Bonuses (Transição Climática +5 pp, qualified jobs +2 or +5 pp, Capitalização PME +5 pp) cannot exceed that cap. Minimum eligible spend is €300,000, maximum under €25M.
  - Eligible costs: equipment, IT and software, intangibles (software "standard ou desenvolvido especificamente"), and, for SMEs only, marketing plans, studies, engineering and certified accountant (TOC/ROC) costs.
  - At least 25% equity is required by the first payment.
  - Payment: advance of up to 10%, advances against invoice or against guarantee, then reimbursement.
- **Deadline.** Opened 15/06/2026. The 27.10.2026 republication gives the close as 06/11/2026 at 17h. The portal API lists the call as "Fechado" with end date 30/10/2026, which contradicts the PDF.
- **Eligible for us: no.** The company will not be incorporated, certified as an SME and registered on the Balcão dos Fundos by 6 Nov. Our plan is also well below €300k of eligible spend.
- **Next call:** not stated. There is no Norte or COMPETE individual Inovação Produtiva call in the Sep 2026–Aug 2027 plan; the only Inovação Produtiva entry is the Algarve RCI.
- **Effort:** est. 60–100 h. **Time to cash:** decision "tempoMedioDecisaoFinal" 60 working days (API); then up to a 10% advance after signing the acceptance and proving the project has started.
- **Publicity:** see 1h.
- **Source:** Aviso MPr-2026-6, republished 27.10.2026, on https://portugal2030.pt/avisos/ (checked 2026-11-01).

### 1b. SICE – Qualificação das PME, individual operations (last call MPr-2024-4)

- **Effort:** est. 50–80 h. **Time to cash:** 60 working days to decision (API). **Publicity:** see 1h.
- **Source:** Aviso MPr-2024-4 (amended 29.07.2024), https://portugal2030.pt/avisos/ (checked 2026-11-01).

### 1c. SICE – Internacionalização das PME, individual operations (last call MPr-2025-16)

### 1d. SI Empreendedorismo (Norte 2030, NORTE2030-2024-87): the only Norte line for start-ups
- **What it is.** Grant for creating new companies out of EU-co-funded R&D projects.
- **Amount and rate.** 75% up to €500,000 per project. Eligible spend from €100k up to €3M. Initial advance of 10%.
- **Conditions.**
  - The company must be between 6 months and 3 years old.
  - TRL 8–9.
  - The promoter must have led or co-promoted a completed EU-co-funded R&D project.
  - Paid-up capital of at least €25,000.
  - At least one employee qualified at level VI or above.
  - R&D costs of at least 20% of operating costs.
- **Deadline.** 31/12/2024 to 31/03/2025. Closed.
- **Eligible for us: no.** We have no prior funded R&D project, and the age, staff and R&D-cost conditions also fail.
- **Successor:** none for Norte in the Sep 2026–Aug 2027 plan. Similar lines are planned only in Alentejo (SIID Empreendedorismo Qualificado, 01/10/2026–12/02/2027) and Algarve (SIAC Empreendedorismo, 30/09/2026–15/01/2027).
- **Source:** Aviso NORTE2030-2024-87, republished 02/2025, https://portugal2030.pt/avisos/ (checked 2026-11-01).

### 1h. Publicity obligations (all Portugal 2030 grants)
The calls (for example MPr-2026-10, page 19, and MPr-2026-11) require compliance with:
- Regulation (EU) 2021/1060, "designadamente nos artigos 47º, 50º e Anexo IX";
- Decreto-Lei 20-A/2023, art. 15;
- the managing authority's specifications;
- the "Guia de Regras de Comunicação para Beneficiários".

In practice, "Seja qual for o valor da operação" (whatever the operation's value), the beneficiary must:
- apply the **co-financing bar** (barra de cofinanciamento) on its site and materials;
- publish the **operation sheet** (ficha de operação);
- display an **A3 poster or electronic panel**;
- make a **video** if the operation's eligible cost exceeds €500,000.

Approved operations are published on the funding programmes' websites. For us this means EU, Portugal 2030 and programme logos on the website for the life of the operation. Checked 2026-11-01. The regulation's own text on EUR-Lex could not be reached (see the end of this file).

**Prerequisites for every Portugal 2030 line** (stated in the calls):
- electronic SME certificate from IAPMEI;
- organised accounting;
- Balcão dos Fundos registration;
- tax and social security in good standing (checked before every payment);
- "registo do pedido de auxílio" before starting work (incentive effect). Costs incurred before the application are not eligible, except preparatory work.

## 3. Startup Portugal

### 3a. Startup status (Lei 21/2023, Portaria 401/2023)
- **Requirements:**
  - constituted in Portugal;
  - under 10 years old, under 250 staff, under €50M turnover;
  - not majority-owned by a large company;
  - **one of**: (i) innovative company recognised by ANI (R&D aptitude or "certificação do processo de reconhecimento de empresas do setor da tecnologia"); (ii) at least one completed venture-capital round; (iii) investment from BPF or funds it manages.
  - Companies that meet everything except that last condition can ask Startup Portugal for a **"Declaração Prévia"**.
  - Application through gov.pt. Documents: declaration of start of activity, IES, certidão permanente, plus the proof above.
- **What it unlocks:**
  - IFICI: 20% IRS rate for 10 years for qualifying hires who lived abroad for at least 5 years.
  - Stock-option regime: "taxa única efectiva de 14% em IRS", paid only when liquidity occurs.
  - Access to Startup Portugal programmes (Business Abroad, Road 2 Web Summit, SIM showcase).
  - "Majorações" (bonus rates) in other support schemes (example given: Mar2030).
- **Eligible for us: after X.** We need ANI recognition, a VC or angel round, or BPF investment, or failing those the Declaração Prévia. Exactly when after incorporation we can apply is not stated: an IES is listed among the documents, and a new company has none until its first year-end.
- **Next date:** continuous. **Effort:** est. 4–8 h (Declaração Prévia route). **Time to decision:** not stated. **Publicity:** none stated. **Cash:** none directly.
- **Source:** https://startupportugal.com/pt/estatuto-startup/ (checked 2026-11-01). The law's own text on diariodarepublica.pt could not be read (see the end of this file).

### 3b. StartUP Voucher (IAPMEI; "StartUP Voucher Innovate", funded by COMPETE 2030)
- **What it is.** For young people up to 29 with a degree, tax-resident in Norte, Centro or Alentejo, not studying, working or earning other income, with a project "ainda em fase de ideia" (still at the idea stage).
- **Support:** €900 a month for 9 months (maximum 2 grants per project), two interim prizes of €1,500, a final prize of €2,000.
- **Calls:** the 3rd phase closed on 25/08/2026; an earlier edition closed 30/01/2026. Next call not stated.
- **Eligible for us: no.** The company already exists and the managers do not meet the profile.
- **Sources:** https://www.gov.pt/noticias/candidaturas-ao-startup-voucher-innovate-ate-25-de-agosto (published 14/07/2026) and https://startupportugal.com/pt/programs/startup-voucher-2/ (checked 2026-11-01).
