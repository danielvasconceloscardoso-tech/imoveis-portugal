## 0. De minimis: Regulation (EU) 2023/2831 (confirmed)

- **Ceiling.** Art. 3(2): "The total amount of de minimis aid granted per Member State to a single undertaking shall not exceed EUR 300 000 over any period of 3 years." Art. 3(3): aid counts from the date the legal right to it is conferred, not from the date it is paid. Art. 3(4): this applies whatever the form of the aid and whether or not EU money funds it.
- **Single undertaking.** Art. 2(2): all enterprises linked by (a) a majority of voting rights, (b) the right to appoint or remove a majority of the board, (c) dominant influence by contract or articles, or (d) control of a majority of votes through an agreement with other shareholders. Links through other enterprises count too. The text speaks of *enterprises*. A partner's other company counts only if one of these control links exists.
- **Period.** In force from 1 Jan 2024, applies until 31 Dec 2030 (Art. 8).
- **Register.** Member States must report de minimis aid to a central register from 1 Jan 2026 at the latest (recital). Once the register exists, companies no longer have to declare the aid themselves.
- **Source.** Official Journal L 2023/2831, 15.12.2023, fetched from the Publications Office Cellar: https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/75417f3b-9aec-11ee-b164-01aa75ed71a1.0006.03/DOC_1 (CELEX 32023R2831). eur-lex.europa.eu itself returned an AWS WAF challenge (HTTP 202). Checked 2026-11-01.

## 6. IAPMEI and IEFP

### 6.1 Certificação PME (IAPMEI)
- **What it is.** Free electronic certificate that the company is a micro, small or medium enterprise. Most incentive systems require it.
- **Amount.** None; it is a prerequisite. Cost: "O serviço é gratuito."
- **Time.** "A decisão ... é disponibilizada imediatamente após o preenchimento integral e subsequente submissão do formulário eletrónico." Steps: register the company (login = NIF), fill in the representative form, then submit the "Primeira Certificação" form.
- **New company.** Can certify on estimated headcount and financial figures. Those estimates must be confirmed through a "Confirmação de Estimativas" at the latest 30 working days after the legal deadline for the annual IES return. If not confirmed, the certification lapses.
- **Eligibility.** Yes, once the Lda has a NIF and Social Security number. The form asks for each partner's holdings and each partner's other companies (linked or partner enterprises).
- **Next call.** Continuous.
- **Effort.** 1–2 h (est.).
- **Time to cash.** Not applicable.
- **Publicity obligation.** None.

### 6.2 IEFP hiring incentives: successors of "Compromisso Emprego Sustentável"

The IEFP page of revoked measures states that **Compromisso Emprego Sustentável** was "revogada pela Portaria n.º 220/2024/1, de 23 de setembro". That Portaria created **Medida +Emprego**. Graduates aged 35 or under are covered by a sister measure, **Emprego +Talento** (Portaria 221/2024/1).

**Common rules for both measures:**
- Open-ended, full-time contract.
- Net job creation, kept for at least 24 months.
- Training is mandatory: either 3 months on the job with a tutor, or at least 40 h with a certified training provider.
- No dismissals in the 3 months before applying.
- Tax and Social Security status must be clear.
- Payment: 40% after the contracts start, 40% in month 13, 20% after 24 months.
- Cannot be combined with other direct support for the same job, including TSU (Social Security) exemptions.
- **Not valid** for hiring a partner of the company, or the spouse or partner of a partner or manager.
- Funded by PORTUGAL 2030, so the PT2030 communication rules apply.

**+Emprego**
- **Who can be hired.** Unemployed people registered with IEFP who meet one of the listed conditions (for example: registered for at least 3 months, aged 35 or under without a degree, aged 45 or over).
- **Amount.** 12 × IAS = **6,445.56 €** (IAS 2026 = 537.13 €). Each uplift adds 35% (2,255.95 €), up to 4 uplifts, for a maximum of **15,469.34 €**. Uplifts apply for: a young person aged 35 or under, long-term unemployed, a person with a disability, an under-represented gender in the profession, or a job in an interior territory.
- **Eligibility for us.** After the first hire.
- **Next call.** The 2026 window (15 Jul–15 Dec 2026) **closed early on 26 Oct 2026** because the budget ran out. The next window is "definidos anualmente": date not stated.
- **Effort.** 6–10 h (est.).
- **Time to cash.** First 40% "após o início de todos os contratos ... no prazo máximo de 20 dias úteis" after the Termo de Aceitação. The time to the approval decision is not stated.
- **Publicity obligation.** PT2030 co-financing notice (see IEFP "Portugal 2030 / Regras de Comunicação").

**Emprego +Talento**
- **Who can be hired.** Unemployed young people aged 35 or under, registered with IEFP, holding a degree at QNQ level 6–8. This **includes young people who emigrated permanently at least 12 months ago**.
- **Salary.** Base salary at least **1,499.15 €** (2026).
- **Amount.** 18 × IAS = **9,668.34 €**. Each uplift adds 35% (3,383.92 €), up to 4 uplifts.
- **Eligibility for us.** After the first hire, at a salary of 1,499.15 € or more.
- **Next call.** The 2026 window **closed early on 21 Oct 2026** (budget reached). Next window: not stated.
- **Effort.** 6–10 h (est.).
- **Time to cash.** Same payment schedule as +Emprego.
- **Publicity obligation.** As above.

## 7. Tax

### 7.1 SIFIDE II (R&D tax credit; ANI)
- **Status.** Extended by **Decreto-Lei 170/2026 of 21 Aug** (under the authorisation in Lei 13/2026). New CFI art. 35: "O SIFIDE II, a vigorar nos períodos de tributação de 2014 a 2026". The same decree-law **revoked the indirect route through SIFIDE investment funds**. ANI's own guides still say "até 2025" (they are out of date).
- **Amount and rate (CFI art. 38).**
  - Base rate: 32.5% of R&D expenses.
  - Incremental rate: 50% of the increase over the average of the two previous years, capped at 1.5 M€.
  - SMEs that have not yet completed two financial years and have not used the incremental rate get a **15% uplift on the base rate**.
  - The credit is deducted from IRC up to the IRC due. Any excess carries forward "até ao décimo segundo período seguinte" (art. 38(4)).
  - Only the share of expenses not covered by a non-repayable state grant counts.
  - Eligible expenses include R&D staff (doctorates at 120%) and running costs up to 55% of staff costs.
- **Can our index method, data pipeline and simulators qualify?** Only partly, and only if framed as R&D. ANI applies the Frascati Manual: work must be **new, creative, uncertain, systematic and transferable**. ANI excludes "a recolha de dados de interesse geral (por exemplo inquéritos ou estudos de mercado)" and "atividades de natureza rotineira relacionadas com o desenvolvimento ou manutenção de software". "O uso de software para uma nova aplicação ou finalidade não constitui em si mesmo um progresso." Software counts as R&D only "sempre que se produza um avanço no campo da informática" or when it resolves a technological uncertainty.
  - **Data pipeline:** no.
  - **Simulators:** no, unless there is new method work.
  - **Index method:** possibly, if it solves a documented methodological problem (for example a hedonic or repeat-sales index across six markets with thin data) and the uncertainty is written up as such.
- **ANI certification.** Apply on sifide.ani.pt "até ao final do quinto mês do ano seguinte ao do exercício". For the 2026 period, that means by 31 May 2027.
- **Eligibility for us.** Yes in law for tax period 2026, but only for R&D spent from incorporation to 31 Dec 2026 (about 6 weeks). In practice the value is near zero: no IRC is due in year 1, so the credit just sits as a carry-forward. **2027 onwards: not stated.** No extension past 2026 is in force on the official texts we checked.
- **Next call.** By 31 May 2027 for the 2026 period.
- **Effort.** 20–40 h per application (est.).
- **Time to cash.** None directly; it only reduces IRC when there is profit.
- **Publicity obligation.** None.
- **Sources.**
  - https://ani.pt/sifide-sistema-de-incentivos-fiscais-a-ied-empresarial/
  - ANI evaluation guide: https://sifide.ani.pt/PDFViewer.aspx?FileId_In=93
  - DL 170/2026: https://files.diariodarepublica.pt/1s/2026/08/16200/0000300011.pdf
  - CFI art. 38: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cfi/Pages/cfi-38.aspx
  - Checked 2026-11-01.

### 7.2 RFAI (Regime Fiscal de Apoio ao Investimento; CFI arts. 22–26)
- **What it is.** An IRC deduction for investment, open to CFI art. 2(2) sectors, which include "c) Atividades e serviços informáticos e conexos" and "f) Tecnologias da informação e produção de audiovisual e multimédia". The exact CAE codes are in the portaria under art. 2(3), which we did not open.
- **Amount and rate.**
  - In art. 107(3)(a) regions: 30% of eligible investment up to 15 M€, 10% above that.
  - In (c) regions: 10%.
  - Eligible investment: new tangible fixed assets (excluding most buildings, passenger cars and furniture), technology-transfer intangibles, and salary costs of new jobs at QNQ level 7–8 (since Lei 82/2023).
  - In the start-up year and the next two years, the deduction can reach 100% of IRC due; after that, 50%. Unused amounts carry forward 10 periods.
- **Conditions.** Organised accounts; keep the assets for 3 years (SMEs); **"Efetuem investimento relevante que proporcione a criação de postos de trabalho e a sua manutenção"** (art. 22(4)(f)). It cannot be combined with other tax benefits on the same investments.
- **Eligibility for us.** **After the first hire, and only when there is IRC due.** Our capex is light (laptops; software built in house is not an acquired intangible). The value in year 1 is about 0.
- **Region.** The Norte rate depends on the 2022–2027 regional aid map table in CFI art. 43, which is published as an image and could not be read.
- **Next call.** None; claimed in the Modelo 22 tax return.
- **Effort.** 3–5 h with the accountant (est.).
- **Time to cash.** Only through lower IRC.
- **Publicity obligation.** None.
- **Sources.**
  - https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cfi/Pages/cfi-02.aspx
  - Same folder, cfi-22.aspx, cfi-23.aspx, cfi-24.aspx, cfi-43.aspx
  - Checked 2026-11-01.

### 7.3 IRC rates for small companies; is Porto or Gondomar "interior"?
- **Lei 64/2025 of 7 Nov (CIRC art. 87).**
  - SMEs and Small Mid Caps pay **15% on the first 50,000 €** of taxable profit, from tax periods starting on or after 1 Jan 2026.
  - General rate: **19% for 2026**, 18% for 2027, 17% from 2028.
- **Eligibility for us.** Yes, automatically, once we are an SME with a profit.
- **Source.** https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/lei_64_2025.pdf (AT copy of DR 216/2025). Checked 2026-11-01.
- **Interior.** **Neither Porto nor Gondomar is interior.** The official PNCT list (territories of the Programa Nacional para a Coesão Territorial, the basis for "território do interior" uplifts and benefits) names only three municipalities in Porto district: Amarante (some parishes), Baião (all parishes) and Marco de Canaveses (one parish union).
  - Source: IEFP copy of the list, https://www.iefp.pt/documents/10181/4287418/Territorios+PNCT_04-02-2026.pdf/8264433a-7568-4da5-be8b-3da1e009b86b. Checked 2026-11-01.
  - So the interior IRC benefit (EBF art. 41-B) and the interior uplifts in IEFP measures **do not apply** while we are seated in Porto or Gondomar.
  - We did not reread the EBF art. 41-B text (the Portal page URL returned 404).

### 7.4 Startup regime, Lei 21/2023 (stock options; EBF art. 43-C)
- **Startup definition (art. 2).** All of the following:
  - in business for less than 10 years;
  - fewer than 250 employees;
  - turnover of 50 M€ or less;
  - not a large-company spin-off;
  - seat in Portugal;
  - and one of: (i) innovative under Portaria 195/2018 or recognised by ANI; (ii) completed a VC or certified-business-angel round; (iii) received investment from BPF.
  - Without (i)–(iii), a prior declaration from **Startup Portugal** can substitute.
  - **Watch-out, art. 2(2):** the VC-round route "não abrange empresas de promoção, intermediação, investimento ou desenvolvimento imobiliário". Our CAE and object must read as data or software, not real-estate activity (consistent with D71 and D14).
- **Stock options (EBF art. 43-C, as worded by Lei 21/2023).**
  - Gains from employee stock or option plans are taxed on **50% of their value**, and only at a later event: sale, loss of residency or gift of the shares. The rights must be held for at least 1 year.
  - Applies when the grantor was a recognised startup in the year before the plan, or an SME or Small Mid Cap, or spends 10% or more on R&D.
  - The ≥20% holders and board members exclusion (art. 43-C(7)) **does not apply** when the grantor was a startup or micro or small company in the previous year (43-C(8)).
- **Eligibility for us.** After recognition, and only from the year after recognition. Relevant once we grant options to a first hire. It is not cash.
- **Next call.** Recognition is continuous, by prior communication to Startup Portugal.
- **Effort.** 3–6 h (est.).
- **Time to cash.** Not applicable.
- **Publicity obligation.** None.
- **Source.** https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/legislacao/diplomas_legislativos/Documents/lei_21_2023.pdf. Checked 2026-11-01. Later amendments to art. 43-C (OE2025/OE2026) were **not checked**.

## 9. Diaspora-linked

### 9.1 PNAID (Programa Nacional de Apoio ao Investimento da Diáspora) and GAID
- **What it is.** Approved by Council of Ministers resolution on 23 Jul 2020. In the government's words, it "Sistematiza[] medidas de apoio já existentes" and creates the **Estatuto de Investidor da Diáspora**, which gives access to "apoios e incentivos próprios com benefícios adicionais para investimentos no interior". It also produced a guide, a municipal network (the Rede de Apoio ao Investidor da Diáspora) and the Gabinete de Apoio ao Investidor da Diáspora (GAID).
- **Does it fund or co-fund projects that *inform* emigrants?** **No.** It has no grant line of its own. It is a coordination framework that adds uplifts to existing incentive systems for *investors from the diaspora*, mainly in interior territories.
- **Eligibility for us.** **No as a funder.** It could matter as a **partner or channel**: GAID and the municipal emigrant offices (GAE) could distribute our guides.
- **Amount, next call.** Not stated.
