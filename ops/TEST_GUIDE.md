# Imóveis Portugal: a guide for testing the site

**Link (private preview, please don't share it further):**
https://design-v2-imoveis-portugal.geralportugalimoveis.workers.dev/pt/lu/

Best on a phone and on a laptop, both. Use the language switch (PT · EN · FR) at the top right.

## What it is

Imóveis Portugal is a media and data brand for people who live abroad and want to buy, sell or inherit a home in Portugal. There are six editions: Luxembourg, France, Switzerland, Brazil, the US and Israel.

The site gives official prices for every municipality, the real cost of buying, and the steps to get there. When someone needs help, it introduces a licensed partner: lawyer, bank, tax adviser, or the partner agency.

It's free for readers. It earns from partner sponsorships and developers' placements, never from selling readers' data.

Launch is on 1 December 2026. This is a preview, so some parts are still being finished.

**The feeling we want:** the reception of a great hotel, calm and prestigious, easy to use, "Apple-like". Nothing should feel like filling in a Google form.

## Ten minutes: what to try

1. **Home page:**
   - Watch the film at the top for a few seconds.
   - In the bar underneath, choose where you live, buy or sell, and a budget, then press **Começar / Start**.
2. **"Passo a passo / Step by step":** follow the route a buyer takes. Does each step feel clear and one click away?
3. **The map** (menu → **Mapa**). It's the heart of the site.
   - Slide the **budget** and watch the places it reaches light up.
   - Tap a place (try Cascais, Comporta, Porto) and look at the card.
   - Use the **search** (magnifier, or ⌘K / Ctrl+K): type "Comp".
   - Switch **Day / Night**.
   - Try the chips: **Golfe**, **Praias**, **10 anos**, **Renda**.
   - Press play on the **2016 → 2026** time slider.
   - Run a **tour** ("Golfe no Algarve", "Madeira").
   - **Compare** two places.
4. **Simuladores / Calculators:** work out the total cost of a home, with taxes, in your currency.
5. **Perguntas / FAQ:** open two or three questions. Are the answers clear?
6. **Concierge** (button at the top right): see how a request would work. Don't send real personal data, it's a test.

## What to tell us

For each, a line is enough. Screenshots help.

1. **First impression in five seconds:** prestige, trust, "I want to belong"? Or not?
2. **Anything that felt slow, confusing, ugly, or like a form.**
3. **The map:** wow or meh? What would make it unmissable?
4. **Photos:** any that feel cheap, generic or wrong for the place?
5. **Words:** any sentence that sounds fake, AI-written or like a brochure.
6. **Phone vs laptop:** anything broken on one of them? Which device and browser?
7. **Would you trust it with your own purchase?** Why or why not?
