**What the repo claims (content/facts/facts.csv, pt-imt-nonresident-flat and pt-imt-nonresident-exceptions):**

1. A buyer who is not a Portuguese tax resident pays IMT at a flat 7.5% on an urban property used exclusively for housing, with no exemption or reduction (Código do IMT, art. 17.º, n.º 10, added by Decreto-Lei 97/2026 of 20 May).
2. In force from 25 May 2026 (the general five-day rule; DL 97/2026 has no specific date for this change). To confirm.
3. Exceptions: (a) the buyer has been considered a Portuguese tax resident; (b) the buyer becomes resident within two years of the purchase; (c) the home is let for housing within six months, at a rent within the DL 97/2026 caps, for at least 36 months in the first five years. In (b) and (c) the difference is refunded on request within six months.
4. Separately, 7.5% is also the flat rate for every buyer above 1 150 853 € (art. 17.º, n.º 1, Lei 73-A/2025 brackets). Mentions of that are tagged "top bracket".
