Two numbers for US clients buying a home in Portugal: 15 days and $10,000.

15 days: once they own property in Portugal, a US resident has 15 days to appoint a Portuguese tax representative or join the tax authority's electronic notification service. The US is outside the EU and the EEA, so one of the two is mandatory.

$10,000: a Portuguese bank account opened for the purchase counts toward the FBAR. US persons file one when their foreign accounts together exceed $10,000 at any time during the year. It goes to FinCEN, not with the tax return.

Before both comes the NIF, Portugal's tax number. It can be requested online through a legal representative, with no tax representative needed for that step.

For wealth managers and CPAs whose clients are looking at Lisbon, Porto or the Algarve, these are the two dates and thresholds that come up first.

Sources: Lei Geral Tributária, art. 19; Autoridade Tributária, NIF leaflet; IRS, Comparison of Form 8938 and FBAR requirements
