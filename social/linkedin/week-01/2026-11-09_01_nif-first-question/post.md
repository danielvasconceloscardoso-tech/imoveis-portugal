Before people abroad ask about prices, they ask about a tax number.

We launch Imóveis Portugal on December 1, with six editions: Luxembourg, France, Switzerland, Brazil, the US and Israel. In Google's autocomplete for each of those six countries, "nif portugal" is among the first suggestions. It's the first document of any purchase, so it's the first question we answer.

What the official sources say:
· A foreign non-resident can get a NIF online through a legal representative, on the tax authority's e-balcão. No tax representative is needed for that step.
· Once they own property in Portugal, they have 15 days to appoint a tax representative or join the electronic notification service.
· Outside the EU and EEA, one of the two is mandatory. Inside, the representative is optional.

For lawyers, banks and advisers with clients abroad, this is usually the first step of the file, and a clear answer saves a call.

Sources: Autoridade Tributária, NIF leaflet for non-resident foreigners; Lei Geral Tributária, art. 19; Google autocomplete, collected October 26, 2026
