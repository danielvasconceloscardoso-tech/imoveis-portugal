Portugal's median price per square metre: €830 in early 2016, €2,264 in mid-2026.

That's +173% in ten years, from INE's local housing price statistics (median value per m² of family dwellings sold over the last 12 months).

In the six places we compare most often:
· Porto +234%
· Grândola, Comporta's municipality, +230%
· Cascais +206%
· Funchal +188%
· Lisbon +177%
· Loulé +165%

One method note: INE changed its method in Q4 2019, and the two series are joined there.

We publish these lines for every municipality on our house-prices page. If you're a developer, a bank or a journalist and want the data behind a chart, the link is in the first comment.

Source: INE, local housing price statistics, indicators 0009490 and 0012234, Q1 2016 to Q2 2026
