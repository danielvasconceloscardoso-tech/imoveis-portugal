"imt portugal" is what people abroad type first.

When we mapped Google's autocomplete for our six editions in October, the Portuguese transfer tax came first in French from Luxembourg, France and Switzerland, in Portuguese from Brazil and in English from the US. Before neighborhoods, before visas.

Here's what the IMT Code says for one €250,000 home, checked on October 24, 2026:

Resident, own permanent home: €7,042 (2026 progressive table).
Resident, other home: €8,105.
Non-resident: €18,750. Article 17(10), added by Decree-Law 97/2026, sets a flat 7.5% for non-residents buying an urban property used exclusively for housing, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies.

The exceptions: your client has been considered a Portuguese tax resident, becomes one within two years, or lets the home within the decree's rent caps. How the first one reads for people who emigrated is still an open question for lawyers.

Stamp duty is 0.8% for everyone. Same apartment, an €11,708 IMT gap.

Sources: IMT Code, art. 17(1), (3) and (10) to (12), 2026 tables and Decree-Law 97/2026; Stamp Duty table, item 1.1
