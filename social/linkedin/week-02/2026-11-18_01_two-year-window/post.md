Two years. That's the window that changes the Portuguese transfer tax for Americans planning a move.

"Moving to portugal from usa" is one of the top searches we mapped for the US. A client who buys before becoming tax resident is a non-resident under the IMT Code: a flat 7.5% for an urban property used exclusively for housing, with no exemption or reduction, unless an exception applies (article 17(10), added by Decree-Law 97/2026).

One exception fits the mover. If your client becomes a Portuguese tax resident within two years of the purchase, the difference is refunded on request, within six months. The full 7.5% is paid first, on the day of assessment or within 30 days.

On a €300,000 home (about $335,800 at the ECB rate of October 23, 2026), that's €22,500 paid at purchase, against €10,542 for a resident's own permanent home.

Two US-side details clients ask about: a Portuguese account used for the purchase counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold, and owning outside the EU means appointing a tax representative or joining Portugal's electronic notifications.

Sources: IMT Code, art. 17(1), (10) to (12) and art. 36, Decree-Law 97/2026; IRS, FBAR; General Tax Law, art. 19
