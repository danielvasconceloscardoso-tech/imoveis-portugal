One square meter in the canton of Geneva buys 2.3 in Lisbon.

For journalists and advisers writing about cross-border buyers, here are three official price series side by side, each with its method.

Canton of Geneva: CHF 10,853 per m², median, existing condominium apartments, 2024 (OCSTAT). That's €11,762 at the ECB rate of October 23, 2026, or 2.3 m² in Lisbon.
Luxembourg City: €10,269 per m², mean, existing apartments, July 2025 to June 2026 (Observatoire de l'Habitat). 2.0 m² in Lisbon.
Paris: €9,692 per m², median, existing apartments, October 2025 to September 2026 (Notaires de France). 1.9 m² in Lisbon.

Lisbon itself: €5,192 per m², INE median for all homes sold in the 12 months to Q2 2026.

The methods differ (mean against median, canton against city, apartments against all homes), so these are orders of magnitude, not like-for-like indices. And the purchase taxes come next: a non-resident pays the flat 7.5% IMT of article 17(10) of the IMT Code, unless an exception applies.

Sources: OCSTAT Geneva, 2024; Observatoire de l'Habitat, Q2 2026; Notaires de France, Q3 2026; INE, Q2 2026; ECB, Oct 23, 2026; IMT Code, art. 17(10)
