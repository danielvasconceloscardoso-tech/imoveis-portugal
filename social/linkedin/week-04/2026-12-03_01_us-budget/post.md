$500,000 in Portugal, five places.

At the ECB rate of October 23 ($1.1193 to the euro), $500,000 is €446,708. Divided by INE's median price per m² of homes sold in the year to June 2026, it buys 86 m² in Lisbon, 91 in Cascais, 108 in Loulé in the Algarve, 123 in Porto and 176 in Grândola, Comporta's municipality.

US clients meet two sets of rules before they meet a floor plan.

On the Portuguese side, a non-resident pays IMT at a flat 7.5% on a home, unless an exception applies, such as becoming a Portuguese tax resident within two years.

On the US side, money waiting in a Portuguese bank account counts toward the $10,000 FBAR threshold. If they later live in Portugal, the Form 8938 thresholds for taxpayers abroad are $200,000 at year end or $300,000 at any time ($400,000 and $600,000 for joint filers).
