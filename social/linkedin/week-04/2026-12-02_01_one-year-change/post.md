+17.7% in a year. That's the change in Portugal's median price per m² of homes sold, INE's figure for the 12 months to June 2026: €2,264, against €1,923 a year earlier.

In space: at the national median, €300,000 bought 156 m² a year earlier. Today it buys 133.

The rise is broad. Lisbon's median went from €4,525 to €5,192 per m² (+14.7%), Porto's from €3,060 to €3,631 (+18.7%).

Over ten years the national line runs from €830 in early 2016 to €2,264, up 173%.
