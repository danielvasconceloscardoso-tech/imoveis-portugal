Proof of funds is part of the closing calendar in Portugal.

Under Lei 83/2017, banks, notaries and lawyers are obliged entities: they must obtain information on the origin of funds when the risk profile or the operation calls for it. Businesses carrying out real-estate activities must identify their clients, with photo ID, before the deal.

For a client who signs from abroad by power of attorney, the check still happens. The documents travel; the client doesn't.

What this means for the professionals around a cross-border purchase:
· the bank sending the money and the lawyer holding the power of attorney can both ask for the origin of the funds;
· a file that is ready before the promissory contract keeps the deed date;
· the transfer tax (IMT) is due on the day it's assessed or within the next 30 days, so the money has to be in place early.

Wealth managers and transfer providers who prepare that file are part of the closing.
