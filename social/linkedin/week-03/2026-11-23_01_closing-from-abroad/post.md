€375. That's the official fee for a deed and its registration at a Casa Pronta office in Portugal.

For clients who live abroad, the fee is rarely the obstacle. The signature is. In Google's autocomplete for Portuguese speakers in Luxembourg, France and Switzerland, "escritura casa" (the house deed) is among the top ten searches about buying in Portugal.

The official sources say no flight is needed. A power of attorney signed at a Portuguese consulate, or before a local notary with the Hague Apostille, lets a representative sign the promissory contract and the deed.

What the official pages say about the closing itself:
· Casa Pronta does the deed and the land registration in one sitting: €375, €700 with a mortgage, €50 per extra property.
· It asks for the ID and tax numbers of both parties, the usage license or its waiver, and the energy certificate.
· Lawyer and notary fees are separate.

For banks, advisers and developers with clients abroad, the signature sets the calendar.
