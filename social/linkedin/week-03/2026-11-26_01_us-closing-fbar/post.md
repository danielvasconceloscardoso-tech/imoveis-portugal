$10,000. When a US person's foreign accounts add up to more than that at any time in the year, an FBAR is due (IRS).

It matters for anyone helping an American client buy in Portugal from home. A Portuguese bank account opened to pay the deposit counts toward that threshold.

What the official sources say about closing from the US:
· The power of attorney is signed before a US notary with the Hague Apostille, or at a Portuguese consulate, such as the one in Newark.
· Deed and registration at Casa Pronta cost €375, about $420 at the ECB rate of October 23, 2026.
· Living outside the EU, the owner appoints a tax representative in Portugal or joins the electronic notifications within 15 days of owning the home.

For US advisers, CPAs and wealth managers, the Portuguese closing and the US filing calendar run on the same dates.
