Yes, per square metre, by a factor of roughly two to five depending on where you compare. The official numbers let you put them side by side, with some care.

Portugal (INE, median value per m² of family dwellings sold in the 12 months to the 2nd quarter of 2026):

- whole country: €2,264
- Lisbon €5,192, Cascais €4,889, Loulé in the Algarve €4,137, Porto €3,631, Funchal in Madeira €3,538, Grândola (Comporta's municipality) €2,543

The three places people usually compare it with:

- Luxembourg City: €10,269 per m², the average registered price of existing apartments from July 2025 to June 2026 (Observatoire de l'Habitat). It's a mean, not a median.
- Paris: €9,692 per m², the median for existing apartments from October 2025 to September 2026 (Notaires de France).
- Geneva: CHF 10,853 per m², the median for existing condominium (PPE) apartments sold on the free market in the canton in 2024 (OCSTAT). That's about €11,762 at the ECB reference rate of 23 October 2026 (0.9227 francs to the euro).

So the price of one square metre in Luxembourg City pays for about 2.0 m² in Lisbon and 2.8 m² in Porto. One in Paris pays for 1.9 and 2.7. One in Geneva for 2.3 and 3.2. Against Portugal's national median, the ratio runs from 4.3 (Paris) to 5.2 (Geneva).

Caveats that matter:

1. The sources aren't identical: mean against median, a city against a canton, different 12-month windows, apartments only abroad against all family dwellings in Portugal. Read the ratios as orders of magnitude, not exact figures.
2. Portuguese medians are sale values, new and existing homes together. Prime areas inside a municipality sit well above its median.
3. Prices in Portugal have moved fast: the national median rose 17.7% in the year to mid-2026, and 173% since early 2016 (€830 per m² then).
4. Purchase taxes differ. A non-resident pays IMT at a flat 7.5% on a home in Portugal unless an exception applies (becoming a Portuguese tax resident within two years is one), plus 0.8% stamp duty. Whether an exception covers someone who lived in Portugal years ago is a question for a Portuguese lawyer or tax adviser.

Source: INE, indicators 0012234 and 0009490 (2nd quarter 2026); Observatoire de l'Habitat, Luxembourg (2026 T2); Notaires de France, prix immobilier (to the 3rd quarter 2026); OCSTAT Geneva, Informations statistiques n° 11, November 2025; ECB euro reference rates, 23 October 2026; Código do IMT, art. 17.º, n.os 10 to 12; Tabela Geral do Imposto do Selo, verba 1.1.
