# Copy v2: the home page (prompt 32 §3, D59)

A proposal. Nothing here reaches another page until the operator approves the words. Each block keeps to the three rules: one to three sentences, at most 45 words, one action. Headlines are statements of at most 8 words, never questions, never two phrases joined by a colon. `node scripts/check-content.mjs` now fails on any banned word or shape in the `home2` blocks, any sentence over 25 words, any block over 45 words and any home section with more than one action.

Removed from the home: the partner agency panel, the home of the week, the two articles and the second action under the hero ("Pedir uma consulta privada"). They stay on their own pages. "Your team for this purchase" stays in the path stations only (D54).

Figures carry their source on the page as before (INE medians for the second quarter of 2026, INE price history since 2016).
