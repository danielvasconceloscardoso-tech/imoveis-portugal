## What the references agree on

1. One message and one action per screen. The newsletter signup is that action on the home page.
2. Serif for statements, sans for everything you click or scan.
3. Numbers are big, and each has a footnote to its source.
4. Warm paper background, ink text, one accent colour, thin rules instead of boxes.
5. Help centre: search first, then a few clear groups.

See BRIEF.md for how this becomes our design.
