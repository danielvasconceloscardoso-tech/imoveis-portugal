## What they miss and we do

1. Every figure linked to its official source, with the date checked.
2. The 2026 non-resident IMT rate and its exceptions (most guides are out of date).
3. Country reporting in the reader's own country (FBAR, 3916, IRPF, pillars, treaties) next to the Portuguese rules.
4. One page per edition, in the reader's language, on a phone.
