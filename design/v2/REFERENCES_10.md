## Ryan Serhant (serhant.com)

1. **Voice:** short, superlative-free imperatives ("Make a move for your future"), then hard numbers: price, beds, baths, square feet on every card.
2. **Design:** black and white, one bold hero (video), dense navigation (Buy, Rent, Sell, New Development) and a phone number in the header; listings as uniform cards.
3. **Aesthetic:** confident, big-city, "we are the market".
4. **Content:** answers "what can I buy, where, for how much"; the call to action sits on every card ("view listing") and in the header ("Call us").
5. **What we take:** sell the life in one line, prove it with a number right under it; one action per card. **Not:** the superlative self-description, since we have no audience figures yet.

## Gary Vaynerchuk (garyvee.com → garyvaynerchuk.com)

1. **Voice:** first person, spoken, empathetic; "document, don't create".
2. **Design:** a content hub: one long piece (podcast, keynote) cut into many native formats per platform, each linking back to the long piece.
3. **Aesthetic:** raw and human over polished.
4. **Content:** answers the questions his audience actually asks, in their words; the call to action is to follow, subscribe or reply.
5. **What we take:** write the FAQ in the reader's own words; the weekly newsletter is the "pillar" and social posts are the cut-downs that lead back to the signup.

## Lucas Valente da Silva (Forbes Portugal, idealista)

1. **Voice:** humble, personal, a story of effort ("começou a lavar carros"); speaks of dreams and relationships, not square metres.
2. **Design:** personal brand across Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, half digital and half local presence (flyers, billboards), per Forbes.
3. **Aesthetic:** youthful, warm, close to the client.
4. **Content:** "who is helping me", then proof through results and recognition.
5. **What we take:** warmth and consistency, the same voice on every channel. **Not:** a personal face (anonymity, hard rule 1).

## Amorim family in Comporta (idealista, DN)

1. **Voice:** few words, calm, respect for "community, nature and tradition".
2. **Design:** land, light and nature first; the product is the place.
3. **Aesthetic:** quiet luxury, lots of space, nothing shouted.
4. **Content:** answers "what is this place and how will it stay this way"; the call to action is discreet (enquire).
5. **What we take:** more white space, fewer words per screen, photos of places with air in them.

## José Cardoso Botelho / Vanguard Properties (idealista, Jornal Económico, Bloomberg Línea)

1. **Voice:** assured and factual about Portugal and international buyers (US and Mexico named), talks about finishes and execution.
2. **Design:** design-led projects (Castilho 203, Infinity, Comporta), "turnkey" with interior design.
3. **Aesthetic:** international, architectural, premium.
4. **Content:** answers "is Portugal right for me and is this well built", with figures on investment and sales.
5. **What we take:** an assured voice about Portugal for international readers, backed by figures; partner developers can carry that voice in labelled sponsored pages.

## Summary for our copy

Belonging (Lucas, Gary) plus calm (Comporta) plus proof (Serhant, Vanguard): one warm line, one number with its source, one action.
