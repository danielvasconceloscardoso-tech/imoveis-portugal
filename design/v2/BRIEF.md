# Redesign v2: one-page brief

Brand DNA: success and trust. Super clean, easy to use, beautiful. A Gold sponsor opens the site and thinks "serious people, I want my logo here".

## Type

1. **Newsreader** (serif, self-hosted variable font, already in the project) for headlines, big numbers and pull quotes. Weight 400–500, never bold. Optical size on.
2. **Public Sans** (sans, self-hosted) for navigation, body, buttons, forms and tables. 17 px body on mobile and desktop, line height 1.6.
3. Small caps labels (11–12 px, letter-spaced) for section names, like Monocle.

Archivo, direction C's wide display face, is dropped: it reads as a tech startup, not as trust.

## Colour

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| paper | #f6f3ee | page background (warm, like YC and Knight Frank) |
| card | #ffffff | answer cards, forms |
| ink | #15181d | text |
| muted | #5c6066 | secondary text |
| rule | #e2ddd4 | thin dividers |
| accent | #1e3a5f | buttons, links: deep "azulejo" blue for trust |
| gold | #a87f2d | only for the Founding Partner offer and sponsor tiers |

Edition colours from direction C stay, but only as a 4 px marker beside the edition name. No coloured page backgrounds.

## Grid

One column of 680 px for reading; a 1120 px frame for the home page and the media kit (12 columns, 24 px gutters). Spacing in steps of 8 px. Mobile first: 20 px side margins, no horizontal scroll.

## Photography

Direction C's colour photos of Lisbon and Porto (D15, Unsplash, credits in design/directions/photos/CREDITS.md). One photo per page at most, never behind text. Missing: coast and houses (needs the Unsplash key, task 8t).

## Tone

Plain words, short sentences, numbers first. Describe, never advise. No superlatives, no "paradise", no "unique opportunity", no promises of returns. We never call the platform independent: we say how we work (official sources, sponsored content labelled).

## Components

1. **Header:** wordmark, four links, edition chip, discreet language switch (globe + "PT"), one button ("Subscribe").
2. **Hero:** one serif sentence, one line of promise, email field + button.
3. **Figure:** big serif number, one line of meaning, footnote with the source and the date checked.
4. **Help centre (FAQ):** "How can we help?", a search field, three stage groups (Before buying, Buying, After buying), answers that open in place, one partner button per answer.
5. **Trust strip:** partner agency with its AMI licence, "Every figure has an official source", "Sponsored content is always labelled".
6. **Partner card:** logo, category, one line, "Sponsored" label.
7. **Report (e-book):** Knight Frank style cover (title block on a photo), contents page, chapter openers with a big number, charts in the accent colour, sources and disclaimer at the end.
8. **Footer:** brand, how we work, privacy, legal notice, editions, languages.
